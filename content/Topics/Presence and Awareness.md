# Presence and Awareness

Dave's most repeated claim, across the podcast, the book and ninety newsletters: **if you are not present, you cannot receive anything.** Not your team, not a mentor, not life. Every other tool in the graph runs on it.

## Subtopics

- **Awareness and the Inner Game** (343 notes)
- **Presence and Attention** (31 notes)

## The one distinction

Presence or threat. [[Above and Below the Line]] is the map, and [[Presence or threat is the simple version of above and below the line]] is the version you can use mid-meeting. Below the line, the mind hears everything as a problem and goes to work on it. Above it, you can see what is actually here.

## What pulls you out

Thinking is mostly escape. [[Being in your head is a strategy to avoid a feeling]], and [[More thinking makes clarity worse, like shaking sand in a bottle]]. The story layer does the rest: [[A fact is something a video camera could record]], and everything else is meaning your mind added. [[Interpreting a situation as a problem is what drops you into blame]].

## Who is noticing

Dave's deeper move is identity: [[The Talker and the Listener]]. [[Hearing a voice in your head means there is a talker and a listener]], so [[Identify as the listener rather than the talker]]. Taken all the way, that becomes [[Identifying as Awareness]] and [[The Storyteller Mind]].

## Acceptance, not resignation

[[You cannot change what you do not accept]], and How am I creating this is the question that converts acceptance into power. [[One Hundred Percent Responsibility]] is presence applied to results.

## Why it matters for the company

[[Your state is contagious to your team]]. A founder operating from presence makes clearer decisions, listens so people feel heard, and doesn't spread fear. [[Clean Fuel and Dirty Fuel]] is the same distinction applied to motivation.
