# Identity and Self-Worth

The question underneath most founder suffering: **who do you think you are, and what do you believe you have to prove?** Dave's answer is that you are already whole, and most of what drives founders is an identity built to earn a worth that was never missing.

## Subtopics

- **Identity Parts and Projection** (206 notes)
- **Shame Approval and Self-Worth** (144 notes)

## The premise

[[You are already whole, perfect and complete]] and [[Everyone is inherently worthy of what they authentically want]]. Everything else in this topic is about what gets in the way of believing that.

## The identity you built to survive

[[Core Beliefs]] form early, around what got you accepted. [[The conditions you learned to survive become the conditions your survival depends on]], and [[Personality is a defence structure built from the feelings you would not feel]]. The narrating voice is part of the same machinery: The narrating voice in your head is a survival mechanism, not your self.

## Sourcing worth from outside

[[Security, Control and Approval]] is the model. [[Most founders source their sense of security, control and approval externally]], and the most expensive version is [[Equating your value to the value of your company is a major trap]]. The correction: [[I am running this company, I am not this company]]. [[No matter what external conditions you create, there you are]].

## Shame

[[Shame says I am bad and guilt says I did bad]]. Shame is the verdict on the self that stops everything else moving. → [[Emotions and Fear]]

## Parts and projection

[[All humans have all the same parts]], and [[Every part of you has an intended positive outcome]]. What you reject in others is the mirror: [[Projection as a Mirror]] and [[Being irritated by someone shows you a part of yourself you have not loved]]. The drama roles are identities too: [[Heroes require victims]].

## Why it matters

[[The Upper Limit Problem]] shows how a self-image caps how good life is allowed to get, and [[People are more frightened of joy and pleasure than of sadness and anger]]. [[Self-discovery matters more than self-improvement]], because improving a self you believe is deficient never arrives. [[Via Negativa]]: the work is seeing through what isn't true, not adding something missing.
