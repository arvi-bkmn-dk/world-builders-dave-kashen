# Set Random Where Am I Reminders

**Practice for:** [[Above and Below the Line]]. This is the exercise that model is meant to be done with. It's borrowed from the [[Conscious Leadership Group]], which Dave credits, and he pairs it with the model explicitly.

## What It Is

The habit-builder for locating yourself above or below the line. Content is so captivating that people forget there's a question to ask at all.

## The Steps

1. Install an app that sends random or intentional notifications (Dave names Mind Jogger on iPhone and Randomly Remind Me on Android).
2. Set it to ask, several times a day:

> literally I have you know have it say four five six times a day where am I question mark or above or below question mark

3. For the first couple of weeks, just answer it. The goal is the mental habit of asking.

## How Dave Uses It

He gives it to clients after they've learned how *they* know they're below the line: constriction in the body, wanting to be right, defensiveness, blaming, wishing something were different. He likens it to putting on 3D glasses that reveal the dimension called context. → [[Making where am I your primary question raises every role you play]]
