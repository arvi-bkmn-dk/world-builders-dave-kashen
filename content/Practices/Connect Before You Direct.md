# Connect Before You Direct

**Borrowed practice.** Taught to Dave by his and his wife's parenting coach (unnamed in the episode). Not paired with a Dave model.

## What It Is

Before you ask a child to do anything, make sure you're connected first. The coach's image is the old MacBook charger with its little green light:

> you want to make sure the lights on before you direct

## The Steps

1. Walk in and connect for a minute: *hey buddy, what are you watching? how was your day?*
2. Then make the request: dinner, homework.
3. Don't assume a connection from an hour ago still holds, because "the light goes out pretty fast."

## Why It Works

Most pushback isn't about the request. Dave's read:

> when we don't feel connected we just resist whatever

When people feel connected they want to cooperate. He compares skipping it to walking into a business meeting and just barking orders. → [[Making yourself wrong triggers counter-will and that is what creates the yo-yo]]
