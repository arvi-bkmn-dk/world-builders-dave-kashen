# Calibrate Your Yes With Your Extremes

**Practice for:** [[Whole Body Yes]] — building the muscle of attunement to all four intelligences.

## Steps

1. **Move your attention into your body.** Around the heart, then below the navel. Ask what your head, heart and gut each want.
2. **Calibrate with your extremes.** Dave's known yes is a fresh powder day on the mountain; his known drain is a full day of tax prep. Those two sensations become the map for everything subtler.
3. **Practise on low-stakes decisions.** Dinner, a free Saturday. A free Saturday used to stress Dave out, trying to find the right answer; asking what he wanted dissolved it.
4. **If the gut goes quiet, check fear and anger.** Ask what you're scared might be true. Anger is the energy of boundaries and wants.
5. **Look for the whole body yes.** Head, heart and gut all saying yes at once.
6. **Widen the circle.** Sense your team as a living organism: where's the aliveness, what wants to happen here?

> Build the muscle here before you use it on whether to launch internationally.

> Notice that a hell yes is never an intellectual conclusion. It's an energy in your body.
