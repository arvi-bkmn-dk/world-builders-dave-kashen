# Go on a No Diet

**Practice for:** [[Whole Body Yes]] — discovering which of your yeses are real.

> There's a practice I learned from them for discovering which of your yeses are real. It's called going on a no diet.

## Steps

1. **Switch your default to no.** For a set period, say no first, at least inside your own mind, to every request: a meeting, a task, a decision your team wants you to make.
2. **Sit with the no and notice what arises.**
   - **Anger** means you actually wanted it. Anger is the energy of wanting and of boundaries.
   - **Relief** means it was never a real yes.
   - **Fear or sadness** — look closer. Often that's the exact feeling you were about to say yes to avoid.
3. **Tell the people around you** what you're doing, so your no's don't catch them off guard.
4. **Treat any no as a veto.** If your head, heart or gut says no, it's a no.

> Don't confuse fear with a no.

When you catch yourself settling for a yes that isn't a whole body yes, look for the belief underneath, usually some version of *I can't have it all.*

> Because if you believed you could find a whole body yes, why would you ever settle for one you don't?
