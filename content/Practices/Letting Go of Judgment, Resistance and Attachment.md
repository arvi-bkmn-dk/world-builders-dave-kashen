# Letting Go of Judgment, Resistance and Attachment

## What It Is

The practical half of *The Course*. Peace is what remains when judgment, resistance and attachment are released, and you can't release what you haven't found. So the practice is a set of locating questions, followed by deliberate exposure to the thing you are holding on to.

## The Steps

1. **Find what you are judging and resisting.**

> To identify what you’re still judging and resisting, simply ask yourself: who or what have I not yet been willing to love?

   Then turn the same question inward: which parts of yourself have you not yet been willing to love?

2. **Find what you are attached to.** Ask what you are unwilling to let go of, then work through the usual candidates.
   - **People:** meditate on a loved one dying or leaving, feeling it all the way through, until you realise you would be okay.
   - **Money:** give it away, and notice any fear or resistance that comes up while you do.
   - **Health and life:** imagine getting sick and dying.

> Money? Give away at least 10% of your net worth.

> In this sense, money can’t buy happiness but giving away money can.

3. **Return to peace every time it is disturbed.** Each disturbance marks something you chose not to love. Identify it, release it and come back, expecting two steps forward and one step back.

## How Dave Uses It

As the working method of a course whose stated promise is inner peace on completion. It is paired with the claim that events are neutral, so what's being released is always a thought about an event, never the event itself. → [[A story is the meaning your mind adds to neutral facts]]
