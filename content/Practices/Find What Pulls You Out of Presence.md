# Find What Pulls You Out of Presence

**Practice for:** [[Planning happens in the present moment, so presence never requires doing nothing]]

> Start by not trying to be present all the time. You relax into presence rather than working your way there.

> You can get so focused on the mechanics of presence that you lose the essence.

Instead of trying harder, notice the moments you're not present, then look at how you create them.

## Steps

1. **Write your own recipe.** If you wanted to guarantee drifting out of presence every time you sat down to work, how would you do it? Whatever you write is how you do it. → [[The Recipe]]
2. **Separate thinking about what could go wrong from worrying about it.** Welcome the fear, acknowledge you might lose the customer, then go respond.
3. **Scenario plan from presence.** If this happens, I'll respond this way; if that, that way. Make none of it a problem, and trust future you to handle future issues.

> And when nothing needs fixing, you can just go create what you want, instead of trying to avoid and fix something you don't want.
