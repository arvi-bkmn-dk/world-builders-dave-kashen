# Feedback Sentence Stems

**Practice for:** [[Sharing what you have been withholding brings you closer, not further apart]]

> Self-awareness is the leg of candor that trips up most leaders. You can be fully willing to give feedback and still draw a blank on what to say.

> Sentence stems are training wheels for exactly this.

## Steps

1. **Finish a stem.** Each one sends you into your own mind to find what's true:
   - *"You would be more powerful to me if..."*
   - *"I notice my energy rises when you..."*
   - *"I notice my energy drops when you..."*
   - *"I think you'd be more effective if..."* (itself a version of feedforward → Put feedback in the context of getting better, not of what went wrong)
2. **Keep a running document.**

> To keep the prompt running year-round, create a document with the name of each of your direct reports, and any time you have a thought about someone on that list, add it.

3. **Give it as quickly as you can.** The document is for when right now isn't practical.
