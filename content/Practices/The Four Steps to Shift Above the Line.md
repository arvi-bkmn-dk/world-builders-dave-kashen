# The Four Steps to Shift Above the Line

**Practice for:** [[Above and Below the Line]] — this is the exercise that model is meant to be done with.

## What It Is

> [!info] From the Conscious Leadership Group
> Dave credits these as "the four steps in conscious leadership" in [[GA 01 - The Inner Game of Life, Danny Miranda Podcast 394]].

Dave's step-by-step for moving from a state of threat to a state of presence on any issue. He walks himself through it live, in writing, on the most charged subject he could pick: the Israeli-Palestinian conflict after October 7th.

## The Steps

> Awareness. Become aware of your state (ATL or BTL)

> Acceptance. Accept yourself for being in that state

> Willingness. Ask willingness questions to explore your willingness to shift

> Shift. Shift from a state of threat to a state of presence, and take aligned actions

## How Dave Uses It

On himself, and in the open.
- **Awareness.** He names that he is below the line.
- **Acceptance.** He names what he is scared of and accepts it: "Welcome, fear."
- **Willingness.** He works through the Conscious Leadership Group's willingness questions, including one he calls "a really hard one": whether he is willing to let go of being right that innocent people shouldn't be killed.
- **Shift.** He takes his own share of responsibility and turns it into specific actions, chosen by a *whole body yes*.

His challenge to readers is to run the same four steps on every issue in their lives, or, if they can't shift yet, to get curious about what is in the way. → [[The Willingness Question]] · [[Whole Body Yes]]

## Also in [[GA 01 - The Inner Game of Life, Danny Miranda Podcast 394]]

The interview gives each step its question: *where am I*, *can I accept myself for being right where I am*, *am I willing to shift*, *how will I shift*. Dave calls the first his North Star. He ran the steps during the ginger episode, starting with acceptance of the fear itself.

> The first is, where am I? Right? Like, the awareness of where am I, uh, specifically meaning, am I above or below the line?

> Can you just accept yourself for being scared?
