# Step into the body of your future self

**Practice for:** [[Creative Tension]] — this is the exercise that model is meant to be done with.

Dave's actual visualization protocol, as he guides it.

**First, get present.** *"Really face what is right now. And **could you let go of wishing it was different**, so you can fully allow and get fully present."* Without this the exercise runs on lack and produces more of it. → [[Visualize the being, not just the having]]

**Then, from neutral, ask what you really want.**

> If we were looking at you from the outside, **what would we notice? What's your posture like? What's the expression on your face?**

> Now **step into the body of future Jesse**, who's there having that experience. **Breathe through his lungs. See through his eyes.** What does it feel like? What do you notice is different **in your mind and your thought patterns**? What's different **in your body**?

> You'll know you're doing it effectively **if you feel inspiration in your body.** It's a **body test.** If you feel the way you think you'll feel **then, now** — great. Because **you're already putting yourself into the being that can create that future.**

Jesse names the woo version — *"raising your vibration"*, law of attraction — and Dave neither endorses nor dismisses it:

> I think there's something to that. 'Cause **even without the woo** — when you feel more energized and alive and excited, **you're more magnetic**, and literally **you have more energy** to do more stuff and create more.

→ [[Creative Tension]] · [[The Meditation Practice]] · [[Aliveness is the north star for authenticity]]
