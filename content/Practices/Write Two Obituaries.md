# Write Two Obituaries

## What It Is

A clarifying exercise Dave sometimes gives clients when the long view is getting lost:

> write their obituary or write two obituaries first write the one head towards

## The Steps

1. Write the obituary, or funeral speech, you're heading toward if you keep going exactly as you are.
2. Write the one you'd want: what your kids, spouse and friends would say.
3. Notice the gap.

## How Dave Uses It

He calls it "a very eye opening exercise". It comes up after host Luke describes using the deathbed view to step down as CEO. Dave's point is that facing death clarifies who you want to be, and most people find the answer is relationships.
