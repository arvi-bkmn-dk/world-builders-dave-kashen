# Real-Time Group Feedback

**Practice for:** [[Sharing what you have been withholding brings you closer, not further apart]]

Dave used to run more anonymous 360 surveys for clients. Over time he started experimenting with feedback given live, around the table.

## Steps

> One person goes on the hot seat for five minutes. Everyone around the table shares feedback using the sentence stems. The person receiving just says thank you. Then you rotate.

The stems come from [[Feedback Sentence Stems]].

## Why it works

It primes the pump of direct feedback across the whole team, and:

> It's also efficient: if six people would have given you the same feedback in private, one round covers it.
