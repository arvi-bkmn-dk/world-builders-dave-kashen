# Four by Four Breathing

**Practice for:** [[Above and Below the Line]] — the fastest tool Dave teaches for shifting from threat back to presence.

## What It Is

Dave introduces it as *"one of the simplest and most effective tools that I have found to get presence,"* learned from the [[Conscious Leadership Group]].

## The Steps

1. Breathe in for a count of four, **in through the nose**. Breathing in through the mouth can trigger the fight-or-flight response.
2. Breathe out for a count of four, through the nose or mouth. An audible sigh on the exhale is welcome.
3. Repeat four times.

## How Dave Uses It

Later in the session he names it as the most efficient reset when you're in a state of threat or still carrying the last thing.

## Also in [[NL 63 - Why Presence Is the Foundation of Everything]]

Dave's written version starts with the feet, before the breath:

> And your feet are about as far from your head as you can get, which is exactly why we start there.

Feel your feet on the floor and the weight in your chair, notice whatever is in your chest or gut without fixing it, then breathe four by four.

> Just 32 seconds.

> That's the doorway back to presence.
