# Go to Dinner as Your Opposite

**Practice for:** reclaiming disowned parts. → [[Projection as a Mirror]] · [[A disowned quality becomes a third rail your system will not let you go near]]

From the Sun Valley Founders Retreat. The group assigns each person a persona to embody at dinner: the quality most opposite to how they normally show up. The warmest person went as cruel, the most humble as arrogant, and Dave as the guy with no interior life whatsoever.

> It felt so freeing and light.

## Steps

1. **The group names each person's opposite.** Other people see your default more clearly than you do.
2. **Embody it for a whole dinner.** Commit to the persona rather than playing at it.
3. **Let others love you for it, and love yourself for it too.**

Why it works:

> You gain behavioral flexibility, no longer constrained by what your identity has decided you’re allowed to be.
