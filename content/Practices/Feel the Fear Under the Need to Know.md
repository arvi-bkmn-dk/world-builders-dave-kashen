# Feel the Fear Under the Need to Know

**Practice for:** [[Clarity is about you, certainty is about the future]]

Dave's way out of a decision that won't resolve. The loop he describes (uncertainty, worst-case stories, fear, escaping into your head) has one thing holding it together:

> Uncertainty, worst-case scenarios, fear, escaping into your head to figure it out: that whole cycle is reinforced by one thing, unfelt fear.

He calls this the most counterintuitive and effective thing he knows.

## Steps

1. **Find the fear in your body.** Where is it showing up? A tightening in your belly, maybe.

> At the physical level, that's all fear is: a sensation.

2. **Welcome it instead of resisting it.** It may get a little more intense, then within a minute or two it dissipates.

> And the figuring-it-out thought patterns start to dissolve with it, because they were only ever defense mechanisms against feeling this.

3. **Ask what's actually answerable.** Not *what's the right answer?* but *what's my best thinking right now?*

> And if the decision still feels hard, it's probably because it's close, 52/48. If the answer was obvious, it would be obvious.

4. **Treat the decision as an experiment.** → [[Hold a decision the way a scientist holds a hypothesis]]
