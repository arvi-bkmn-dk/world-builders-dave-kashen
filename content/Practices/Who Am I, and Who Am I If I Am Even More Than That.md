# Who Am I, and Who Am I If I Am Even More Than That

## What It Is

The challenge Dave leaves [[Danny Miranda]]'s audience with. He is clear that the wording isn't his: "these aren't my words, it's some spiritual guru."

## The Steps

> sit quietly and ask yourself the question Who am I and see what arises and then ask the question if I'm even more than that Who am I and sit quietly and see what arises?

> And continue until you know when you're done.

## How Dave Uses It

As the cut-to-the-chase version of the disidentification work that runs through the interview. Each answer is an object you *have* (a role, a body, an image), so the next round asks past it. Diana Chapman ran a related shortcut with Dave as her client: imagine removing the chip in your brain that holds the thought *I am Dave*, and notice what's there. For him the answer was nothing, and the nothing felt like peace. → [[Identifying as Awareness]]
