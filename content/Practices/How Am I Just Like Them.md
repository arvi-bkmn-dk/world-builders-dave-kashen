# How Am I Just Like Them

**Practice for:** [[Projection as a Mirror]] — this is the exercise that model is meant to be done with.

## What It Is

Dave's working form of owning your projections. The default tendency is to look for difference, and this exercise deliberately looks for sameness instead.

> I’ve heard the practice referred to as ‘owning your projections.’

> I find tremendous value in the exercise of asking myself: “How am I just like them?” (How am I a liar, a coward, a bully, a jerk, an idiot, ignorant, racist, sexist, superficial, materialistic, etc.?)

## The Steps

1. Pick the person or group you are judging most harshly.
2. Ask how you are just like them.
3. Notice which of two things you find: you show the trait yourself, or you keep that part of yourself locked away and won't let it out.
4. Acknowledge and accept the part you had rejected. Compassion for the other person follows.

## How Dave Uses It

On the hardest possible targets, a Hamas terrorist and Benjamin Netanyahu. He finds righteousness in himself, feeling like a victim, moments of wanting to lash out, the pull of believing Spirit is directing him, nationalism, and wanting to keep status and control. His claim is that this makes behaviour easier to choose, not harder:

> Paradoxically, this makes it easier, not harder, to choose which parts we let guide our choices and behavior. What we resist persists.

> one of the practices I do is can I see the things I have an aversion to in other people?
