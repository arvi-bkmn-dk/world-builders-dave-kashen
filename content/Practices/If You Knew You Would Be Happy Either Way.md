# If You Knew You Would Be Happy Either Way

**Practice for:** [[The intensity of a feeling is not a guide to what you want]] — hearing the want underneath a loud feeling.

A question Dave takes from [[Joe Hudson]]:

> "If you knew you'd be happy either way, what would you do?"

> What that question does is remove the belief that your choice determines how you'll feel, which is usually what's generating the most noise in the first place.

## Steps

1. **Name the feeling that's loudest.** Fear, sadness, guilt, dread. Be specific.
2. **Ask: if I knew I'd be happy either way, what would I choose?**
3. **Ask: am I willing to feel what this choice actually requires me to feel?**

> That willingness is where the real decision usually lives.
