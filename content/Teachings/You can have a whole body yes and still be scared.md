# You can have a whole body yes and still be scared

The easy misreading of *only say yes to a whole body yes* is to treat every fear as a no. Dave rules it out:

> Don't confuse fear with a no.

> You can have a whole body yes and be scared at the same time.

His own example: he's publishing a book soon, and he's scared people won't like it.

> If being scared meant you don't have a whole body yes, you'd never do anything courageous.

> The things that scare you and that you really want to do generally lead to the most growth.
