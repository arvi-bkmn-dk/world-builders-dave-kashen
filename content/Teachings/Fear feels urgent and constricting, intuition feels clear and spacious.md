# Fear feels urgent and constricting, intuition feels clear and spacious

The answer to a question Dave says founders bring him constantly: *I trust my instincts, but sometimes I can't tell if it's real intuition or just fear disguised as wisdom.*

The test is somatic, not analytical:

> * **Fear** feels urgent, constricting, and anxious.
> * **Intuition** feels clear, spacious, and obvious — almost like it was there all along.

*Urgent* is the most useful tell. Fear generates pressure to act immediately, which is precisely what prevents the check. Intuition carries no deadline.

The procedure around it:

> 1. **Pause before reacting.** Create a "sacred pause" between stimulus and response.
> 2. **Notice what's happening inside.** What sensations arise? What thoughts? What emotions? Just observe — without labeling anything right or wrong.
> 3. **Separate signal from noise.**
> 4. **Trust what remains after the noise settles.** The deeper knowing is always there. It was just buried under mental clutter.

Dave names the two failure modes it sits between: **pure intellect** — overanalysis, endless pro/con lists, trying to think your way to certainty — and **pure emotion**, acting on gut without awareness, "where that gut feeling could just be fear, insecurity, or past trauma talking."

> The key isn't to abandon logic or emotion — it's to observe them both without getting lost in either.

And the question to substitute at the decision point: instead of *what should I do?*, ask **what's actually happening inside me right now?**
