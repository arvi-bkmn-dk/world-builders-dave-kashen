# A newborn is worthy before accomplishing anything, and so are you

A thought experiment Dave uses with clients who source their worth from their company:

> imagine a child being born right coming into the world is that child worthy yes of course

Everyone says yes, though the baby has accomplished nothing. And you were once that baby. Worthiness was never earned, so it can't be lost with a startup:

> we're human beings not human doings human beings are inherently worthy

Dave frames the work as releasing rather than gaining. Let go of the story that your worthiness depends on success, and you gain freedom and, paradoxically, succeed more easily. → [[You can never have enough success to prove you were worthy]] · [[Equating your value to the value of your company is a major trap]]
