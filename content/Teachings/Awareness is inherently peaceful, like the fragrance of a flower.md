# Awareness is inherently peaceful, like the fragrance of a flower

Then the inference:

> **And I am awareness itself.**

The flower image is doing precise work. A fragrance isn't something a flower achieves, maintains, or can be talked out of; it is simply what the thing is, given off without effort. So peace on this account is not a state to be produced or defended — it is what is already there when the effort of [[Identifying as Awareness|selfing]] stops.

Dave calls this the ultimate one available: *"it's the ultimate shift we can make as a human being. It'll affect everything that you do, how you show up."*
