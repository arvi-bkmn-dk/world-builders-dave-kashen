# The people asking for your presence are calling you home

Dave on the gift that matters most in a family:

> I believe that is the greatest gift that anyone can give anyone else is their full presence

and, "annoyingly", one of the hardest. His story: as a founder, then as a new coach off at workshops seeking enlightenment, his wife kept asking him to just be here with the family, and for years he heard it as nagging. At one of those workshops he realised the thing he was seeking was presence, and she had been pointing at it all along:

> oh she's like my angel calling me home

The shift also changed how he experiences his kids' demands. Their need for attention stops being a drain on his purpose and becomes an invitation back to the thing he actually wants. → [[Aliveness, freedom, peace and wholeness are already here]] · [[Chasing spirituality is another ego game]]
