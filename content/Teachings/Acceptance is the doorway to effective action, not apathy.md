# Acceptance is the doorway to effective action, not apathy

The objection Dave heads off directly, because it is the one every ambitious founder raises: if I accept that revenue missed plan, haven't I just agreed to it?

> Acceptance is not becoming apathetic and passive. It's the doorway to effective action.

The distinction is between accepting **the facts** and accepting **the future**. "It is what is" is a statement about what has already happened, which is not negotiable in any case. It says nothing about what happens next, and it is precisely what frees attention to work on what happens next.

Dave's marker for the difference is the *direction attention moves* afterwards. Real acceptance is followed by curiosity, learning and ownership — "this is where the creator mindset begins." Resignation is followed by nothing; attention stops. If you've accepted a situation and feel no pull toward the next question, you've landed in resignation, not acceptance.

This is the same correction he applies to non-attachment: dropping the grip on an outcome is not dropping the outcome. → [[The less attached you are to an outcome, the easier it is to create]]
