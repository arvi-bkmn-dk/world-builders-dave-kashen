# Fear makes bad predictions

The episode's closing move, and Dave aims it at a specific superstition.

Jesse names it: *"if I feel the fear, it's gonna come true. I can't even go there."* Dave's label:

> It's almost like a version of **magical thinking.** You could do it either way — *if I let myself feel that fear, then that bad thing's gonna happen.* And sometimes people do this magical thinking around manifesting: *if I just visualize it, it's gonna happen.*

The respectable version has a book behind it — Andy Grove's *Only the Paranoid Survive*: *"if I don't think about everything that could go wrong, then I'm gonna miss something."*

Dave's answer is to check fear's track record:

> Talk about making good and bad predictions — **fear makes bad predictions.** If you think about how many times your mind has made up a story based on fear…

> So this is just a **meta version** of that. The story that fear tells me is: *if I feel the fear and let go of it, then I'll end up creating the thing I fear.* From my experience, **often the opposite is true.**

The instruction is an experiment, not a belief:

> **Run the experiment.** That's the best way to get better at predicting — run experiments and see what actually happens.

Jesse's spec for it: *"articulate and feel the fear around the scariest version of what you think could happen, and then see if it leads to that, or if it leads to the opposite."*
