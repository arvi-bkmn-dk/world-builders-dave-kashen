# Follow the impulse, not the compulsion

The practice Dave gave Jesse years ago, and Jesse says he still uses it daily.

> Quiet my mind. Try to get to an acceptance of whatever is here right now. And then **notice what is coming up — and whether it's a compulsion or an impulse.**

> **Compulsive** is: *oh, I'm scared, I have to do something to relieve this.*

> **Impulse** was: *I would love this to be — I want to color in a coloring book, or I want to research this industry.*

Jesse's own examples of compulsion are the ones that look most like diligence: *"I'm worried about the business, so I'm gonna send a bunch of emails to try to get meetings."* → [[Time management is often the presenting issue for fear avoidance]]

**Why compulsion never completes.** Dave links it to addiction:

> What creates addiction is trying to **fill a need with something that doesn't actually fill that need** — or trying to **create a feeling with something that doesn't actually create that feeling.**

> If you think you need money to feel safe or free — well, **money doesn't actually make you feel safe or free.**

Jesse's image: *"you're hungry and you're just eating candy. It doesn't satiate you, so you crave more."* Dave: *"I guess that's not enough sugar. Same — I guess that's not enough money."*

**The contrast, stated cleanly:**

> **Compulsion:** I don't feel peaceful now because I don't accept things as they are. Something's wrong. So there's a compulsion to get rid of that feeling — with things that don't get rid of it. **That's the cycle most people are in.**

> **Impulse:** I accept things as they are. I feel peace simply by accepting them. And then, **without any sense of lack**, a creative impulse arises — just because we're human — **and I follow it.**

> Then **life just becomes about following aliveness and excitement** — not because you don't feel alive now, but **because you do.**

And Dave's report on whether it works commercially: *"for my lived experience now, coaching hundreds of founders — **following aliveness and excitement is an amazing strategy to be wildly successful and love your life.**"*
