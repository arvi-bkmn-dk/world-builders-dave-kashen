# You can play to win without being attached to winning

The answer to the objection that non-attachment means lowering your effort.

So the variable is not intensity. It's whether the outcome is load-bearing for your worth. → [[Apathy is not caring, non-attachment is not believing your well-being depends on the outcome]]

The dating comparison makes the cost concrete: *"I really need this to work out"* produces desperate and clingy, *"and it's not attractive."* Whereas unattached and present, *"we can just be ourselves."*

→ [[The Paradox of Attachment]] · [[The less attached you are to an outcome, the easier it is to create]] · [[The more you enjoy it, the more efficient and effective you are]]
