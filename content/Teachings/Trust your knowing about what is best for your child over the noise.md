# Trust your knowing about what is best for your child over the noise

After Dave and his wife took their sons' phones away, his younger son went through something like withdrawal, pleading that video games were the only thing that made him happy. Dave held the line:

> I have to trust my knowing as a dad of what's best for him

There's endless outside advice on the right way to parent, and Dave ranks it below one thing: the knowing that's available from a quiet, present mind, "higher than any book". Presence is what makes that knowing audible.

Part of the knowing is that screens are a cheap substitute for what kids really want. → [[Most of what we want is a substitute for what we really want]] · The right answer versus the answer that feels right
