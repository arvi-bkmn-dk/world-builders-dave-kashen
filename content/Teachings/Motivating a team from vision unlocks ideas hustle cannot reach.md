# Motivating a team from vision unlocks ideas hustle cannot reach

The team-facing version of clean fuel is a change in one sentence. Dave's example client ran on the standard model: *if we don't get this right, this bad thing happens — we won't be able to raise the next round.* The shift was to: *if we do get this right, here's what becomes possible and here's who we get to serve.*

The result Dave points to isn't morale, it's output of a specific kind. The culture lifted and creative energy was, in Jesse's phrase, unleashed — and the team produced an idea that doubled the business overnight. Dave's argument for why that is causal rather than coincidental:

> Those are the kind of things that are not in the local maximum that we can hustle our way to. They only happen when we're in this sort of more expansive, present, creative mode.

The distinction between kinds of gains is the useful part. Fear-driven teams are effective at climbing the hill they are already standing on, because threat narrows attention to the known and the immediate. The non-obvious move — the one that changes the shape of the business rather than its slope — requires the expansive state that fear reliably forecloses.
