# The intellect is wired for survival but presents itself as concerned with your fulfillment

> The brain is an organ. A very useful one, and a limited one.

Most of it is concerned with survival, calibrated for predators, running in a world where the physical threat is close to zero. Threats to social status register like threats to survival, so a tough board meeting sets off the same alarms.

Then the deeper trick:

> The intellect is concerned with your survival, but it presents itself as concerned with your fulfillment.

It promises contentment once you hit the results, and billionaires are living proof the promise doesn't hold.

> I like to say the heart pumps blood and the mind pumps thoughts.

> We don't treat every heartbeat as urgent news.
