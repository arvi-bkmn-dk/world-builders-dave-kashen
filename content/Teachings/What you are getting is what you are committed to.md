# What you are getting is what you are committed to

Dave's most confrontational move in the episode, made while Jesse was spending years as CEO of a business he openly said he didn't like.

> Dude, you're committed to that. You're literally committed to this world of running a business you don't like and saying you don't like it. That's all one big commitment you've made.

Jesse's objection was the obvious one — *what do you mean commitment? I'm committed to being a billionaire* — and the distinction Dave draws is between stated commitments and revealed ones:

> What you're doing is what you're committed to. What you're getting is what you're committed to.

The test is output, not intention. Whatever your life is reliably producing is what you are actually committed to, regardless of what you say you're aiming at. → [[Your life reflects your unconscious commitments]]

Jesse's excavation of what the arrangement was paying him is the useful part, and it's genuinely uncomfortable:

> I get to say I could be that, without ever having to take the risk of being that — because I'm in this really comfortable seat.

Staying in a disliked job protected an identity. Claiming the billion-dollar ambition while never testing it kept it permanently undisproven. The cost of leaving wasn't the job; it was the risk of finding out. → The Founder Fear Map
