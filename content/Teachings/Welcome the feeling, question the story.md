# Welcome the feeling, question the story

Dave states his coaching formula outright here, more compactly than anywhere else in his work.

> There's a general theme I would say of: **welcome the feeling, question the story.**

He demonstrates it live, on himself. Having just misremembered a baseball statistic, he notices "a little bit of a twinge of embarrassment" and uses it as the worked example rather than moving on.

Then he applies it to Gagan's account of letting people go. Gagan names sadness, embarrassment and guilt. Dave takes each apart:

> I would welcome the feeling of embarrassment, 'cause feelings just are. But I would question the story that you did something wrong.

> Guilt requires a story that you did something wrong, more in the sense of ethically. Again — welcome the guilt as a feeling, and I would invite you to question that story. Because you might be letting them go find their next amazing career.

The split is between two things that arrive fused. The **feeling** is not up for debate; resisting it is what makes it persist. The **story** attached to it is a claim about reality and can be wrong — and usually is.

It generalises the shame instruction from the previous episode — [[Welcome the shame but do not believe the thoughts that come with it]] — into the standing method for any emotion.
