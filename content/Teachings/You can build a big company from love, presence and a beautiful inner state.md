# You can build a big company from love, presence and a beautiful inner state

The question Dave says he is asked more and more: *"is it possible to experience inner peace while still being ambitious and successful?"*

The belief in the way:

> *"It just feels like the only way to build a successful company is to sacrifice everything else: my health, my family, my peace of mind. And even then, I'm not sure it's enough."*

> This **either/or, binary thinking** typically shows up when we're **below the line**, operating from fear and scarcity. We think we have to choose. Peace or ambition. Family or company. → [[Above and Below the Line]]

And Dave does not dodge the counter-evidence:

> There are so many examples that seem to confirm it. **Elon Musk. Travis Kalanick.** Many fear-based founders and toxic cultures do seem to create massive valuations.

> So here's what I said to the group: **Yes, we know you can build a big company from fear, lack and stress. But let's raise the bar. Because you can also build a big company from love, presence and a beautiful inner state. Now THAT'S worth aiming for.**

That's the actual move — not denying that dirty fuel works, but refusing it as the standard. → [[Clean Fuel and Dirty Fuel]]

His name for it is **surf and turf**:

> One of my core values I jokingly refer to as "surf and turf." **I want to have it all. Both the steak and the fish.** Not settling. Not placing conditions on what's possible. **Not buying into the false binary.**

The existence proof: *"one founder I coach is building what's likely to be a billion-dollar company. And he's doing it while playing golf, spending time with his family, and enjoying his life… He's deeply fulfilled, **not despite his success, but because of how he's building it.**"*
