# Resting as awareness goes further than sourcing validation from within

A refinement of Dave's own language. He usually talks about sourcing security, control and approval *within* rather than from others. Here he says even that framing still assumes a lack that has to be filled:

> It's just stopping sourcing validation from without.

The deeper move isn't to generate worthiness internally. It's to notice that the question doesn't apply to what you actually are:

> could you rest as, as awareness that is beyond the question of worthiness and safety and freedom

Is awareness worthy? The question becomes absurd. The only thing verifiably here is awareness meeting the present moment, and awareness is "whole, perfect, and complete just as it is." → [[Identifying as Awareness]] · [[Security, Control and Approval]]
