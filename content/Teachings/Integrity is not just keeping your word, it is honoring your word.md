# Integrity is not just keeping your word, it is honoring your word

> Integrity isn't just keeping your word — it's **honoring** your word. That's a crucial distinction. Life happens. Priorities shift. You can't always keep every commitment. But you can honor your word by **renegotiating** when needed or **cleaning up** when you break agreements.

Dave turns this into a numbered team agreement:

> * **90%** of the time, you do what you say you'll do
> * **9%** of the time, as soon as you know you won't deliver, you **renegotiate** the agreement
> * **1%** of the time, you acknowledge your broken agreement and **clean it up**

The 9% is the part most teams don't have language for — the renegotiation is not a failure, it is the standard being met. And the effect on how accountability feels:

> When your team has this agreement, accountability becomes an **act of service.** You're supporting each other to stay in integrity, which is energizing.
