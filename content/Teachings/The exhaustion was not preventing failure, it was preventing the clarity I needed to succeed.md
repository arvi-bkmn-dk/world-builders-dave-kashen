# The exhaustion was not preventing failure, it was preventing the clarity I needed to succeed

The retrospective verdict on four years of sixteen-hour days, reached only after coaching and the Landmark Forum:

> I wasn't operating effectively. I was operating **performatively.** I couldn't think clearly. I had **no mental spaciousness** to step back and see the bigger picture.

> The exhaustion wasn't preventing failure. **It was preventing the clarity I needed to succeed.**

This is the strongest version of the energy argument in his work because Dave is the case study rather than the coach. The hours were bought with the thing the hours were for.

And the outcome makes the point sharper still. Wellsphere pivoted, reached six million monthly visitors, and entered acquisition talks in August 2008 — then Lehman collapsed and the price fell.

> All that suffering. All that effort to control the outcome — and the macro environment, **completely outside my control, decided what the outcome would be anyway.**
