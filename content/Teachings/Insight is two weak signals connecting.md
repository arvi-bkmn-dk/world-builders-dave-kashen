# Insight is two weak signals connecting

The mechanism Dave offers for why clarity arrives in the shower and never at the desk:

> **Insight is caused by two weak signals connecting.** And so if there's **noise in your mind**, your brain can't hear the weak signals enough to connect them.

Which turns "get still" from a spiritual instruction into an engineering one. The signals are already present; they are simply quieter than the effort being spent to find them.

> Ironically, when we're trying to figure it out, that's more of the **agitation** that gets in the way of clarity — versus doing something that **shifts your state** when you're not even thinking about it. And sometimes clarity will emerge.

His list of what actually works: meditation, slowing down, bringing a question into meditation, letting go of the thought that something should be different — and Jesse's additions, exercise and a walk in nature. *"I was out on a run and I had this epiphany, or I was in the shower and I had this epiphany."*

It pairs with the sand-in-the-bottle image from the same conversation: agitating a murky bottle to make it clear is the thing keeping it murky. → [[More thinking makes clarity worse, like shaking sand in a bottle]]

Dave admits he falls for it himself: *"I fall into this trap as a coach too — oh God, this client wants to be clear, I gotta help 'em be clear."*

## Also in [[MB 04 - Reflections and Recommendations 2023]]

The 2023 reflections name two ways of getting answers. Effort with your mind to figure it out, or get still and let answers arise. He says the second takes more patience and consistently gives wiser answers.

> There are two distinct ways of getting answers. The more common way is to effort with your mind to ‘figure it out’ or ‘solve the problem’. The less common way is to get still and allow answers to arise.
