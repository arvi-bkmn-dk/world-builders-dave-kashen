# The test of a co-founder relationship is whether you could fire them and keep the friendship

When Dave and his best friend started Fearless Ventures, they agreed the order upfront: personal and spiritual growth first, the friendship second, the business of the fund third.

The ordering isn't naive about business. It depends on context:

> Within the context of the company, we do what we believe is best for the company. But in a broader context, we prioritize the relationship.

> You don't prioritize the relationship over the company inside the company. But outside of it, the friendship comes first.

Which gives Dave a hard, checkable test:

> The ultimate test for whether you've done it well is if you could fire your co-founder from their role and maintain the friendship.

> It's built and rebuilt, one honest conversation at a time.
