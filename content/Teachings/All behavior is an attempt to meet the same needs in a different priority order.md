# All behavior is an attempt to meet the same needs in a different priority order

One of Dave's 2023 reflections, and useful because it explains behaviour that doesn't match stated intentions. **The six-needs list is widely attributed to [[Tony Robbins]]** (the Six Human Needs). Dave doesn't name him in this bullet, though he did Robbins' programs that year. The application to his own schedule is Dave's:

> All of our behaviors are attempts to meet our needs. We all need the same things (Certainty, Variety, Significance, Love/Connection, Growth, Contribution), but not in the same priority order.

The order is the point. Two people with identical needs act differently because they rank them differently, and once you can see your own ranking, you can choose it. Dave's example is himself:

> It explained some of my behavior that didn’t make sense to me — like how I’d say I wanted to do more one-to-many courses and programs and then fill my time with one-on-one coaching sessions.

He had been ranking Certainty and Significance above Love, Growth and Contribution, so the schedule followed the ranking rather than the goal. → [[Security, Control and Approval]]
