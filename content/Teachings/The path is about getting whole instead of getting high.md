# The path is about getting whole instead of getting high

Dave's distinction between two things easily confused, told through Ram Dass.

> For many, many years he was on a chase to feel high. And a lot of us are on that chase — whether it's through business or through drugs or through spirituality. Oh, I want to feel that high, that awakening experience, that adrenaline experience.

The intervention came from Alan Watts:

> Alan's like — dude, you're addicted to getting high. What's up with you? And he finally realised that the path was really more about **wholeness. About getting whole instead of getting high.**

The distinction matters because the chase is invisible when the object is respectable. Business, drugs and spirituality are the same structure — a state you are trying to reach and reach again — and the peak experience keeps ending, so the chase restarts.

Wholeness is not a state to attain; it is what is left when denied parts are let back in. Which is why shadow work is the route to it: you cannot feel full while excluding pieces of yourself. → [[Fulfilled just means feeling full]]

Dave attaches his standing line about the deferred life:

> If you can't be happy now, you won't be happy then — but you can be happy now. And so many people play that deferred life game. Once I have this, then I'll be happy, then I'll be safe, then I'll feel fulfilled. It's just bullshit. It doesn't work.
