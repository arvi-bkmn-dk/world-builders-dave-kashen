# Chasing spirituality is another ego game

Dave's warning about what happens immediately after someone is convinced by him — and it's a genuinely unusual thing for a coach to name about his own material.

> A lot of people, from their ego, will be like: okay, it's not about chasing money. I just heard from Dave and Jesse — it's about chasing *spirituality*. That's the better thing to chase and attain. And that also is just another ego game.

The object changed; the structure didn't. Something is missing, something must be acquired, there is a self that will be adequate once it has been. Swapping revenue for presence leaves the machinery intact and gives it better cover, because the new goal is unimpeachable.

Everything in Dave's work is exposed to this. Presence, non-attachment, emotional capacity, wholeness — each can be pursued exactly the way a founder pursues a number, at which point it becomes [[Bigger is better is a treadmill that never arrives|a treadmill]] with a superior vocabulary. And it will be harder to spot, because nobody warns you about being too committed to growth.

[[Via Negativa]] is the structural answer: if there is nothing to attain, then any framing as attainment has already gone wrong regardless of the object.

The same guard appears in Jesse's closing note — *this is not a should* — and in his willingness to say the old way worked. → [[The tragedy is placing a lower order value above a higher one]]

## Also in [[GA 01 - The Inner Game of Life, Danny Miranda Podcast 394]]

Dave's own example of the whiplash: three days after a journey in which he felt immersed in love, he was checking his crypto portfolio.

> You just like, you just found out that you're God. And you're still obsessed with money.
