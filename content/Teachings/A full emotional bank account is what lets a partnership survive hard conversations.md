# A full emotional bank account is what lets a partnership survive hard conversations

Co-founder breakups are hard to keep calm, with egos and money at stake, so the deposits have to be made before the withdrawal is needed: appreciation, vulnerability, time spent on the relationship itself. Most people, he notes, are stingy with appreciation.
