# If you are not present you cannot receive anything

Dave opens with a scene most founders will recognise: a one-on-one where you suddenly realise you haven't heard a word for five minutes, because your mind was rehearsing, fixing, running scenarios.

> Many people chalk this up to poor focus.

His reframe is that the layer underneath is presence, and that it is not optional:

> Most people think presence is a nice-to-have.

> But if you're not present, you can't receive anything. Not from your team, not from a mentor, not from life itself.

> No conversation, no framework, no insight can reach you if you're not actually here.

That makes presence the precondition for everything else he teaches. A framework you read while absent never lands; feedback you hear through a filter isn't the feedback that was given.

> The lessons are right there. They just require you to be here to receive them.
