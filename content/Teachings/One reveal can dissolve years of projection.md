# One reveal can dissolve years of projection

[[Alex Lieberman]] co-founded Morning Brew and helped build it into a 200-person, $75 million a year business. For eight years he carried a story that he was the weak link, that his co-founder was the real talent and he was along for the ride.

Dave's question in the coaching session was simple: had he ever shared this with his co-founder?

> He hadn't. Not once in eight years.

Dave asked him to. The call ran two and a half hours, and Alex learned his co-founder saw him as invaluable.

> Imagine if Alex had that conversation eight years earlier. All the stress that would have saved.

> One reveal, one honest conversation, and eight years of projection dissolved.

The length of the withhold is the point. Nothing about the relationship changed during those eight years except the story, which grew because it was never tested against the one person who could disprove it.
