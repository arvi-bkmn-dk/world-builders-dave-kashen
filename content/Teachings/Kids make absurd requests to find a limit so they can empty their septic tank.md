# Kids make absurd requests to find a limit so they can empty their septic tank

The second idea from Dave's parenting coach, and the one that helped him most as the parent who hated setting limits. Kids have little power, so frustration builds all day:

> their septic tank is filling they need to empty it

Sometimes they make absurd requests (dessert before dinner, stay out late) precisely because they're looking for a limit to push against, so the frustration has somewhere to go.

Seeing it that way turned setting limits from *I've upset them* into service. Dave describes long emptying sessions ("I hate you") that ended with kids "like warm little puddles, I love you".
