# Treat the startup as an emotional intensity gym

The training metaphor that turns [[The Law of Expanding Possibilities]] into a practice.

> Over time, you can learn to lean into emotional intensity like you might choose to add weight to a bench press at the gym. The more reps of being with a certain feeling will strengthen your capacity to be with that feeling.

> Start thinking of your startup as your emotional intensity gym.

The instruction inside the metaphor is the demanding part: instead of resisting intense feelings, *look forward to them* — the way you'd look forward to lifting. That is a genuine inversion. It reclassifies the hardest moments of building a company from costs to be minimised into the reps that raise the ceiling.

The condition on a rep counting is precise: you allow yourself to fully feel the emotion "without fixing it, resisting it, numbing it, making it a problem, or turning it into a story about yourself." Enduring an intense feeling while narrating it doesn't build capacity — it is the resistance that makes the feeling last.

This is the same claim as Dave's opening image of the startup as a growth dojo, now with a mechanism attached. → [[The startup is a growth dojo but most founders run it as a whipping room]]
