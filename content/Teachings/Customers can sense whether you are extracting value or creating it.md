# Customers can sense whether you are extracting value or creating it

> Are you operating out of lack and scarcity, making choices to squeeze more money from others for yourself?

> Customers can sense the difference, and as everything accelerates, the extraction game stops working faster than it used to.

Dave's example comes from [[Eric Ries]]'s *Incorruptible*: Costco's $1.50 hot dog, unchanged for about twenty years. Costco could have squeezed out more margin many times over; it kept the customer promise instead, and the promise became the business.

> It's the ultimate win-win.

> Choices made from scarcity look reasonable in the moment. They compound into a company customers can feel.
