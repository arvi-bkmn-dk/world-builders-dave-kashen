# We adopt our parents' negative patterns in an attempt to feel connected and loved

The core framework of the Hoffman Process, which Dave describes in his write-up after completing it in May 2023. Bob Hoffman calls it the **Negative Love Syndrome**. A child's survival depends on parental love, so whenever that love is cut off or made conditional, the child emulates the parent's negativity in order to stay connected.

> So, when our parents were acting out their negative (not loving) patterns and we felt a sense of separation from them, we innocently and unconsciously adopted their negative patterns in an attempt to feel connected and loved.

Those adopted patterns then get in the way of relating to yourself and others with love. The Process works them through a **Cycle of Transformation**: Awareness, Expression, Compassion & forgiveness, and New ways of being. Dave singles out the second step, because it treats a pattern as something physical:

> Expression (using intention, voice and body to say “No” to the pattern and “Yes” to what we are moving toward. Patterns live in the body as trapped energy that needs to be released.)

His own case shows how far a single pattern reaches. The core one he found was a belief:

> Interestingly, I discovered that my core pattern is believing that “spirit isn’t real” and that cuts off my connection to Spirit at the knees.

Underneath dozens of smaller ones, he names a core shame message and reports forgiving his parents and stepparents by reflecting on their own lives. → [[The conditions you learned to survive become the conditions your survival depends on]] · [[Hoffman Institute]]
