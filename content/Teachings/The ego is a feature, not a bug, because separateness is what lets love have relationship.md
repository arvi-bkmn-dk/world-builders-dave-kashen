# The ego is a feature, not a bug, because separateness is what lets love have relationship

Dave's answer to a question that bothered him after glimpsing that we are love at our essence: if that's true, why come here and suffer? He credits a mentor, Brian Franklin, with the reframe:

> your left hand can't have the experience of loving your right hand, right?

If you are oneness, there are experiences you can't have (relationship, loving someone *as* someone), because there is no other. A mechanism that makes you perceive yourself as separate is what makes those experiences possible:

> The ego is a feature. It's not a bug, right? The experience of separateness is part of the experience of being human.

Dave says this helped him accept being here instead of wishing to be back in the peak state: "apparently I chose to be here." It rules out the version where the ego is an error to be eliminated, and so it rules out wanting to be somewhere else. → [[Brian Franklin]]
