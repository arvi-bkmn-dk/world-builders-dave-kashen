# Believing love must be earned is itself a kind of loneliness

> The mask is usually older than the company.

Chesky said he felt guilty whenever he wasn't working, believing deep down that success would make people love him. Dave says most entrepreneurs hold some version of it:

> I'm not inherently worthy and lovable, but I can become worthy and lovable by achieving enough.

A founder at his last retreat put it plainly:

> "I've struggled my entire life with the belief that I have to 'earn love' in life and working hard so that I can be deserving of my existence."

> That belief is itself a kind of loneliness.

> If love has to be earned through achievement, you can never trust that anyone loves you for you.

> When we're disconnected from ourselves, we end up feeling disconnected from everyone else.
