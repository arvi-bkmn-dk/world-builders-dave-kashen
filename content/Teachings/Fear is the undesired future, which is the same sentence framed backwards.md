# Fear is the undesired future, which is the same sentence framed backwards

The second drift out of Flow. Humans are, as far as we know, the only species able to imagine what doesn't exist — the capacity that built civilisations — and the survival mechanism points it at threats by default. So a founder who started out inspired slides from imagining the desired future to imagining the **undesired** one.

"If we can't hire the team members we need, we're not going to hit our goals." "If we don't hit our goal, we won't raise our next round." "If I can't learn to be a better leader, we're going to fail."

Dave's observation about these is what makes the teaching usable: they are *logically identical* to their positive forms.

> "If we can hire the team members we need, we're going to be able to hit our goals!" You're saying the same thing, but framing it entirely differently — to both yourself and your team.

Same information, same uncertainty, opposite state. Which means the correction costs nothing in honesty — you are not being asked to believe something rosier, only to state the same conditional in the direction you want to travel. → [[What you focus on is where you steer]]

## Also in [[MB 05 - Founders, It Doesn't Have to Be This Hard]]

The article's case: a founder of a nearly 1,000-person company who realised he had been motivating the team almost entirely from fear. After switching, a cold inbound he would once have ignored led to revenue doubling nearly overnight.

> He realized that he had been almost exclusively motivating team from fear, saying things like: “If we don’t achieve our goal, we won’t be able to raise the next round we need.”

## Also in [[GA 02 - A CEO Coach Reflects on His Performance as a Dad, Dad Pod]]

The parenting version. Dave calls using imagination to picture the future you don't want "absurd", points to *Inside Out*, where the characters take over Riley's imagination to picture every bad outcome, and applies it to reacting to a child's missed practice. → [[Ask whether you are responding to your child or to your fear of who they might become]]

## Also in [[GA 03 - The Inner Game of Entrepreneurship, The Conscious Entrepreneur Podcast]]

Dave's 80/20 for founders:

> we use this tool of the imagination to imagine the future we don't want to have happened

Most of it isn't deliberate scenario planning, just a drifting mind. Catch it and shift to the future you do want.

## Also in [[WS 01 - The Inner Keys to Becoming a World-Class Leader]]

> we use it to imagine the future we don't want to have happen

In the Q&A Dave shows it live. A participant felt a partner's $200k buyout ask as a threat, but felt nothing when Dave asked for the same amount. The threat wasn't the ask; it was the imagined future of paying it and not being able to provide for his family.
