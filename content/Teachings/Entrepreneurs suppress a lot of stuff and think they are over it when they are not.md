# Entrepreneurs suppress a lot of stuff and think they are over it when they are not

Jesse's observation from running [[The Facts and Stories Exercise]] on his own history, and the reason the exercise is worth doing on material you believe is settled.

> I think a lot of entrepreneurs suppress a lot of stuff. They don't actually let it go. They think they're over it, but they're not. I thought I was over things — but I wasn't, until they got dredged back up. And then it actually let me process them and sort of let them through, and then they were in the rear and they just didn't hold a charge anymore.

The diagnostic is **charge**, not belief. Whether you think you're over something is not evidence, because suppression and resolution feel identical from the inside — in both cases you aren't thinking about it. The difference only shows when the material is raised and something either fires or doesn't.

This is why Dave's coaching gets curious about words like *regret* rather than accepting them at face value, and why he asks Jesse to identify the two or three things in his career history that still carry "the biggest charge." The charge is the locator.

The mechanism for clearing it is the same as everywhere else in Dave's work — feel it through rather than reason it away. → [[An unresisted emotion moves through the body in 60 to 90 seconds]]
