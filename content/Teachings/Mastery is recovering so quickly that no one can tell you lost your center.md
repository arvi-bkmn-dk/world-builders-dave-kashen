# Mastery is recovering so quickly that no one can tell you lost your center

A story Dave says he shares with almost every client. An Aikido master in the middle of a room fends off attacking students like flies. Afterwards a student asks how he stays centred through all of it:

> Oh, I don't stay centered. I just recover so quickly. You can't tell.

It changes what the goal is. Dave doesn't claim, or aim, to be present in every moment. The game he describes is speed of return:

> I'm just playing the game of how quickly can I notice and shift and notice and shift and I can't always shift right away.

He pairs it with grace for the moments he can't shift yet: *I'm a human, I'm in a state of threat.* The standard is fast recovery, not never falling. → [[The Four Steps to Shift Above the Line]]
