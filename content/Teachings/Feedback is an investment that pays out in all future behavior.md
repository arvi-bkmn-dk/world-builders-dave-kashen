# Feedback is an investment that pays out in all future behavior

Dave opens his case for feedback with [[Andy Grove]]:

> In High Output Management, Andy Grove describes feedback as one of the highest-leverage activities available to a manager. Tell someone something once, and they can do it differently for the rest of their career.

> That's an investment. You make the investment of feedback, and you get the returns of all future behavior.

Performance is only half of what it buys, though. The other half is connection. → [[Sharing what you have been withholding brings you closer, not further apart]]

> So feedback does two things at once. Each person gets better at what they do and through an increase in trust, everyone moves faster together.
