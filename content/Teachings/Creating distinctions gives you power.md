# Creating distinctions gives you power

Dave's answer to why *look within yourself* usually fails as advice — and his justification for a book full of frameworks.

The mechanic and the amateur are looking at identical hardware. What differs is resolution — and resolution is what converts an undifferentiated bad feeling into a locatable, workable part.

This is the job description for the entire work. Every model in the vault is a distinction, and the reason Dave keeps supplying them rather than advice is that advice acts on the symptom while a distinction changes what you can see. → The Concentric Circles of the Inner Game · [[Content and Context]]

Dave uses the same metaphor on air, crediting the **Landmark Forum** — *"our job here is to create distinctions that were previously not distinguished for people, so that they have more power to create the experience they want in their life."* → [[Insecurity and arrogance are two sides of the same coin|HoE 20 - Ego]]
