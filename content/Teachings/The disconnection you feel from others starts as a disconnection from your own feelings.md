# The disconnection you feel from others starts as a disconnection from your own feelings

After listing the ways people listen through filters, Dave names what every filter has in common: a feeling arises while someone is talking, and the mind pulls you into your head to manage it.

> Every one of these filters is the same escape playing out in conversation.

So the sense that conversations aren't landing is not first a communication problem:

> Because you're not listening to your own inner experience, you can't be with someone else's experience.

The order matters. Better listening technique won't fix it on its own, because the absence starts inside. The same curiosity you'd give another person has to be turned on your own sensations first:

> But the whole reason you can't listen to others is that you're not listening to yourself.
