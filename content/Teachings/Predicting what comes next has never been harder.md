# Predicting what comes next has never been harder

Dave borrows [[Tim Urban]]'s image: human history as a thousand-page book, 250 years a page. Farming arrives on page 950, most of the modern world on page 1,000. Move someone from page 999 to 1,000 and they'd die of shock, and page 1,001 will likely be as different again.

> Which means our ability to predict what comes next is probably the lowest it's been in human history.

Fear lives at the edge of the unfamiliar, so this is a frightening moment, and what Dave notices is the response:

> People are grasping at certainty.

> The truth is, we just don't know.

> If ever there was a time to acknowledge all that we don't know, now would be a good time.

> In that acknowledgement comes curiosity and creativity.

Dave closes with [[Jon Kabat-Zinn]]:

> "You can't stop the waves, but you can learn to surf."
