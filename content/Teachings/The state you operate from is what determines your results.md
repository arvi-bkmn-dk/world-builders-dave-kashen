# The state you operate from is what determines your results

This is the thesis the whole episode — arguably the whole body of work — rests on.

> The state you're operating from is ultimately what determines your results.

It is what makes this an *inner* game rather than a set of techniques. Every other teaching in the episode is a special case of it: fear-motivated teams reach only the local maximum; attached founders show up less creatively; unarguable language delivered from threat still lands as an attack; the same startup is a dojo or a whipping room depending on the state inside it.

It also reframes what coaching is for. If results came from tactics, the work would be to supply better tactics. Because results come from state, the work is to change what the founder is operating from — after which the tactics they already know start producing different outcomes.

The corollary is uncomfortable and Dave doesn't dodge it: state is not fully under conscious control, and it cannot be faked. You can't decide to be unattached, or decide to be present. What you can do is notice the state, find what's driving it, and let it shift — which is why so much of the practice is detection rather than execution.

## Also in [[MB 04 - Reflections and Recommendations 2023]]

The 2023 reflections break state into three pillars and pair them with a daily practice (Tony Robbins' Priming Exercise, silent meditation, and reading his *I am* declarations). He adds that most people live at a local maximum.

> The three pillars of state are (1) physiology — your posture, voice and how you move your body, (2) focus — where you focus your attention, and (3) language — what you say to yourself, especially about yourself and your life.

> You are likely living your life at a local maximum, based on your limiting beliefs about yourself, who you are and what’s possible.
