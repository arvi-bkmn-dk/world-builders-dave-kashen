# Humans are wired to survive, not to thrive, lead or build companies

Dave's reframe of why founders struggle, offered as relief rather than diagnosis:

> The biggest issue you have is that you're human, and humans are not wired to thrive.

> Humans are not wired to lead, and humans are not wired to be entrepreneurs.

The patterns that get in the way are not personal defects. They are *"natural, normal human patterns"* that nearly every founder he has worked with shares, built for a far more dangerous world than the one we live in. So the work is less about acquiring skill than about unlearning:

> So it's as much a process of of sort of unlearning, of transcending our default wiring as it is of, you know, skill or knowledge attainment.

A client in his thirties asked him why nobody had ever taught him this. Dave's answer is that we're simply not taught how to thrive as human beings. → [[Presence or threat is the simple version of above and below the line]]
