# Awareness itself is the sixth sense

Dave's model for where a founder actually stands when making a decision, and it adds a layer most people don't know they have.

The usual picture has two circles: an **inner circle** of thoughts, feelings and sensations only you can perceive, and an **outer circle** of external conditions — what others say and do, the weather, the market.

> But there's an **inner inner circle** beyond your five senses. There's one sense that's even more powerful — **awareness itself.** It's not just noticing what your eyes see or what your ears hear. It's the part of you that watches your own mind at work.

What the third position buys is symmetry:

> When you start to operate from your inner inner circle, you have as much choice in how you respond to internal experiences as you do with external conditions. It starts to matter less whether you're hearing a voice inside your mind or outside your ears; or whether you're noticing internal moods or the weather outside.

That is the whole move. Thoughts and moods normally arrive with authority because they seem to come from *you* — from inside the inner circle. Step out one layer and they become conditions like any other, which you can respond to rather than obey.

Which enables:

> * Notice self-doubt as just another passing thought vs being consumed by it.
> * Recognize fear without letting it drive you into fear-based decisions.
> * Create space to respond with clarity instead of reacting impulsively.

It is the talker/listener distinction extended past thoughts to the whole interior. → [[Hearing a voice in your head means there is a talker and a listener]] · [[Identify as the listener rather than the talker]]

Dave's summary of what separates the best founders: "they don't just think. They don't just feel. **They observe.**"
