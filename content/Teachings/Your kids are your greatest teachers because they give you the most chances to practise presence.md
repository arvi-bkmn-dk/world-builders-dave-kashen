# Your kids are your greatest teachers because they give you the most chances to practise presence

Dave takes the cliché literally:

> if the game of life is learning to be present in a wider and wider variety of circumstances my kids present lots and lots and lots of opportunities for me to play that game

Kids trigger parents precisely because parents care so much about them and their future, which makes them the hardest place to stay present and so the best training ground.

The story that goes with it: years ago Dave drew the line on a flip chart for his boys, seven and nine. Later, upstairs in the loft, his younger son spilled something on the white couch, one of Dave's triggers, and just as Dave was about to react:

> he's like Daddy are you under the line

Dave cracked up and shifted immediately.
