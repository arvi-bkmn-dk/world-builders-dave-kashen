# The intensity of a feeling is not a guide to what you want

When the founders of the [[Conscious Leadership Group]] told Dave to go all in as a partner or move on, imagining leaving brought up sadness so strong he could barely sit with it. So he did what most founders do:

> I turned it into evidence. If it hurts this much to think about leaving, I must really want to stay.

> The feeling was completely real. But it was pointing me away from what I truly wanted.

Dave's distinction:

> Feelings are weather (immediate, real, and not permanent). Wants are more like a compass bearing.

> Grieving is the most pure way to honor something that you loved. It doesn't mean you should keep doing it.

The founder who won't fire someone because the conversation brings dread, the one keeping a dying product alive because shutting it feels like loss: the feeling is real in every case, and it isn't pointing at the want.

> When I left the Conscious Leadership Group, I cried my eyes out.

> And it was one of the clearest decisions I've ever made.
