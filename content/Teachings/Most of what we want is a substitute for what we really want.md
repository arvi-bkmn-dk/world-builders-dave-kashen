# Most of what we want is a substitute for what we really want

→ [[Be-Do-Have]] · [[Do-have-be is backwards and it never arrives]]

## The Claim

**Dave runs it on himself.** His stated want was to take five years of coaching, become a CEO, build an awesome culture and a wildly successful business. Traced back:

→ [[Ask any founder what they want then ask why five times and it traces back to a feeling]]

**And then the move that makes it more than an unmasking.** The feeling is not in the future — it is the precondition of having wanted it at all:

→ The wanting contains the seed of the thing you are wanting

→ Inauthentic vs Authentic Wants · [[Authentic wants arise from wholeness, compensatory behaviors arise from lack]]

## What Changes Once Nothing Is Missing

Dave names the tell. Under *something is missing*, the question is a quiz with a right answer, time feels scarce, and a failed experiment is a mistake:

> You kind of feel like the constriction and tightness around it

→ [[Constriction or expansion in the body is the shorthand test]] · [[Aliveness is the north star for authenticity]]

## The Ambition Version

→ [[The whole game is rigged because when never arrives]] · [[Everyone is inherently worthy of what they authentically want]]

## Also in [[GA 02 - A CEO Coach Reflects on His Performance as a Dad, Dad Pod]]

The parenting version. Screens and video games are

> a lot of these things are cheap substitutes for the things they really want

The dopamine of winning a game stands in for the aliveness of learning or connecting. Kids don't know that yet, but parents do, and Dave trusts that the real thing, once experienced, loosens the pull of the substitute. → [[Trust your knowing about what is best for your child over the noise]]
