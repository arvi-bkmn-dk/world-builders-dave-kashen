# Empowerment and love meet at the top of the mountain

The hinge between the sixth and seventh distinctions, and Dave credits Joe Hudson with the image.

> There's sort of two sides. The mountain one is unconditional love and one is empowerment. And if you let yourself fully open your heart and feel unconditional love, it feels pretty much the same as I'm Superman on top of the mountain. They actually meet.

Two routes that look opposite — one about power and invulnerability, one about open-heartedness — arriving at the same state. Which makes sense once you see what both remove: **the need to defend**. Nothing to protect, so nothing to close around.

Jesse gets there first in the conversation and Dave endorses it fully — someone genuinely empowered "is likely to also be open-hearted and sensitive and empathetic, because you're so powerful that you don't feel the need to make someone small. In fact you do the opposite."

This is the structural reason the seven distinctions end on **love over defense**. Dave: "I believe they're very intentional in the arc of the seven, and I could see how the others build up to this recognition of I don't need to defend, and openness to love."
