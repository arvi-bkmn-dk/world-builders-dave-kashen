# Asking permission lets feedback be invited instead of imposed

The first of Dave's three ways to start this week:

> "I have some feedback for you. Is now a good time?" They almost always say yes, and the yes matters. You've honored their sovereignty. They've invited it in instead of having it come at them.

The content is the same either way. What changes is whether the person chose to receive it.
