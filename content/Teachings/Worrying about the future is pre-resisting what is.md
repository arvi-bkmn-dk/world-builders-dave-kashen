# Worrying about the future is pre-resisting what is

If planning doesn't take you out of presence, what does? Dave's client said planning meant worrying about what could go wrong, and the worry became a story he was a player in.

> Thinking through what could go wrong can be part of making a plan. Worrying about it is a story, and the story has a specific shape.

He had learned to accept what is in the moment. Then he'd decide that if a certain future moment happened, he would resist it, so now he had to make sure it never did.

> He was pre-resisting what is.

The alternative isn't to stop considering risk:

> Fear itself isn't the problem; it invites you to pay attention.

> Worrying about a decision before it arrives spends the energy you'll need when it does.
