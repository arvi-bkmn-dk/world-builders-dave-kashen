# The same activity done from a different source

Jesse raises the fear that doing this work means blowing up your life — and then dismantles it.

> Sometimes you have to wake up and go to the woods and become a guru. That happens to people. But what I've seen for myself and others, **more often than not, is: it's the same thing. It's just doing it in a different way.**

His example is his wife, an Enneagram Two:

> She's the helper, she loves to help other people and make them feel great. And she **does it often from below the line**, from a feeling of lack — *I need them to love me back, and that's why I'm gonna do this.* And then she starts to feel **frustrated and resentful.**

> But **when she's above the line, she does the same exact stuff.** She still takes care of a bunch of people. She's just like: **I'm doing this because that's the creative impulse arising for me.**

Identical behaviour, opposite source, completely different result — for her and for everyone around her. Which is the whole [[Content and Context]] distinction landing in a life rather than a meeting: what changes is not the content of the day but the state it issues from.

Jesse's extension, offered as the spiritual reading:

> **Jesse's gonna be an entrepreneur, he's gonna start businesses. That's gonna happen.** He can allow it to happen via his **creative impulses, his love and joy** — or he can **suffer his way through it.**

This is a useful corrective to how the rest of his work can be misread. Nothing here requires you to leave. The question is almost never *what should I do instead*, it is **where is this coming from** — and the answer changes the experience without changing the calendar.
