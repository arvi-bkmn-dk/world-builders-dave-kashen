# Your outer results are a reflection of your inner world

The thesis sentence of the entire body of work, from the first email:

> The Inner Game shapes **how you see the world and yourself** — and therefore how you lead, make decisions, and overcome challenges. **Your outer results are a reflection of your inner world.**

> In other words, **the Inner Game drives the success of the Outer Game.**

The causal direction is the whole claim. Not that inner life matters *as well as* results, but that it is upstream of them. → [[The Inner Game and the Outer Game]] · [[Content and Context]]

Dave's three supports:

> **Mindset drives action.** Your beliefs impact every business decision you make. Founders with resilient, growth-oriented mindsets have boundless positive energy and turn challenges into opportunities, while those driven by **fear or lack** make choices that limit growth and drain your energy. → [[Clean Fuel and Dirty Fuel]] · [[Core Beliefs]]

> **Leadership starts within.** Authentic leadership doesn't begin with managing others — **it starts with leading yourself.**

> **Lasting fulfillment.** → [[You win twice]]

He grounds the claim in his own history rather than asserting it:

> The insight around the importance of the Inner Game was **hard earned by me over 30 years.**

> About 15 years ago, I discovered that I'd been driven by a story I told myself — **that I was unworthy.** This story fueled my drive, actions and decisions from a sense of fear and lack.

> Despite achieving all of these "outer" successes, **I still felt unfulfilled.** I realized that the fulfillment and contentment I thought I'd find after climbing to the top of the mountain **simply wasn't there.**

→ [[I felt more pressure to not fail than to succeed]] · [[Ask any founder what they want then ask why five times and it traces back to a feeling]]
