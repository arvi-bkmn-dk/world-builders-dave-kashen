# In a group, everyone's work is everyone's work

Why Dave coaches individuals in front of a group, and why a participant watching someone else's recipe saw their own:

> I always say, like, everyone's work is everyone's work

It rests on shared humanity: we have the same parts and the same patterns, so watching someone else get coached often reveals what one-on-one can't.

> It was a total blind spot for me. I didn't even realize, and then I see it in them.

One-on-one coaching can only work with what's in the person. In a group, another person's pattern becomes a mirror for your own blind spot. → [[All humans have all the same parts]]
