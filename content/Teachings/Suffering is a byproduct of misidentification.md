# Suffering is a byproduct of misidentification

Dave's short course on inner peace opens with a grammar lesson and turns it into a diagnosis. You *have* thoughts, feelings, sensations, a body and an image of who you are. You are not any of them.

> You have thoughts, feelings, and sensations. You are the subject. Thoughts, feelings and sensations are the objects.

The suffering comes from getting the grammar wrong. Whatever you mistake yourself for becomes the thing you must control to be okay:

> Suffering is a byproduct of mis-identification.

> When you think you are the image and narrative you have in your mind about who you are (37-year old female, went to this school, work at this job, have this many kids), the focus of your life becomes improving the image and narrative (money, status, success, fitness, attractiveness, etc). You think you need to control your image to be ok.

Identify with thoughts and feelings, and life becomes chasing the good ones. Identify with the image, and life becomes improving it. Identify with the body, and life becomes keeping it safe. The same move sits under all three: something outside your control has been made a condition of being okay. → [[Identifying as Awareness]] · [[Self-ing is the mental effort to create and protect an image of who you are]]

## Also in [[GA 01 - The Inner Game of Life, Danny Miranda Podcast 394]]

[[Danny Miranda]] reads the subject-and-object passage from *The Course* aloud to Dave and adds his own gloss: that so much suffering is conflating the image of who we are with who we actually are.
