# Your stories set the emotional climate of your team

Dave's argument for why the storyteller work is a leadership skill and not a personal-development exercise. Believed stories shape behaviour and inner state; the founder's inner state sets the company's tone; so the stories ripple outward.

The mapping he gives is one-to-one:

- If you assume the worst → your team becomes fearful.
- If you catastrophise → your team becomes reactive.
- If you judge yourself harshly → your team feels like they're never enough.
- If you operate from old narratives → your culture inherits them.

The third is the least obvious and the most important. Harsh self-judgment feels private and self-contained — it is aimed inward, at no one else. But the standard a founder applies to themselves becomes the standard the room believes is in force, and nobody needs to be criticised out loud for that to happen.

> Your stories determine the emotional climate of your team.

This is the same mechanism as [[Your state is contagious to your team]], one level upstream: the story produces the state, the state propagates.
