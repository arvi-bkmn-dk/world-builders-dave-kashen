# Is your soul dying or just uncomfortable

Dave's test for whether a leap is actually due — offered right after the story of making the call that cost him a guaranteed retention bonus, one month after his first son was born, as sole provider.

Reason one for not waiting:

> **My soul was dying.** It was such a burning desire at that point. I don't know if I could deal with one more day of doing work that wasn't aligned and on purpose when I now knew what was.

> If you're sitting on a similar decision right now, check: **Is your soul dying or just uncomfortable?** There's a difference between *"this is hard and scary"* versus *"I can't do one more day of this work that isn't aligned with who I am."*

The distinction matters because everything in his work otherwise argues **against** escaping discomfort — [[Resistance to the emotion is what blocks flow, not the emotion|resistance]] is the thing to work on, not the situation. This is the carve-out: some discomfort is material to be felt, and some is a signal that the life is wrong. Confusing them in either direction is costly.

Reason two was about modelling:

> I imagined my future self talking to clients in this predicament and realized: **I need to be the change I want to see in the world.** I need to live the way I'm going to coach.

> And I thought about my son — **who do I want to be as a role model for him?** What model do I want to set for him of pursuing purpose over status and money?
