# The art of leadership is discernment

Dave's closing caveat on [[Conscious Listening]], and it generalises to every tool in his work.

> **The art of leadership is discernment.**

The variable he names is **dependence**: *"if there's someone on your team who is dependent on you, where it wouldn't be reasonable for them to solve the issue on their own, of course you can direct them."* Newer people may need coaching, directing, guiding, teaching. The listening tool is for **interpersonal issues where there's emotion.**

This is the same shape as [[You do not want to get stuck in any mode]] — the answer is never a rule, it's the capacity to read the moment. → [[Content does not determine your decision quality, context does]]
