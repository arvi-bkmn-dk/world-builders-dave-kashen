# Hold a decision the way a scientist holds a hypothesis

The last step of Dave's way out of the need to know. Once the fear has been felt, the decision can be held differently:

> Once you've felt the fear, the grip of needing to be right loosens, and something better becomes available: pursuing truth.

> Hold your decision the way a scientist holds a hypothesis, something to test, not a position to defend. If a scientist gets righteous about their ideas, they're screwed.

> Take the action, uncover the next card, learn. Value speed of learning over looking good.

This is the decision-making side of [[Experiments Instead of Failure]]. There the experiment protects you from coding yourself as a failure. Here it protects the decision from your need to be right.
