# Your company's results come from the density of creative energy aimed at your vision

Dave reduces company success to two things, and traces both to the founder:

> From my experience, your company's success comes down to who's on your team and the state those people operate from.

> Together, those two things determine the density of creative energy directed toward creating your vision.

A team led from fear wastes that energy bracing against what might go wrong. At Fetch Rewards, the CEO had been motivating himself and his team with *if we don't do these things, we won't be able to raise.* Dave challenged him to flip it inside himself first: *if we do these things, these good things will happen.*

> Same thing, framed entirely differently.

> The tension started to loosen, and at the same time, the creative output dramatically increased.

New ideas, new business lines, new products. Dave is careful not to oversell it: the CEO had practised conscious leadership for a while, *"but that one shift was really powerful."*
