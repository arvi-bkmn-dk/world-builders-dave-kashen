# Success depends on how much of your team's creative energy goes into creating the future you want

The measure Dave calls a hidden vector, and repeats so it lands:

> The key to success is the percentage of your and your team's creative energy that's focused on creating the future you want.

It reframes performance as an allocation problem. Most of a founder's and a team's creative energy isn't spent on the future at all. It goes to surviving threats that aren't there: managing perceptions, being right, avoiding feelings, motivating through fear. Anything that frees that energy moves results, which is why Dave treats inner work as a business lever rather than a wellness add-on.

The same arithmetic appears in the second trap of creative tension: energy spent on *"the gap between how it is and how we wish it already was"* is energy not spent creating. → [[Creative Tension]]
