# Morning meditation is not the same as living meditation

Dave's answer to founders who think the first step has to be a ten-day silent retreat. You don't even need to meditate to start, and meditating isn't the finish line either:

> there's an interesting distinction we could make about like the morning meditation versus living meditation

He knows people who meditate for up to an hour every morning and walk into work triggered as usual. The practice that matters is continuous noticing: *what thoughts is my mind pumping right now?* "The heart pumps blood, the mind pumps thoughts."
