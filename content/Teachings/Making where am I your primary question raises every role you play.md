# Making where am I your primary question raises every role you play

Most people keep content in front of them and think about context now and then. Dave says that after long practice it flipped for him:

> My primary attention is on context.

In practice that means one question, asked moment to moment in the middle of a conversation, with clients, in-laws or kids: *where am I? Above or below the line?* He calls it his North Star, and describes the effect as a rising tide that lifts every boat:

> Like I'm a better father, a better husband, a better coach, a better business partner, a better investor, like all the things.

That is why he puts the practice ahead of his roles, "even before my role as a husband, father". Improving your state improves every role at once, instead of optimising each one separately. → [[Content and Context]] · [[Above and Below the Line]]
