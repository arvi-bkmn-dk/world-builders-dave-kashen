# Joy is the experience of recognizing your true self

> This teacher **Rupert Spira** said: **joy is the experience of recognizing your true self.**

Jesse immediately connects it to the exits and the birth stories from earlier in the episode:

> Maybe the reason your ego disappears when you make a billion dollars is just — for one moment **you don't have that worry or fear.**

Dave: *"or you're not wishing anything was different."*

Which makes the definition do explanatory work rather than sounding poetic. If joy is recognition of the true self, then it appears whenever the ego's activity briefly stops — and the events people credit with producing joy are simply the events large enough to interrupt it. The sunset, the birth, the exit: none of them *made* the joy; each of them stopped the storyteller for a moment.

Dave adds Loch Kelly's version:

> **If there's no problems, what's here now?** That's another way to access it. If the ego is the **problem solver** and there's no problems, **it can relax for a moment.** But then it goes right back and **invents new problems to assert its relevance.**

That last clause is the mechanism behind everything in this episode — the storyteller is not malfunctioning when it produces a new problem after a good one is solved. It is doing the only thing that keeps it necessary.
