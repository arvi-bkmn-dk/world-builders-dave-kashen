# There is a direct inverse correlation between love and judgment

Dave connects acceptance to love through a single structural claim:

> **Love by its nature is unconditional.** So when we **remove a condition** that our mind has for how things need to be, **we experience love.**

> There's a **direct inverse correlation between love and judgment.**

Which produces two instructions, and they are the same instruction:

> If you want to really love somebody — **let go of your judgment.** If you want to really love your life — **let go of your judgment.**

And then the reframe of a very common wish:

> A lot of people are like: *I wanna have a life I love, I wanna have a job I love.* And most people think that means **this specific set of external conditions will be met.** But the other way to think about it is: **I will no longer judge my conditions, and then I will have a life that I love.**

> **To love your life is simply to let go of judging it** and thinking it should already be different than it is.

Dave is clear the target is **any** condition, named plainly: *"you could point that at a specific person — **I wish my wife was more adventurous** — okay, could I just accept her exactly as she is? You could point it at a team member, your company, your fitness level, your revenue, your profits, how much you've raised, how long it's taking to raise. **Your feelings.**"*

> Any condition of life, you could choose to judge as *this is bad, it shouldn't be this way* — **or not judge it.**
