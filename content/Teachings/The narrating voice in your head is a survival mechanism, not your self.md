# The narrating voice in your head is a survival mechanism, not your self

The claim that makes the rest of [[The Storyteller Mind]] possible. The voice narrates everything, weaves tales of future doom, tells you what you must achieve to finally be happy, compares you to others, and tells you who you are and what's possible for you.

> Most founders assume this narrator is their own inner self. They think it's them speaking, and it's the voice of truth. It isn't.

What it is instead: "an ancient survival mechanism that interprets neutral facts through the lens of fear and conditioning." Old, useful in its original context, and systematically biased.

The consequence Dave draws is about *entrapment* rather than accuracy:

> When you think you're the storyteller, you get trapped in the stories.

If the voice is you, its verdicts are self-assessments and there's no vantage point from which to decline them. If it's a mechanism, you can hear it, name its character, and choose whether to engage — which is precisely what [[The Story Release Technique]] operationalises.
