# What's mine to do is the question that replaces obligation with choice

The question Dave says he asks himself "more and more these days", and the one his four-step shift lands on:

> This is a question I ask myself more and more these days. Facing reality as it is — with all the light and all the dark, envisioning the future that I want, what’s mine to do?

What makes it different from *what should I do* is the emotional starting point. It comes after acceptance and after taking responsibility, so it isn't driven by guilt:

> You can then ask yourself “What’s mine to do?” without a sense of judgment, guilt or obligation, and brainstorm potential options

He uses his own responsibility inventory (money, time and attention, writing and sharing) to generate options, and then chooses among them by a whole body yes rather than by what seems right. → How am I creating this is the question that converts acceptance into power · [[Whole Body Yes]]
