# The 80-20 of culture is who you let into your organization

Every company ends up with a culture, whether anyone follows the values or not. Dave's claim is about where it actually gets set:

> The 80/20 of culture is who you let into your organization.

> Who you hire, who you fire, who you promote, who you compensate, who you recognize, and who gets your attention as the leader.

> Every one of those decisions expresses what you value, whether you've defined it or not.

Which is why culture erodes through reasonable exceptions: you badly need an ML engineer, you finally find a great one, and they have sharp elbows when a value is collaboration.

> All these little marginal decisions are how founders undermine culture, one reasonable-sounding exception at a time.

The loudest statement is the leadership team:

> Your team watches who you put in charge, and they read it as the clearest statement of what you actually value.
