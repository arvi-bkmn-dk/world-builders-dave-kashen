# When you focus on the money it obscures your authentic path

From a coaching exchange on the call (personal details not extracted). Someone measures their life against an income target set years earlier. Dave doesn't dismiss wanting money, but names what the target does when it becomes primary:

His evidence is his own move out of finance, investing and startups into coaching: focusing on serving, and finding the money followed, but only after he was willing to walk away from it.

> I was willing to walk away.
