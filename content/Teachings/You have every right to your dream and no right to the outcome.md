# You have every right to your dream and no right to the outcome

None of Dave's advice about rejection means lowering your expectations:

> I'm a dreamer. I love believing in my clients' dreams and encouraging them to dream bigger.

> The trick is to hold the dream without being attached to it.

> You have every right to your dream and to the conversation. You have no right to the outcome.

> the less attached you are to the outcome, the easier it is to create it.

His proof was at home. When his sixteen-year-old announced he didn't want college and wanted to be a race car driver, Dave felt fear and the urge to control his son's future. He got out of the way and supported it. His wife took him to go-kart training and then his first real races, and he won four out of five.

> You never know how things might unfold when you stop trying to force life to match your idea of it, and start treating it as a dojo you can work with instead.
