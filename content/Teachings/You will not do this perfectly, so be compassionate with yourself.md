# You will not do this perfectly, so be compassionate with yourself

The point Dave insists on adding after the interview is over. Fifteen years into practising presence, he still gets triggered, says things he regrets, and admits he has spanked one of his kids, which he is not advocating:

> you're not going to do it perfectly and just I just be compassionate with yourself

The disclosure is the teaching. A coach modelling imperfection gives permission that a flawless example can't, and self-attack only drops you further below the line. → [[The belief underneath is that I have to whip myself to act good]]
