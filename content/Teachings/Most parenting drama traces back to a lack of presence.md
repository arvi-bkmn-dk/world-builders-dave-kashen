# Most parenting drama traces back to a lack of presence

Asked what he sees across the fathers he coaches, Dave goes straight to root cause:

> most dads are not present most of the time and that's the real issue

Either they don't know another way of being exists, or they know and can't or won't shift in the moment. When you're not present, every reactive pattern kicks in, which is why parenting feels so complex. Seeing a single cause is a relief: study your particular triggers so you catch them faster, but the heart of it is being in threat.

For founder-dads he adds two specifics. It's less about hours than presence, and the hardest moment is the context switch: going from ninety miles an hour on Zoom straight into dad mode.
