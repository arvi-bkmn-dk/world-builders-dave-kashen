# Aliveness is the north star for authenticity

The instruction Jesse kept hearing on their calls — *"I just want you to follow your aliveness, Jesse"* — and this episode is him asking what it actually means.

> Aliveness as a north star, and in some way as maybe **the truest guide to our unique, authentic path.**

> For some reason this unique being feels **energized** in certain situations and doing certain things. I don't really know why, but it does seem to be **one of the things that makes us unique.** And so it feels part of living an authentic life.

Dave sets it against his own first 25 years:

> I have a whole story about the first 25 years, when I was living an **inauthentic** life, trying to prove my worth and basically pursuing **status** and safety and worthiness — **not pursuing aliveness.**

**How you know you're in it** is physical, not conceptual: *"literally feeling energy in your body,"* weightless, buzzing, rising — versus *"constricted, condensed, sort of heavy."* Jesse's examples are a goal in a match, a new business idea where he can see all the pieces, and the moment his children were born. Dave's own is a four-day retreat where he jumped off cliffs.

The image he offers for navigating by it:

> I sometimes think — imagine there's like a **string or a rope** that's the path we're meant to be on. And the way you know whether you're on it is **you feel the energy of it.** When you get off it, you've disconnected from that life-force path.

> Your job is to **detect** — rather than figure out what you're supposed to be doing.
