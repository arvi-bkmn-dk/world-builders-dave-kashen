# Ask whether you are responding to your child or to your fear of who they might become

The distinction Dave calls one of his biggest "hacks" as a dad. His son doesn't want to go to soccer practice:

> am I really responding to like him not wanting to go to soccer practice today or am I like oh my God he's not going to be disciplined

In the second case

> then I'm reacting to my fear of who I don't want him to become

and he yells, forces, or bargains, none of which is about today. From presence the questions are different: are you tired, not feeling well, in a fight with a friend? It's the undesired future collapsing the present conversation, the same pattern he names in founders. → [[Fear is the undesired future, which is the same sentence framed backwards]] · [[Above and Below the Line]]
