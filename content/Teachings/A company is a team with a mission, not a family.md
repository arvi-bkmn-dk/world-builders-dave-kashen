# A company is a team with a mission, not a family

Once someone is hired, attachment spikes: they're your person now. Over time many founders start to feel the team is a family, and then letting someone go feels like betrayal. You don't fire family.

Dave's better frame is a coach and their players:

> The best coaches in the world genuinely love their players. And they still trade them when the team needs it.

> That's because the purpose of a family is the family. The purpose of a team is a mission.

> You can love someone and still recognize they belong somewhere else.
