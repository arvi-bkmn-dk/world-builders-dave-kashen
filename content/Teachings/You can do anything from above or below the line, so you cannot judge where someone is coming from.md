# You can do anything from above or below the line, so you cannot judge where someone is coming from

[[Danny Miranda]] raises it: someone who looks from outside like they're chasing money or fame may actually be in creative tension, acting from love. Dave agrees and generalises:

> You can do anything from a state of threat, ego, and, and from a state of presence, love.

The same action (selling a company, building a following, working long hours) can come from either state. So the behaviour doesn't tell you the source:

> I don't think you can know from the outside where someone's coming from and it's easy to judge.

His move when he does judge is to use it as information about himself, not about them. And he adds that needing to be right about where someone is coming from is itself a below-the-line signal. → [[Your judgments of others mark the places you have not grown]]
