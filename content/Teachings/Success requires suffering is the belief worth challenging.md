# Success requires suffering is the belief worth challenging

Jesse opens on the eating-glass, punched-in-the-face memes, and Dave distils what the whole podcast is actually arguing against:

> The simple way I've been thinking about it lately is that it is **challenging the belief that success requires suffering.**

> It's a **very pervasive belief.**

Jesse: *"I got our tagline."*

What makes it hard to dislodge is that the evidence looks supportive. Dirty fuel **works** — *"there's a lot of examples of companies that have been successful… it's just dirty, in the sense that it's toxic. It leaves its toxic residue."* And the culture reinforces it: *"chips on shoulders put chips in pockets,"* weaponise your trauma, glorification of drive from lack.

> It's pretty **counterculture** to say you can build a multi-billion dollar business from heart, from a place of joy and inspiration, and not a sense of lack.

Dave's reading of why clients resist letting it go:

> The most common belief is that it's just **bullshit** and it won't work and I won't be successful. *I'm not interested in being content or fulfilled if that means I'm not gonna be successful.*

> Which is a little funny — 'cause if you ask, *why do you wanna be successful?* … usually they'll get to some version of **I want to be fulfilled.**
