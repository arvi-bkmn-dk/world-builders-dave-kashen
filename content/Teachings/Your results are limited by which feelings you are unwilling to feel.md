# Your results are limited by which feelings you are unwilling to feel

The core statement of [[The Law of Expanding Possibilities]], and the most consequential claim in Dave's work because of what it displaces.

> Most founders think they need better tactics, more discipline, clearer strategy, sharper execution. At the root, your business is not limited by those things. It's limited by your nervous system.

The stated law: the scope of what's possible for you is directly determined by your capacity to be with intense feelings.

The supporting observation is one every founder has witnessed — under identical pressure, some leaders collapse while others become *more* grounded, creative and powerful. Dave attributes the difference to capacity for intensity rather than to temperament or talent, which matters because capacity is trainable and temperament isn't.

The reason the law binds is that growth *manufactures* intensity. Expand the vision, raise more capital, grow the team, take on bigger customers — and fear rises, self-doubt appears, shame surfaces, uncertainty expands. Every increase in scope is also an increase in emotional load, so the two cannot be decoupled:

> What's possible for you and your company can only actualize as fast as your emotional capacity expands.
