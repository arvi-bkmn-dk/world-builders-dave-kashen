# Be radically selfish about getting what you came for

The invitation Dave gives at the end of the workshop, when the formal time is over:

> Let me invite you to be radically selfish.

> If there's anything you want, here here's your time to get it.

It works because most people don't ask. Two participants said outright they wouldn't have asked their question without the permission. Dave treats the ask itself as the practice: *"I'm appreciate you being radically selfish and asking for what you want."*
