# Who you are being makes a bigger difference than what you are saying

Dave's Twitter handle used to be *@AwesomeCulture*. Years of building mission, values, value-based interview questions, culture reviews and rituals left him *"a little disheartened"*: a leader would get triggered, fear would spread, and the stated values turned into hypocrisy.

> Which is why, if I had to choose between putting effort into designing a founder's culture and putting effort into their state, I'd choose the state.

> Who you're being makes a bigger difference than what you're saying, because the moment those don't match, people believe who you're being instead.

> Your state can't help but spread through your organization.

The hard part is that you can't easily see it:

> Most of us are like fish in water, unable to see the water we're swimming in.
