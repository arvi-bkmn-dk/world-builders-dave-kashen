# Your state is contagious to your team

> Founders underestimate how energetically sensitive their teams are.

The transmission claim, stated in both directions. Operating from blame, frustration and resentment gets mirrored back. Consistently modelling curiosity instead of judgment, responsibility instead of blame, openness instead of defensiveness, presence instead of reactivity — the team rises to match.

What makes this operationally serious rather than a truism is that it runs on state, not on communication. A founder can say all the right things about psychological safety while being below the line, and the room will read the state. Which means culture work that doesn't touch the founder's state is decorative.

The payoff Dave names is specific and measurable: accountability focused on learning rather than punishment, which allows "mistakes to become information, people to tell the truth faster, and for ownership to be energizing."

> When a founder steps into creator consciousness, their culture becomes much more resilient, clear, and alive.

The same principle appears across all five frameworks — the founder's fears become the team's ceiling in [[The Founder Fear Map]], the founder's stories become the team's climate in [[Your stories set the emotional climate of your team]]. This is the general form.
