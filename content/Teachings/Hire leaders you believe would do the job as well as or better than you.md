# Hire leaders you believe would do the job as well as or better than you

> I often encourage my CEO clients to be bored in order to build a billion-dollar company.

Most founders think it's absurd. Dave's claim is that one decision makes it possible: a leadership team that can run the company without you. Most founders say it's a priority and end up with a very good team instead.

> You want to hire leaders you genuinely believe would do the job as well as or better than you could.

> When you do this, you barely have to think about them.

Then the CEO's job shifts to vision, strategy and the work only they can do, the kind of thinking time Bill Gates spent hours a day reading in. The alternative is its own kind of hard:

> When you settle for a good team instead of a truly exceptional one, you end up spending your time managing, motivating, and putting out fires.

Dave closes with [[Eric Ries]]'s line from *Incorruptible*: choose your hard.

> I understand the compulsion to settle. But don't.
