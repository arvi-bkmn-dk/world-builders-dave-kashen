# Feeling stuck comes from trying to control what you cannot control

Dave opens with gravity:

> Right now, you are stuck to the earth.

> Does that bother you?

Nobody wakes up frustrated that they can't fly. Yet founders feel stuck all the time: in a business that isn't growing, a co-founder issue, a pattern of working harder with nothing shifting. The difference, Dave says, is not the situation:

> But the feeling of stuckness isn't caused by actually being stuck. It's caused by trying to control something you can't control.

> The moment you stop trying to control what isn't yours to control, the stuckness dissolves.

This sits alongside [[Feeling stuck means you have reached your growing edge]] rather than replacing it: that note reframes stuckness as an edge worth reaching; this one names what keeps you pressed against it.
