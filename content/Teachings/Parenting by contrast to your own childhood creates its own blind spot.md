# Parenting by contrast to your own childhood creates its own blind spot

Asked how his father shaped his own fathering, Dave's answer is "mostly by contrast". He didn't like how distance felt, so he gives his boys the opposite: playful, affectionate, "I love you unconditionally" a dozen times a day. He names the cost of the overcorrection himself:

> I'm so focused on making sure I feel connected with my boys and they feel connected with me

and so

> I'm not as good about setting limits um and enforcing consequences and discipline which I believe is important for them um because I fear disconnection

Doing the opposite of your parents is still being run by your parents. The reaction sets the direction, and a different gap opens on the other side. His correction is the reframe he uses at work:

> sometimes love is fierce and firm and that doesn't feel like connection
