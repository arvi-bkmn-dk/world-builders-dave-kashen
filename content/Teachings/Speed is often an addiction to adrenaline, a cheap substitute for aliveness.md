# Speed is often an addiction to adrenaline, a cheap substitute for aliveness

The frenetic energy feels like commitment. Dave's read is that the system is simply uncomfortable without it. A second driver, he adds, is believing you have to do it all yourself: *"A lot of that, I think, comes from thinking you have to do it all yourself."*

## Also in [[NL 75 - Why Trying to Speed Up Keeps You Behind]]

> A lot of us are, in a real sense, adrenaline junkies.

> The system has simply learned to feel uncomfortable without the frenetic energy.
