# An unresisted emotion moves through the body in 60 to 90 seconds

The most concrete claim in Dave's emotional work, and the one that makes "feel it" a bounded instruction rather than an open-ended risk.

> When you stop resisting an emotion, you no longer experience it as painful. Without resistance, the energy will move through your body in 60-90 seconds. Allow it to move all the way through.

The reason this matters is that the fear underneath emotional avoidance is usually about *duration*, not intensity — that if you let yourself feel the shame or the grief, it will not stop. A stated time horizon answers that directly.

It sits inside four practices for expanding capacity, and the sequencing is deliberate:
1. **Focus on the body** — anchor in the specific physical sensations, stop constricting against them.
2. **Separate raw sensation from mental interpretation** — "most suffering comes from the story, not the emotion itself." The narrative is what extends the duration. → [[The Storyteller Mind]]
3. **Welcome the sensations** — the 60–90 seconds.
4. **Breathe and express** — breathe into it; if intense, match it with sound or movement, safely.

Step 2 is what makes step 3 achievable. An emotion held without its story is a passing physical event; the same emotion wrapped in a story about what it means about you has no natural endpoint.
