# End a visualization with or something better for the highest good of all concerned

Dave is a big believer in visualization, and he closes his own with a clause that keeps it from turning into attachment:

> or something better for the highest good of all concerned

His ego's best idea is only its best idea right now, and "something better" might not look better at first. It might be losing millions instead of making them, if that is what frees him from an attachment.

He names the trap the clause protects against. Even inside creative tension:

> if I visualize my desire people say but then I get attached to it

and at that point you're back on dirty fuel. → [[Creative Tension]] · [[Preference is not attachment]]
