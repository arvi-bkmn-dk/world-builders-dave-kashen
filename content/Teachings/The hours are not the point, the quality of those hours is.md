# The hours are not the point, the quality of those hours is

Dave refuses both sides of the hours argument.

> The goal isn't to work fewer hours. It's not even about delegation versus involvement. **The goal is flow.**

> You can work **80 hours a week in a state of flow**, doing work you love, fully engaged, energized. Or you can work **40 hours in a state of grinding**, forcing, struggling.

> The hours aren't the point. The quality of those hours is everything.

Which is why "Founder Mode" read as hustle-and-grind misses the actual opportunity — "to spend their time doing what they're uniquely great at, in ways that feel alive."

His line for it:

> **Play to win. But make sure you're actually playing.**

This is the same distinction that runs through the burnout material: the drain is resistance, not volume. → [[Burnout is caused as much by resistance as by working a lot]] · [[There is a way of creating results that feels like flow rather than grind]] · [[Force is trying to control by fighting against life, power is creating by flowing with life]]
