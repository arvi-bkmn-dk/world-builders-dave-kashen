# Five default patterns drain a leader's energy, and each has an inner key

The spine of the *Inner Keys* workshop: five human defaults that act as brakes, and the shift that releases each one.

| Default obstacle | Inner key | Where it lives in the graph |
|---|---|---|
| **Threat** — scanning for danger, constricting | **Presence** | [[Presence or threat is the simple version of above and below the line]] · [[Above and Below the Line]] |
| **Control** — managing others and ourselves | **Communicating to reveal** | [[Communicating to Control vs Reveal]] |
| **Resistance** — to unfamiliar, intense emotions | **Welcoming** | [[An emotion plus resistance is a different experience from the same emotion alone]] |
| **Rightness** — the need to be right | **Preference** — what do I actually want | [[Figuring out the right answer is a strategy for avoiding the feeling]] |
| **Fear** — lack and fear as motivation | **Inspiration** | [[Creative Tension]] · [[Clean Fuel and Dirty Fuel]] |

On control, the energy cost is concrete:

> When we're trying to control, we we're grasping, and we actually kinda kink the pipe.

On resistance:

> The emotions we resist is one of the biggest limiting factors to our energy and aliveness.

In the workshop he runs three of the five as live exercises (threat to presence, resistance to welcoming, fear to inspiration), admitting *"I have a tendency to wanna do everything, and I've been getting feedback lately that less is more."*
