# Seeing people as other is what makes violence feel inevitable

Dave's name for it is **other-ing**. From below the line, the stories we tell about other people and groups flatten them, and the most damaging flattening is making them *other*:

> The moment we stop noticing our shared humanity, our sameness, our interdependence and interconnectivity and yes, our oneness, we lose our compassion and understanding, and the path to violence becomes inevitable (a lose-lose outcome).

> When we see people as other than us, we automatically perceive them as threatening.

The cycle runs from perceived threat to disconnection, blame, provocation, defence and attack, and it isn't only physical. Dave argues that the mental and emotional forms (judgment, dislike, hate) may do more damage, because everyone lives with them every day.

The counter-move is effortful, because looking for difference is the default: deliberately look for sameness. → [[How Am I Just Like Them]] · [[Win for All]]
