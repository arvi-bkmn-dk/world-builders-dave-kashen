# To be responsible is to be response-able

Dave's etymological move, and the argument for responsibility as **power** rather than duty.

**The mechanism of the alternative** — why blame is expensive rather than merely unpleasant:

Dave quotes Eckhart Tolle: *"your happiness ultimately arises not from the circumstances of your life but from the conditioning of your mind."*

**And the standing confusion he heads off**, which is the same one he corrects on the podcast:

His joke for it, the turkey sandwich: a boy complains two days running about his lunch, his friend says *why don't you ask your parents to make something different*, and he replies — **"you leave my parents out of this. I make my own lunch."** → I make my own lunch

## Also in [[GA 01 - The Inner Game of Life, Danny Miranda Podcast 394]]

The tripping-on-a-root example. Blame the city and you learn nothing. Notice you were on your phone and you're better able to respond next time.

> So all that is to say, when we take responsibility, we become better able to respond to the same kind of situation in the future because we see ourselves as the creator of it.
