# One to three core beliefs shape everything

Dave's theory of leverage, given as the answer to *what would you do with thirty minutes to change someone's life as much as possible?*

> Every person has 1 to 3 core beliefs.

Not dozens. A handful, each of the form *X equals Y* where Y is love, safety, success or worth — and each generating a large share of a person's behaviour without ever being stated.

Jesse's was **money equals love**: "the more money I make, the more I will get love," with money-equals-security and money-equals-success stacked around it. What it had been producing downstream was a verdict — *if I'm not a billionaire, I won't be worthwhile.*

The small number is what makes the claim actionable. If suffering came from hundreds of scattered patterns, coaching would be endless triage. If it comes from one to three load-bearing stories, there is a shortest path.

Dave is also dismissive of origin work: where the belief came from is "not all that important, somewhat obvious." The work is loosening the grip, not tracing the history. → [[Core Beliefs]]
