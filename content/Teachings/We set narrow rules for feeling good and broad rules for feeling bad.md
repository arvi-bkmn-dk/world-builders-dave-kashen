# We set narrow rules for feeling good and broad rules for feeling bad

What Dave took from Tony Robbins' *Date with Destiny*. People share values but hold different internal rules for when those values are met, and there are *away* values (states we avoid) as well as *toward* values (states we seek).

His own top away value made the idea concrete:

> My top away value was Rejection and my internal rule was “anytime I”m not invited to or included in something.”

That rule explained reactions he had found inexplicable, and all the ways he had limited himself to avoid rejection. The general pattern is an asymmetry:

> We tend to make very narrow internal rules for what has to happen to experience our Toward Values (e.g., must have $25 million to feel free) and very broad values for what has to happen to experience our Away Values (e.g., not being invited to something to feel rejected).

Almost nothing qualifies as good and almost anything qualifies as bad, so life is set up to feel bad by default. Rewriting the rules, in Dave's words, is "akin to re-creating your own blueprint." → [[Tony Robbins]]
