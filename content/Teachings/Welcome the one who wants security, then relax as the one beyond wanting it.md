# Welcome the one who wants security, then relax as the one beyond wanting it

The practice Dave runs live on air, from the **Sedona Method**, applied to each of the three needs in turn.

The sequence, for each of security, control and approval:

1. **Find the one who wants it.** *"Can you find in yourself the one who wants security — that seeks safety outside yourself?"*
2. **Welcome that part.** *"Don't go making that wrong or bad. We're all human. We all have that part."*
3. **Relax as the one beyond it.** *"Just for now, could you relax as the one that's beyond wanting security?"*

Two design choices carry the whole thing.

**The wanting part is welcomed, not eliminated.** Dave frames the method as "pointing us to a deeper inquiry and connection to our true self, **without making it wrong or bad that we're human** and have these ego patterns." An attempt to stop wanting security would be another version of the same striving. → [[Welcome the shame but do not believe the thoughts that come with it]]

**"Just for now."** The invitation is temporary and therefore available. Not *become someone who needs no approval* — only relax as that one, for a moment. The phrase recurs at every step.

Dave characterises the method as "a practice of deep welcoming and deep noticing," using contrast to do the work.

Jesse's addition is how to aim it at something live: rather than the abstract need, take a specific issue — *I really want this fundraising round to go well*, *I want my wife off her phone* — and find which of the three it sits under. "They all come back to one of those three, or multiple."
