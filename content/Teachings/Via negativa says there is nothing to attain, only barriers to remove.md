# Via negativa says there is nothing to attain, only barriers to remove

> The via negativa is the idea that there's actually nothing to attain. It's always already here. There are just some things that are in the way.

Dave's structural claim about how this work proceeds, and it inverts the shape of every self-improvement project. You are not accumulating a quality you lack; you are identifying what blocks one you already have.

The Rumi line he calls a favourite:

> Your task is not to seek for love, but to identify all the barriers you've built against it and embrace them.

**Embrace** is the operative word, and it's consistent with everything else in Dave's method — [[The Gradation of Allowing]], [[If you cannot welcome the feeling, welcome the resistance]], [[No emotion is painful, the resistance to it is]]. The barrier is met rather than defeated, because fighting it is more resistance.

Read this way, the whole toolkit is subtractive. [[The Facts and Stories Exercise]] removes a belief. [[Core Beliefs]] loosens one. [[The Entitlement Practice]] removes a baseline. [[The Meditation Practice]] adds nothing at all. None of them install anything.

It is also what makes [[Chasing spirituality is another ego game|the ego's next move]] visible — if there's nothing to attain, then any version of this framed as attainment has already gone wrong, however elevated the object.

> [!quote] [[Your task is not to seek for love, but to identify the barriers you have built against it|Your task is not to seek for love, but to identify the barriers you have built against it]]

## Also in [[MB 04 - Reflections and Recommendations 2023]]

Dave's 2023 reflections open with the image, and name the purpose of his life as removing those barriers for others.

> Imagine a light bulb walking around its whole life asking “where’s the light?”

> I’ve heard this described as the Via Negativa — the path of letting go rather than of attainment.
