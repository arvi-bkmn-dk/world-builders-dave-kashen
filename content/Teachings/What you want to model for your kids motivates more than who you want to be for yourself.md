# What you want to model for your kids motivates more than who you want to be for yourself

Dave on why becoming a father gave him the courage to leave money on the table and become a coach just as his first son was born:

> what do I want to model for them yeah is in some way even more powerful motivator than who do I want to be for myself

He notices he can tolerate not being his best self when it's only about him, but not when he thinks of what his sons are watching. The same session gives the reason modelling matters more than instruction:

> who you're being and how you're being has a much greater impact than anything that you say to your kids

It's the parenting form of his leadership claim that state travels further than words. → [[Your state is contagious to your team]] · Being is the seed of doing
