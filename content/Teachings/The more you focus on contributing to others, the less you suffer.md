# The more you focus on contributing to others, the less you suffer

A single line in Dave's 2023 reflections, stated as something he noticed rather than argued:

> The more I focus on contributing to others, and the less I focus on myself, the less I suffer. It’s the ultimate win-win.

It fits the rest of his account of suffering. If suffering comes from managing an image of yourself, then attention pointed at someone else is attention not spent on that management. He pairs it in the same list with the observation that surrender makes his coaching more powerful: the less effort goes into himself, the more comes through. → [[Suffering is a byproduct of misidentification]] · [[Win for All]]
