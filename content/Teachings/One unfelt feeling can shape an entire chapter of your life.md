# One unfelt feeling can shape an entire chapter of your life

> One of my longtime clients didn't know he was sad.

In a lawyer's office, going through a sale, the market wasn't giving him the number he'd always had in his head. He wanted to sell and be done, and didn't realise he was grieving the exit he'd imagined.

> He thought he was being rational. So instead of feeling the sadness, he built around it.

The contract he signed included a call option at exactly that number. He ran the company five more years, and the enterprise value didn't move by a dollar.

> one unfelt feeling had shaped an entire chapter of his life.

> Most people are organizing their entire lives around the unwillingness to feel a single feeling, instead of around what they actually want.

When he finally felt it, he let go of the number, brought in a new CEO, and sold, and says he's happier and more peaceful for it.
