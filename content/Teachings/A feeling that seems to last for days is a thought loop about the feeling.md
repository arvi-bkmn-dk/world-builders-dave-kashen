# A feeling that seems to last for days is a thought loop about the feeling

Emotions are physical before they're conceptual, and felt directly, they are fast:

> when you're actually feeling an emotion, it tends to move through quickly, often in under two minutes.

So a feeling that seems to have been with you for days, or years, is usually something else:

> you're likely in a thought loop about the feeling rather than actually feeling it.

The mind generates stories about what the emotion means, and those can run indefinitely. The practice is the opposite:

> What you're practicing is contact with the sensation itself.
