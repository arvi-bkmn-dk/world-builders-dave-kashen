# Closing your heart does not protect you from the pain of leadership, it makes you carry it alone

Some founders keep their team at a distance on purpose, because they might have to fire someone someday. Dave says it works the other way:

> Ironically, the more connected you are, the more people know you care about them, and the easier it becomes to give hard feedback.

> Closing your heart doesn't protect you from the pain of leadership. It just guarantees you carry that pain alone.

The same logic applies to the conversations you avoid:

> Avoiding a hard conversation means swallowing what you actually think, feel, and want.

The hard conversation with a co-founder, a partner or a team member reconnects you twice: to yourself, because you finally said the true thing, and to them, because now they know the real you.

> each one reconnects you twice.
