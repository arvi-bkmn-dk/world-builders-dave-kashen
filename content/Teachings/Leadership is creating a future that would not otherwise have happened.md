# Leadership is creating a future that would not otherwise have happened

Dave opens the workshop by defining the word before teaching anything about it:

> One of my favorite definitions of a leader is somebody who creates a future that otherwise would not have occurred.

The definition moves leadership away from title and authority toward *creation*. By this measure a founder managing a company that would have grown the same way without them is not yet leading, and someone with no title who changes where things are heading is.

He is explicit that it isn't founder-only: *"if you have a different context for being a leader, feel free to kinda translate what I'm saying."* It is also what makes [[Creative Tension]] a leadership tool rather than a personal one: a declared future that the team pulls toward.
