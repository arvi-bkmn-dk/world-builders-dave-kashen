# Integrating a transformation looks like a toddler learning to walk

Asked by a participant coming out of a big personal shift how to handle falling back into old patterns, Dave's first thought is pendulation:

> They're like, you know, few steps and then they tumble, and then a few more steps and then they tumble. So transformation is like that.

> It's actually the natural way that your system's integrating it.

The practical value is what it prevents: reading a stumble as proof the change was never real. *"That's useful to know so you don't go, oh, shit. I lost it. It's all gone. I've been there."*
