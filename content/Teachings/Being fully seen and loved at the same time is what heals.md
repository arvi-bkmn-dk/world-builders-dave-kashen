# Being fully seen and loved at the same time is what heals

Dave's account of why the appreciation work is more than pleasant.

> One of the most powerful experiences you can have is to feel fully seen and loved at the same time. Most people are terrified to feel fully seen, because they think they won't be loved — because there's all this shame and all these stories.

The two conditions have to hold **simultaneously**, and shame's whole structure is the belief that they can't. If what you actually are were visible, the love would stop; so you show an edited version and receive love for that, which never reaches the hidden part and therefore never disconfirms anything.

Andrew is the live demonstration, and Dave names it to his face:

> Polished visionary, that's good. So I just want you to see the polished visionary, and let's hide away the vulnerable part where I don't know what I'm doing or I have doubt.

Which is why Dave keeps appreciating Andrew for precisely the thing Andrew wants edited out — his vulnerability — and why Andrew keeps refusing it. The appreciation is aimed at the hidden part, which is the only place it could do any work.

> To be fully revealed — in all of you — and loved at the same time is deeply healing.

The five hours of appreciations at the Sun Valley retreat is the scaled version: "people were feeling seen in a way they maybe never felt seen."
