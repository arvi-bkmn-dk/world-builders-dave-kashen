# Strip away what you make it mean and support becomes available

Dave's diagnosis of why he refused help for so long:

> What kept me from accepting support for so long was making it mean something about me.

> Taking meds felt like cheating. It felt like proof that the inner work wasn't working.

> Strip that meaning away, and the support becomes available.

He is explicit that the lesson isn't about mental health:

> Because this isn't limited to only mental health.

> The bigger question is: where in your life would no longer making something mean something about you finally give you permission to let support in?

For founders the obvious candidates are hiring help, a coach, a co-founder, or delegating, anywhere accepting support has been filed as evidence of inadequacy.
