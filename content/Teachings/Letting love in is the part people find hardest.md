# Letting love in is the part people find hardest

The **love seat**, from the second Sun Valley retreat:

> Each person sat for five minutes while the rest of the group shared what they appreciated about them.

> It sounds simple. **But it was the thing people were most nervous about.** Hearts racing. Voices shaking. **Just how difficult it is for most of us to simply let love in.**

A room of founders who raise money from strangers and fire people — undone by five minutes of being appreciated with nothing to do but receive it. The difficulty isn't the exposure, it's that there is no task in it. → [[The acknowledgement party flips from asking to giving]] · [[Deflecting a compliment happens in layers and starts with feeling good]]

Dave folds receiving into the definition of having it all:

> That's an essential part of 'having it all' too: **not just achieving, but receiving** and actually letting yourself feel loved. Feel joy. Feel peace. **Not deferring those feelings to some future milestone, but allowing yourself to experience them now.**

Which is the same claim as [[The less attached you are to an outcome, the easier it is to create]] arriving from the other side: the feelings are available now, and the capacity to receive them is the thing to build.

One founder's verdict on the retreat: *"more powerful than a psychedelic journey because the profound shifts happened while sober and felt a lot more integrated and sustainable."* → [[Rejecting humanity is just ego in disguise]]
