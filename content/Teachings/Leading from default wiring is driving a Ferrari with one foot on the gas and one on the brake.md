# Leading from default wiring is driving a Ferrari with one foot on the gas and one on the brake

Dave's analogy for why capable founders burn out:

> You're a Ferrari, or you're driving a Ferrari, but you have your foot your left foot on the gas and your right foot on the brake.

The answer from the room was burned-out tires, and Dave agrees: you won't go as fast or as far, and you'll burn out, because you're exerting energy in one direction while your system pushes the other.

> It's like we wanna move in a direction, but so much of our internal wiring, our internal system is actually hitting the brakes at the same time

The brakes are the default patterns, and taking your foot off them adds no horsepower; it releases what was already there. That's why he focuses on the inner game: *"your outer results are a reflection of your inner world."*
