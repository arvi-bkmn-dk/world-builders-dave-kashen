# Leaders need conviction, not certainty

Asked how founders can be real with their teams, Dave starts with a distinction:

> conviction versus certainty I think this is a really important distinction

What stops founders from being authentic is believing they must have certainty: know all the answers, never admit fear. Dave thinks that belief is false, and self-defeating:

> if you feel like you have to pretend certainty I know we're going to get there I know how we're going to get there when you don't have it you know people smell that

Conviction is different and essential: holding a compelling vision and believing in the team. His ideal is both at once, *I don't know all the answers, I believe in us, I'm scared too sometimes, and I know we have what it takes*. He credits [[Patrick Lencioni]]'s *The Five Temptations of a CEO* with naming the invulnerability trap, and ties vulnerability to trust. → [[Trust is an efficiency multiple]]
