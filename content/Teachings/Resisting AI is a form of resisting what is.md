# Resisting AI is a form of resisting what is

One of Dave's clients measured the productivity gap between engineers fully using AI tools and those who weren't.

> The gap was 30x.

> Not 30%. Thirty times.

For founders not building AI, *"the treadmill sped up."* For their engineers, the resistance is human and understandable, and Dave applies the same principle he applies to any reality:

> Resisting AI is a form of resisting what is.

> It's like saying I wish today was Monday when it's Tuesday. You're going to suffer.

For the leader, that means holding two things:

> So you hold both: empathy and being grounded in reality.

> But you need to be grounded in the reality that if you don't adapt, probably your company won't make it, and then everyone loses their job.

And a reframe for the engineers who do adopt it, from a client who went into software because it felt like casting spells:

> AI doesn't take the magic away. It makes you a more powerful magician.
