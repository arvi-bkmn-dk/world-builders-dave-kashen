# Sharing what you have been withholding brings you closer, not further apart

The second purpose of feedback, the one leaders miss:

> The less obvious purpose of feedback is connection. When I finally share the thing I've been withholding from you, we end up closer, not further apart.

Dave defines candor with the [[Conscious Leadership Group]]:

> The Conscious Leadership Group describes it as the intersection of three things: honesty, openness, and self-awareness. Not just saying true things, but saying all of what's true for you.

> And connection compounds. The more connected your team, the more trust there is, and the more powerful the team ultimately becomes.

The fear is that honesty will cost the relationship. Dave's experience is the reverse: what you withhold is what puts distance between you. → [[Withhold, withdraw, project is how relationships calcify]]
