# You can care about your impact without controlling someone else's experience

Putting other people's feelings in the *can't control* folder can sound like not caring. Dave closes that gap with his marriage:

> I don't believe I "make" my wife upset. I see her as the creator of her own experience.

> So I choose differently.

He knows that, given how she's organised herself, snapping at her tends to lead to her feeling upset. He doesn't own her upset, and he doesn't use that as licence either.

> That's the distinction. You take responsibility for your own state and being in presence, which naturally influences others in a positive way, without attempting to control their experience.
