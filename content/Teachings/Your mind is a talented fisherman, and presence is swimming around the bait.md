# Your mind is a talented fisherman, and presence is swimming around the bait

Dave's metaphor for step one of the inner game, once you've seen that you're the listener, not the talker:

> imagine you're this happy little fish swimming along in the present and your mind is just like talented fisherman and it knows just what bait to use

*Oh you screwed that up. Oh what if this happens.* The job isn't to stop the fisherman casting. It's to swim around the bait, or, if you notice you're hooked, to unhook and come back to the present.

The metaphor carries the attitude. Getting hooked isn't a failure, because the fisherman is skilled and knows exactly what bait works on you. → [[Identify as the listener rather than the talker]]
