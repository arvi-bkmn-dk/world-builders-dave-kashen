# A belief is just a deeply held story

The move that puts beliefs within reach of the same tools as everything else.

> A belief is really just a fancy way of saying deeply held story. It's not a fact.

Once a belief is reclassified as a story, the whole apparatus of [[The Storyteller Mind]] applies to it — it can be named, asked about, and tested with *how could the opposite be true?* Left in the category of "belief," it sits closer to identity and gets defended rather than examined.

The difference from an ordinary passing story is depth and reach, not kind. Jesse's *money equals love* wasn't a thought he had on a Tuesday; it was, in his words, "really showing up everywhere in my life" — in what he did, how he thought, and the suffering he generated for himself.

This is also why Dave's thirty-minute answer is beliefs rather than behaviours. Behaviours are downstream of a handful of stories held so long they stopped presenting as stories. → [[One to three core beliefs shape everything]]
