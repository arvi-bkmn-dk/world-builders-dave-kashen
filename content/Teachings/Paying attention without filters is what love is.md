# Paying attention without filters is what love is

Dave describes true listening as the attention of a scientist absorbed in a subject: wonder, fascination, curiosity, no agenda. Then he turns the same attention inward, noticing a feeling the way you'd notice every line and curve of a flower.

His conclusion joins three things he usually teaches separately, presence, love and seeing clearly:

> This quality of attention (toward yourself, toward others, toward whatever is here) is what love is.

> That's what it means to see reality clearly.

> And the team that sees reality clearly wins.

So love here isn't a feeling added on top of attention. It is the attention itself, once nothing is filtering it, and that same unfiltered attention is what lets a team see what is actually happening.
