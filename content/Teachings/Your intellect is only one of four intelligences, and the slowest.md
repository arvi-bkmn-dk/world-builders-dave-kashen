# Your intellect is only one of four intelligences, and the slowest

A client's team found a perfect CTO. At a happy hour, the client's wife shook his hand once. On the drive home:

> "I can't tell you that. I just got a weird feeling."

That was Thursday. On Monday the CTO didn't show up, and at one o'clock he reneged by email.

> My client's wife couldn't explain what she felt, because it didn't come from the part of us that explains things.

> But most founders are running every decision through the part of us that explains things.

| Intelligence | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Head** | thinks: breaks problems into steps, imagines what doesn't exist yet; the slowest of the four |
| **Heart** | the emotional centre and the centre of desire |
| **Gut** | intuition: a grounded knowing that arrives instantly |
| **Universal** | not limited to the body; the mind as a receiver more than a generator |

Two tells Dave gives. Between head and heart:

> Following what your head wants requires effort and discipline. Following what your heart wants is effortless.

And for the gut:

> If I ask someone what their gut says and they take a while to answer, they're not answering from their gut.

With a caveat: fear and shame can block the gut, which is why *trust your gut* fails as advice on its own.

> Your intellect can only take you where it can already see. The other three know the way to everything else.
