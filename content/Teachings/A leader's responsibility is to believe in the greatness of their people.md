# A leader's responsibility is to believe in the greatness of their people

Dave's line to clients who can't let someone go:

> a leader's responsibility is to believe in the greatness of their people. And if you can't, let them go find a leader who can.

Holding on is usually framed as kindness. Dave frames it as a hero move:

> Holding onto someone you should let go is a hero move.

> But can you see them as a powerful human capable of being with those feelings, rather than as a victim you need to protect from them?

> Keeping them isn't protecting them.
