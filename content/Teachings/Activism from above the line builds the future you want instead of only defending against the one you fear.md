# Activism from above the line builds the future you want instead of only defending against the one you fear

With a force as big as AI, Dave names the trap: reacting from fear, looping in doom, freezing or thrashing.

> Activism from below the line says, this is bad, this is a problem, and stops there.

> Activism from above the line is different.

Get present to what's actually happening, welcome the fear instead of bracing against it, and then the step most people skip:

> imagine the most beautiful future you can, and bring your creativity to building that, instead of just defending against the one you're afraid of.

> Beliefs tend to be self-fulfilling. If too many of us stay in doom, no one brings the creativity required to build the better version.

> The shift is from victim to creator.

> what's mine to do here, and how can I contribute to the future I want to live in?
