# Ask whether you want to share a feeling for you or for them

Dave's guardrail for leaders being emotionally open at work. He finds it absurd that leaders are expected to have no emotions, and he also warns against the other extreme. The question he asks:

> are you wanting to share that feeling for you or for them

A leader's primary function is to serve. Unburdening your fear onto the team is different from letting them see your fear so they have permission to feel theirs. The first belongs with a coach or peers; the second builds connection. Neither is a rule. It's discernment, moment to moment, and it gets clearer the more present you are. → [[The art of leadership is discernment]]

## Also in [[NL 82 - It's Only Lonely at the Top If You Can't Be Yourself There]]

Applied to a lonely founder: share with the full team only what serves them, and find two or three people on the leadership team for the rest.

> Most people wildly overestimate how invulnerable they need to seem.
