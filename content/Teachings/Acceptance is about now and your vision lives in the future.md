# Acceptance is about now and your vision lives in the future

At a workshop for the [[One Mind Accelerator]], founders building mental health technology raised the concern Dave hears constantly: if I accept myself and my startup as they are, will I lose my fire? For them it was sharper, because the conviction that people shouldn't suffer felt inseparable from the mission.

Dave's answer is about scope:

> Accepting what's true right now, in this moment. That's the whole scope of it.

> It says nothing about the future.

> Acceptance is about now. Your vision lives in the future. They don't cancel each other out.

So the move is to accept that people suffer from mental health issues today, imagine a world where they don't, and work from that image. The alternative has a cost:

> One keeps you present and open. One keeps you in a constant low-grade state of resistance to reality, which is exhausting, and over time, corrosive to the work.

> Telling yourself the world shouldn't be this way is no different than insisting the sky shouldn't be blue.
