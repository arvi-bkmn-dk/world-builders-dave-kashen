# The healthiest co-founders set aside time to talk about the relationship itself

He quotes a mentor on why it's worth it:

> I had a mentor who said once that our most fulfilling lives and relationships are one five minute sweaty palm conversation away.

Skipping those conversations lets withholds build into resentment, which changes the trajectory of the whole relationship.

## Also in [[NL 65 - Co-Founder Relationships That Can Navigate Anything]]

Restated in writing, with the mentor's line recast as a deposit:

> Every one of those conversations is a deposit.
