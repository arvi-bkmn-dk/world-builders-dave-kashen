# You block yourself from feeling it now by believing you need the conditions to feel it

Dave states this twice because Jesse nearly moves past it.

> I believe **when I create those conditions in the future, then I'll finally feel the way I want to feel.**

> And the irony is: **the way you block yourself from feeling the way you want to feel right now is by telling yourself you need these conditions to feel it.**

Then again, sharper:

> The nuance I wanna add, just to re-emphasise — **the way you're blocking yourself from being happy now is by believing you need the $40 million to be happy.**

The belief is not merely mistaken. It is **the active mechanism** of the deprivation. Nothing about the present moment prevents the feeling; the rule *not yet, not until* does — and that rule is self-issued, renewed daily, and survives every milestone because it was never about the milestone.

Dave names why it is defended so hard:

> **The whole thing is like a setup.** Because it's like — *okay, well I don't wanna feel happy now, because then I won't be motivated to go get the $40 million that I need to make me happy.*

Which is the dirty-fuel bargain in one sentence: happiness withheld deliberately, as the engine. → [[Clean Fuel and Dirty Fuel]] · [[The less attached you are to an outcome, the easier it is to create]]

Jesse's plain version for founders: *"if you aren't happy right now, 40 million or a hundred million or a billion — it's just not gonna make you happy."*
