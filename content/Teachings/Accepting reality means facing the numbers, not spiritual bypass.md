# Accepting reality means facing the numbers, not spiritual bypass

Dave heads off the two misreadings of acceptance that make entrepreneurs reject it.

**It is not spiritual bypass.** "Some people refer to it as spiritual bypass — like it's all good, man, we just accept everything as it is. That's not what we're talking about either." Acceptance is not a mood you apply over the facts.

**It is not resignation.** Asked whether accepting a 10% conversion rate means settling for it, Dave says the opposite — acceptance *requires* being more grounded in the number, not less:

> When we talk about accepting reality as it is, included in that is really being grounded in reality. Not a head in the sand, ignoring, pretending it's not true or ignoring the numbers. Really knowing what is so. Okay, it is 10%, and facing that and feeling whatever you feel around it.

Jesse names the failure mode from the founder's side, and includes himself in it: entrepreneurs are "rose-coloured glasses people" who will look at a number and immediately asterisk it — "it is 10%, but that's just 'cause so-and-so was on vacation." The number was seen and then neutralised before it could land.

Dave's clarification of *what* is being accepted resolves the apparent paradox. Reality means the present and the past — "it's part of the written record" — which is categorically different from the future, "which we actually can influence." You accept what is already fixed. You do not accept what has not happened yet.

And the emotional range stays open. "You might be angry, and that's totally welcome. There might be a real sense of guys, this isn't okay with me." Acceptance is not equanimity about the result; it is refusing to argue with what already occurred.

## Also in [[GA 01 - The Inner Game of Life, Danny Miranda Podcast 394]]

The interview takes it to war and child poverty. Acceptance applies to what is and what has been, both part of the written record, and it is what makes creating peace possible.

> If I can accept everything as it is, I enter a state of peace from which I'm more available to create peace in the world.
