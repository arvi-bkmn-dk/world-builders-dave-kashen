# We are wired for a threat level of ten in a world that is mostly a one

Dave's evolutionary account of why below the line is the default. The brain didn't upgrade in place. It is layered, reptilian inside mammalian inside prefrontal, "all these different species in one", and the oldest layers only care about survival:

> a lot of our more primitive parts of our brain are primarily concerned with the question, Am I dead yet?

So we scan for what's wrong, and we're very good at it. The problem is calibration:

> This is what's so pernicious, I think, about our wiring, is that we're like wired for a threat level 10, even though we live in a world where threat level is actually 1.

He says the neurobiology hasn't evolved as fast as the environment. That is why a log in the river reads as a crocodile, and why the ego mind, a problem-solving machine, keeps finding problems to justify itself. → [[Above and Below the Line]] · [[Your mind tells stories with problems in them because problems hold attention]]
