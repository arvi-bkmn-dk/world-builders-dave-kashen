# We do not see the world as it is, we see the world as we are

The mechanism that makes a persona self-confirming.

> We don't tend to see the world as it is. We see the world as we are. And so we'll project our personas onto the world.

Dave runs it through the firefighter. Someone identified as the one who stays cool in a crisis will **filter** — "only see the crises, 'cause to a firefighter everything's a fire" — and will also **create or attract** them, drawing in people who read situations as emergencies.

> Our world becomes like this self-validating experience where yeah, everything's a fire and I'm a firefighter.

The clinical detail is that the person experiences this as something happening *to* them:

> Lots of clients are sort of bemoaning it — oh God, Dave, I'm constantly having to fight fires. Not realising that they're actually attracting, creating, perceiving the world as a series of fires so that their persona gets validated.

The complaint and the identity are the same structure. Which is why the situation never resolves by handling the fires: handling them is what the persona is for.

Dave extends it to goals. A founder who draws worth from rescuing "may actually create situations where they have to come in and save the day" — unattainable targets included. → [[Heroes require victims]]
