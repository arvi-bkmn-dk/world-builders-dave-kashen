# The Seven Core Distinctions

## What It Is

Seven paired distinctions, each an *X over Y*. Dave notes the order is deliberate and that the arc builds toward the last one.

| # | Distinction | The move |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Connection over perfection** | stop getting it right, start connecting with the human |
| 2 | **Enjoy over manage** | float the river rather than steering it |
| 3 | **Feel over figure** | feel the feeling instead of thinking your way around it |
| 4 | **Want over should** | act from authentic wants, not inherited obligations |
| 5 | **Authenticity over improvement** | discover who you are rather than fixing who you are |
| 6 | **Empower over power** | be fully authentic regardless of consequences |
| 7 | **Love over defense** | live undefended |

## The Model

**1 — Connection over perfection.** Perfecting the thing blinds you to the person. The team that spends a year building the perfect product nobody wants, versus the team that connects with its market and iterates. Dave's note: "connection is felt" — it is not a checklist of rapport moves.
→ [[Connection is felt, not checked off]]

**2 — Enjoy over manage.** Life flows whether or not you steer it. Two consequences: you can enjoy the thing you are already doing without changing it, and enjoyment is *more* efficient than discipline, not less.
→ [[How much more could you just enjoy the things you are already doing]] · [[The more you enjoy it, the more efficient and effective you are]] · [[If the person wants to do the thing you need them to do, they do not require a lot of management]]

**3 — Feel over figure.** Already in this graph as [[Feel Over Figure]]. Going to the mind to figure out what to do is usually a way of not feeling; clarity is on the other side of the feeling.
→ [[90% of the time the issues my clients bring are literally not real]]

**4 — Want over should.** Most people run on should. "According to whom?" The leadership version is startlingly simple: tell people what you want.
→ [[Did you tell them you want them to do it]]

**5 — Authenticity over improvement.** Nature grows without instructing itself to. Self-improvement launched from *something is wrong with me* is the deferred life.
→ [[Nature grows and we are part of nature]] · [[If you could not improve anything about yourself, what would you have to let go of to feel peaceful]]

**6 — Empower over power.** Power is what you reach for when you do not want to feel fear. Empowerment is "being fully authentic regardless of the consequences" — Superman on a mountain with no kryptonite.
→ [[Empower is being fully authentic regardless of the consequences]]

**7 — Love over defense.** We learn in the first eight years to close our hearts to feel safe. What gets defended is the ego — so recognising that it is not who you are is what makes living undefended possible.
→ [[What you are defending tends to be your ego]] · [[Empowerment and love meet at the top of the mountain]]

## How Dave Uses It

He had just finished the ten-week Masterclass the day of recording. He is explicit that the course works because it is "highly experiential — very little content and a lot of practice exercises," and that concepts alone do not shift patterns: "if it's just an intellectual concept but you haven't had the shift somatically, you just don't shift the patterns."
