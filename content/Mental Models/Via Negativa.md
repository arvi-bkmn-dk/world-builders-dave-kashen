# Via Negativa

## What It Is

The structural correction Dave applies to his own field, and it's aimed at the trap people fall into immediately after being convinced by him.

> A lot of people, from their ego, will be like: okay, it's not about chasing money, it's about chasing spirituality. That's the better thing to chase and attain. And that also is just another ego game.

→ [[Chasing spirituality is another ego game]]

**Via negativa** is the idea that there is nothing to attain. The thing is already present; there are only barriers in the way.

> Your task is not to seek for love, but to identify all the barriers you've built against it and embrace them. — Rumi

## The Model

**1. The thing being sought is already here.** Aliveness, freedom, peace, fulfilment, wholeness — "they're always already here." Which means seeking is the wrong verb; it presumes absence. → [[Aliveness, freedom, peace and wholeness are already here]]

**2. What's in the way is thoughts and beliefs you made up.**

> When you get still and start noticing all the thoughts and beliefs that are in the way of recognising that, then you have the opportunity to see through those thoughts and beliefs — which are just made up, your mind just made them up.

**3. Embrace the barriers rather than defeat them.** Rumi's phrasing is exact, and it's consistent with [[The Gradation of Allowing]] — the barrier is met, not fought.

**4. The beggar's chest.** Dave's image: a man begs his whole life sitting on a chest. At the end he finally opens it — bars of gold, the whole time.

## How Dave Uses It

As a guard against the achievement frame reasserting itself in spiritual clothing. Everything in his work that could become a target — presence, non-attachment, emotional capacity, wholeness — is vulnerable to being pursued the way a founder pursues revenue, at which point it becomes [[Bigger is better is a treadmill that never arrives|another treadmill]] with better vocabulary.

It also explains why his practice is subtractive. [[The Meditation Practice]] adds nothing. [[The Facts and Stories Exercise]] removes a belief. [[The Entitlement Practice]] removes a baseline. The via negativa is the logic they share.

Its clearest statement is [[You are already whole, perfect and complete]].
