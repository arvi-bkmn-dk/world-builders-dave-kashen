# Are you willing to have what you want

A question Dave asked Jesse years ago that Jesse still repeats, and its value is that it refuses to resolve into one meaning. Three readings surfaced in the episode, all live at once:

**1. Will you accept and enjoy the life you already have?** By definition you are doing a series of things to produce the life you're living. Are you willing to just have it?

**2. Will you do what's necessary to get what you want?** The ordinary reading, and the one an ambitious person hears first.

**3. Will you believe you deserve it?** Dave's addition, and the one he thinks is load-bearing:

> Most people believe they have to become something they're not already in order to have what they want. If you really look at your life — and I relate to this for much of my life — it was like: it's coming soon. At that next milestone, then I'll deserve it.

Jesse's description of the question as "screwy, because it pushes you in both directions at the same time" is the point rather than a flaw. It holds acceptance and ambition together, which is the same double move as [[Creative tension requires accepting current reality, not making it wrong]].

Dave's parallel: *love what you do* has the same ambiguity — do you only do things you already love, or do you add enjoyment to what you're doing by being present to it?
