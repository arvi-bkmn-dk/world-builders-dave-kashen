# The Law of Expanding Possibilities

## What It Is

The most far-reaching of the five, and the one that reframes business problems as nervous-system problems.

> The scope of what's possible for you is directly determined by your capacity to be with intense feelings.

Not better tactics, more discipline, clearer strategy or sharper execution. Capacity. → [[Your results are limited by which feelings you are unwilling to feel]]

## The Model

**Step 1 — Emotional capacity sets the leadership ceiling.** Plot capacity-to-feel on one axis and scope-of-possibility on the other; they move together. Every expansion — bigger vision, more capital, a larger team, bigger customers — *raises emotional intensity*. Fear rises, self-doubt appears, shame surfaces, uncertainty expands. That isn't a sign something is wrong; it's the signature of growth.

**Step 2 — Recognise the comfort zone.** Inside it, intensity stays under your threshold and everything feels manageable. When the company outgrows the founder's capacity, one of three things happens:
1. You shrink the vision (you can't hold the weight of a bigger one).
2. You escape into coping behaviour (overworking, overthinking, numbing, avoidance).
3. You collapse under pressure (self-doubt, overwhelm, shutdown, defensiveness).

> Most founders misdiagnose these as performance issues. They're emotional capacity issues.

→ [[Emotional capacity problems get misdiagnosed as performance problems]]

**Step 3 — Expand capacity.** Four practices:
1. **Focus on the body** — anchor attention in physical sensation; stop constricting against it.
2. **Separate raw sensation from mental interpretation** — the story extends the feeling, not the feeling itself. → [[The Storyteller Mind]]
3. **Welcome the sensations** — unresisted, the energy moves through in 60–90 seconds. → [[An unresisted emotion moves through the body in 60 to 90 seconds]]
4. **Breathe and express** — breathe into it; match intensity with sound or movement if needed.

Trained like a gym: reps of being with a feeling build capacity for that feeling. → [[Treat the startup as an emotional intensity gym]]

## How Dave Uses It

It reverses the usual causality — capacity first, possibility second — which is why he'll work on a founder's ability to feel shame or fear when the presenting problem is a hiring decision or a stalled raise.

> Intensity becomes fuel, not threat. Uncertainty becomes creative space, not danger. Doubt becomes information, not something about your identity.

> Unicorn founders aren't the ones who feel less. They're the ones who can feel more without shutting down.
