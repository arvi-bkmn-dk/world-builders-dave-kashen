# All models are false, good ones are useful

The preamble Dave opens his trainings with, and the licence under which everything else in the corpus should be read.

The word *map* is the tell. A map is judged by whether it gets you somewhere, not by whether it resembles the terrain — and every framework in this vault is offered on that basis. So the right question about [[Above and Below the Line]], [[The Five Core Emotions]] or [[Fight is villain, flight is hero, freeze is victim|the drama triangle]] is never *is this true?* but **does using it change what I can see and do?**

It also pre-empts the most common objection to inner-game material, by conceding it up front. Dave isn't claiming the two-state model is how minds actually work; he's claiming it is a good enough approximation to act on — and inviting the skepticism rather than defending against it.

This pairs with [[Creating distinctions gives you power]]: a distinction doesn't have to be ontologically real to give you resolution you didn't have. The mechanic's parts list is also a simplification.
