# The Responsibility Roadmap

## What It Is

A map of the fork every founder hits the instant a situation occurs. Dave's counterintuitive claim is that what blocks responsibility is not weakness or avoidance — it is **interpreting the situation as a problem**. That single interpretation routes you onto the victim path automatically.

> Taking responsibility for your current situation empowers you to create the future you want.

## The Model

Two paths from the same neutral facts:

1. Situation → **"This is a problem"** → Blame → **Powerless Victim**
2. Situation → **"This is what is"** → Responsibility → **Empowered Creator**

Dave notes these correspond to the Conscious Leadership Group's Below the Line / Above the Line. → [[Presence or threat is the simple version of above and below the line]]

**Step 1 — Start with the facts, which are always neutral.** We missed the deadline. A key hire backed out. Revenue came in below plan. Nothing has gone wrong yet.

**Step 2 — Recognise the victim path.** Labelling it a problem makes the mind ask *who's to blame?* — including self-blame ("I should have known better"). Blame buys a moment of righteousness and costs you your power. → [[Interpreting a situation as a problem is what drops you into blame]]

The cultural cost is specific: people hide mistakes, risk-taking slows, challenging conversations get avoided, and accountability starts to feel like punishment.

**Step 3 — Take the empowered path: "it is what is."** Acceptance is load-bearing, not passive. → [[You cannot change what you do not accept]] · [[Acceptance is the doorway to effective action, not apathy]]

**Step 4 — Ask the creator question: "How am I creating this?"** Aimed at empowerment, not self-blame. Have I set clear expectations? Did I withhold feedback? Did I assume someone understood? Am I modelling what I want? → [[How am I creating this is the question that converts acceptance into power]]

**Step 5 — Take aligned action without drama.** Once grounded, the next step is usually obvious: a clearer agreement, the avoided conversation, clarified ownership, a hiring or firing decision.

**Step 6 — Model creator consciousness; your state is contagious.** → [[Your state is contagious to your team]]

## How Dave Uses It

As the mechanism behind accountability that doesn't feel punitive. When accountability is focused on learning rather than blame, mistakes become information and people tell the truth faster — which is a performance outcome, not a cultural nicety.
