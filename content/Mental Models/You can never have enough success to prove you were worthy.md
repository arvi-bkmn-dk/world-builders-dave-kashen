# You can never have enough success to prove you were worthy

Dave's diagnosis of the dominant motivational engine in his industry, and why it cannot deliver.

The costs, from his own first startup: *"we worked around the clock, I was tethered to my phone, slept poorly, let many of my relationships languish, and was stressed out most of the time."*

That last clause is the whole argument. The project fails not because the bar is too high but because it is answering a question that was never valid — so no evidence can settle it.

→ [[Core Beliefs]] · [[No matter what external conditions you create, there you are]] · [[I felt more pressure to not fail than to succeed]]

## Also in [[GA 01 - The Inner Game of Life, Danny Miranda Podcast 394]]

Dave's own 25 years of it: investment banking, a hedge fund, the best schools and a startup, all inside a story that he was unworthy.

> you can't fill an imaginary void, right?
