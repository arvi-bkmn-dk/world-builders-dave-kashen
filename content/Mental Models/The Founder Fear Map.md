# The Founder Fear Map

## What It Is

Five core fears, each producing one predictable leadership breakdown, each of which then reproduces itself in the team. It is step 1 of [[The Edge Expansion Process]] and the most directly diagnostic thing in Dave's toolkit — a founder can usually identify their own in under a minute.

## The Model

| Core fear | Leadership breakdown | What the team learns |
|---|---|---|
| **Fear of judgment** | Lack of authenticity | Focus on optics and politics; mistakes hidden; energy drains |
| **Fear of failure** | Lack of risk-taking | Play small, wait for permission, innovation slows |
| **Fear of conflict** | Lack of candor | Tension goes underground; underperformance tolerated |
| **Fear of letting go** | Lack of delegation | Ownership stops; you become the bottleneck |
| **Fear of being wrong** | Lack of decisiveness | Clarity vanishes; meetings drag; priorities muddle |

**The crossing move for each** — the unfamiliar path:
- *Judgment* — be yourself even when investors and team give conflicting advice.
- *Failure* — go for it even though you might fail.
- *Conflict* — have the sweaty-palmed conversation; give direct feedback.
- *Letting go* — delegate, and let your leaders fail, learn and grow.
- *Being wrong* — make bold choices in the face of uncertainty.

Dave's note on **fear of letting go** is the sharpest line in the map: *your overfunctioning enables others to underfunction.* Covering gaps teaches the team that you will always step in.

> These fears aren't logical. They're physiological.

## How Dave Uses It

Diagnostically and in both directions — from symptom to fear as much as fear to symptom. A team that won't make decisions points back at a founder's fear of being wrong; tolerated underperformance points at fear of conflict. The organisational symptom is the visible end of an emotional edge.
