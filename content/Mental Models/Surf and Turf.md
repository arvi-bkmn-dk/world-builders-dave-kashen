# Surf and Turf

## What It Is

Dave's name for one of his core values, and his refusal of the founder's central either/or.

> One of my core values I jokingly refer to as **"surf and turf." I want to have it all. Both the steak and the fish.**

> I don't believe you have to choose between the inner path and the outer path. You can do the deep inner work **and** still build a thriving company. You can experience peace, stillness and presence **AND** still be fiercely ambitious.

> That's what surf and turf means. **Not settling. Not placing conditions on what's possible. Not buying into the false binary.**

## The Model

The belief it replaces, in founders' own words: *"it just feels like the only way to build a successful company is to sacrifice everything else: my health, my family, my peace of mind. And even then, I'm not sure it's enough."*

> This either/or, binary thinking typically shows up when we're **below the line**, operating from fear and scarcity. → [[Above and Below the Line]] · [[Finite is not the same as scarce]]

What makes the model credible is that Dave **concedes the counter-evidence** instead of arguing with it:

> There are so many examples that seem to confirm it. **Elon Musk. Travis Kalanick.** Many fear-based founders and toxic cultures **do** seem to create massive valuations.

> So here's what I said to the group: **Yes, we know you can build a big company from fear, lack and stress. But let's raise the bar. Because you can also build a big company from love, presence and a beautiful inner state. Now THAT'S worth aiming for.**

The move is not *dirty fuel doesn't work*. It is *dirty fuel working is not a good enough standard.* → [[Clean Fuel and Dirty Fuel]]

## Why It Is Built This Way

**It's a standard, not a prediction.** Surf and turf doesn't claim the inner path produces bigger outcomes — it refuses to accept suffering as the price of entry, and then raises what counts as winning.

**Both halves get tested at once.** At the Sun Valley retreat the room contained a founder who had just shut down a company after raising $20M and one who had IPO'd — and neither outcome determined inner state. → [[Peace and success are completely independent of each other]]

**Receiving is part of "all."** The love seat exposed that the founders found being appreciated harder than achieving. → [[Letting love in is the part people find hardest]]

Dave's existence proof: *"one founder I coach is building what's likely to be a billion-dollar company. And he's doing it while playing golf, spending time with his family, and enjoying his life… deeply fulfilled, **not despite his success, but because of how he's building it.**"*

> **Success without suffering.**
