# The Quadrinity

> [!info] Not Dave's model — attributed
> Dave names the source without being asked: *"the Quadrinity Check-In, a framework I **learned from the Hoffman Institute**."* And the term itself: *"the Quadrinity is a term **coined by Bob Hoffman** that represents the four aspects of a human being."*
> He did the Hoffman Process himself — a 7-day residential retreat, 6–12 May 2023 — and notes that *"the Hoffman Process used to be called the Hoffman Quadrinity Process."*

## What It Is

Dave's counter to founders running on intellect alone.

> The Quadrinity recognizes that we are much more than just our intellectual selves.

> To help founders overcome this overreliance on intellect, I've been sharing a powerful tool with clients and our IGE community: the Quadrinity Check-In.

## The Model

Four aspects of a human being, each consulted separately:

- **Emotional**
- **Intellectual**
- **Physical**
- **Spiritual**

The check-in is the practice: rather than asking *what do I think*, you ask each of the four in turn, which surfaces the three that the intellect normally speaks over. → [[The Quadrinity]]

Its use in NL 22 is diagnostic — separating genuine threat-reading from mind-generated paranoia. The intellectual self is the one that builds the case; the other three report something different. → [[Paranoia is not the same as clear-eyed awareness]]

## Why It Is Built This Way

**Four registers, deliberately not one.** Same design logic as [[Thoughts, Feelings, Sensations]]: a fused self-report can't be worked with, and the intellect will happily answer on everyone's behalf. Separating the channels is what makes disagreement between them visible — and the disagreement is the information.

**It is a check-in, not an analysis.** Dave hands it out as something to run, not to understand: *"if you try the Quadrinity Check-In this week, I'd love to hear what you discover."*

## Also in [[MB 03 - My Experience of the Hoffman Process]]

Dave's Hoffman write-up confirms the lineage in his own words, and describes most people as out of balance: intellect, body and emotions in conflict and cut off from the spiritual self. He reports making a truce between his own intellect and his emotional child.

> The Quadrinity is a term coined by Bob Hoffman that represents the four aspects of a human being: the Emotional, Intellectual, Physical, and Spiritual.

## Also in [[NL 22 - When Paranoia Does More Harm Than Good]]

*The pre-promotion teaching note, folded into the model: the four selves and the order Dave runs them in.*

### What It Is

A check-in against the claim that founders run almost entirely on intellect.

> Most of us think of ourselves as our minds, treating our bodies like vehicles to transport our heads around. We operate primarily from intellect — analyzing, planning, worrying — while disconnecting from other parts of ourselves that offer equally valuable wisdom.

Four selves, each with its own wisdom and needs:

| | Self | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Intellectual** | the thinking mind — analyzer, planner, critic, storyteller |
| 2 | **Physical** | the body — sensation, energy, tension, intuition |
| 3 | **Emotional** | the feeling centre — joy, fear, anger, sadness, excitement |
| 4 | **Spiritual** | the highest, authentic self — clarity, wisdom, compassion |

### The Practice

Work through them in order, starting *below* the intellect:

- **Physical** — bring attention down from the head into the body. Notice posture, energy, tension. Ask: *what do you need right now?*
- **Emotional** — connect with what's present. Dave adds an unusual prompt: **how old does your emotional self feel in this moment?**
- **Spiritual** — the source of clarity and compassion
- **Intellectual** — heard, but not in charge

The ordering is the design. Starting with the body puts the loudest self last.

### Why Dave Uses It

It is his answer to **paranoia as a leadership style** → [[Paranoia is not the same as clear-eyed awareness]]. A founder hit by a bad press cycle spiralled into imagined collapse; the check-in interrupted the intellect's monopoly on the situation.

An extension of [[Thoughts, Feelings, Sensations]], adding a fourth layer.
