# The Seven Storyteller Archetypes

## What It Is

A cast of seven named characters covering the stories founders' minds most commonly generate. The naming is the point: giving a recurring pattern a character makes it far easier to disengage from than trying to evaluate each thought on its merits. It is step 2 of [[The Story Release Technique]].

## The Model

| Archetype | Tells stories of… | Which produces… |
|---|---|---|
| **Achiever Alex** | needing to achieve | stress, workaholism |
| **Bossy Brett** | what you *should* do | righteousness, inauthenticity, anxiety about getting it right |
| **Comparison Charlie** | comparison | envy, feeling less than, a frantic pace |
| **Controlling Cody** | needing to control | constant planning, rehearsing, exhaustion |
| **Critical Casey** | inadequacy | perfectionism or avoidance |
| **Doomsday Dylan** | imminent failure | overdrive, catastrophising, anxiety |
| **Victim Val** | victimhood | disempowerment, complaint, blame |

Dave is explicit that the list isn't closed — invent one if a pattern doesn't fit neatly.

## How Dave Uses It

By having clients identify their *primary* storyteller rather than all seven. His example: a client who recognised Comparison Charlie, and now identifies the speaker every time his mind starts measuring him against a founder who raised more or exited bigger — then lets go of the story. The recognition does the work; no argument with the content is required.

Note the overlap with [[The Founder Fear Map]] — Critical Casey and the fear of judgment, Doomsday Dylan and the fear of failure, Controlling Cody and the fear of letting go. The storyteller is the narration; the fear is what it is protecting.

## The BBG Expansion — seven became eleven

The Brand Kit table runs **eleven** archetypes, in a fuller schema than the original — **Archetype · Core Story/Belief · Fear/Root Emotion · Behavioral Pattern · Freedom Path:**

| Archetype | Core story | Root fear | Pattern | Freedom path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Critical Casey** | *"You're never enough."* | Unworthiness | harsh self-judgment, perfectionism | **Self-Compassion** |
| **Sergeant Sawyer** | *"If you don't push harder, you'll fall behind."* | Failure / loss of control | overdrive, burnout | **Trust in Pace** |
| **People Pleasing Parker** | *"If they're happy, I'm safe."* | Rejection | over-accommodation, self-abandonment | **Authenticity** |
| **Doomsday Dylan** | *"Something bad is coming."* | Uncertainty | catastrophizing, hypervigilance | **Presence / Grounding** |
| **Comparison Charlie** | *"They're ahead, I'm behind."* | Inadequacy | envy, overcompensation | **Gratitude / Celebration** |
| **Fault Finding Frankie** | *"Nothing's ever good enough."* | Disappointment / control | criticism, restlessness, dissatisfaction | **Curiosity / Appreciation** |
| **Regretful Riley** | *"The best (or worst) has already happened."* | Regret / nostalgia | rumination, attachment to past | **Forgiveness / Renewal** |
| **Victim Val** | *"Life's happening to me."* | Powerlessness | blame, helplessness, stagnation | **Empowerment** |
| **Controlling Cody** | *"If I manage everything, I'll be safe."* | Fear of chaos | micromanagement, rigidity | **Trust / Surrender** |
| **Fixer Finley** | *"If I solve it all, I'll be enough."* | Guilt / inadequacy | over-responsibility, exhaustion | **Acceptance** |
| **Futurist Flynn** | *"I'll be happy when…"* | Restlessness / discontent | future-chasing, never arriving | **Presence** |

**What the expansion tells you.** Several map onto material Dave already teaches under other names — Victim Val is [[Above and Below the Line|below the line]], Fixer Finley is [[Heroes require victims|heroing]], Futurist Flynn is [[You block yourself from feeling it now by believing you need the conditions to feel it|I'll be happy when]], Comparison Charlie is *comparison is the thief of joy.* The **Freedom Path** column is the genuinely new contribution: each archetype now points at its own antidote rather than only being named.

The alliterative naming convention matches Dave's own instruction elsewhere to give personas **silly, easy-to-remember names** → Personas.
