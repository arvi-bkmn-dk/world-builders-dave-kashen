# The Enneagram Box

## What It Is

Not Dave's framework — the **Enneagram**, which he uses as a map of the default patterns a founder starts inside. He calls it the **box**.

> A box of patterns that may have served you as a child but are now unconsciously running your life.

Why he prefers it to other typologies:

> Unlike other personality assessments that tell you **what** you do, the Enneagram reveals **why** you do it. Your Enneagram type isn't just a label — it's the lens through which you see the world, make decisions, and respond to stress by default.

→ [[Orgs and Collaborators Index]] · see [[Quote Attributions]]. Dave and Jesse also reference it in [[HoE 09 - Emotions and the Wisdom in Them]] and [[HoE 11 - Personas and the Fear Triangle]], where Jesse notes the Enneagram's own position that **you are all nine numbers** — the type is where weight accumulated, not the whole of what is there.

## The Nine

| | Type | The belief underneath |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | The Perfectionist | you must be good and right to be worthy |
| **2** | The Giver | you must give fully to others to be loved |
| **3** | The Achiever | worth comes from what you do, not who you are |
| **4** | The Individualist | seeks meaning and authenticity; feels like you don't quite fit |
| **5** | The Investigator | safety through intellectual mastery and self-sufficiency |
| **6** | The Loyalist | craves certainty; vigilant about threat and authority |
| **7** | The Enthusiast | avoids pain by staying busy with new possibilities |
| **8** | The Protector | must be strong to protect yourself and others |
| **9** | The Peacemaker | harmony at all costs, including your own needs |

## The Model

**The core mechanic:** [[Your greatest strength when overused becomes your greatest weakness]].

**The cost of not knowing your box** is that the pattern generates the very problem you're working to solve:

> The founder who micromanages isn't just struggling to delegate; they're unconsciously creating a team that can't function without them. The leader who avoids conflict isn't just preserving harmony; they're building a culture where important issues go unaddressed.

> These patterns become the **invisible ceiling on your leadership.**

**What knowing it buys** is choice rather than a label:

> Seeing these patterns allows you the choice to respond with intention rather than react from habit. Leading to a full range of perspectives, not just your default setting.

Which is the same destination as [[You could behave any way any other human behaves]] — full behavioural flexibility — reached through typology instead of through persona work. → [[Either you play your persona or it plays you]]
