# The Inner Inner Circle

## What It Is

Dave's model of where a founder is actually standing when they decide something — and it adds a third position most people don't know they have.

| | Contains |
|---|---|
| **Outer circle** | external conditions — what others say and do, the market, the weather |
| **Inner circle** | thoughts, feelings and sensations only you perceive |
| **Inner inner circle** | **awareness itself** — the part of you that watches your own mind at work |

> There's one sense that's even more powerful — awareness itself. It's not just noticing what your eyes see or what your ears hear.

It answers the question founders bring him most: *I trust my instincts, but sometimes I can't tell if it's real intuition or just fear disguised as wisdom.*

## The Model

**Why the third position matters.** From the inner inner circle, internal experience becomes as much a condition to respond to as external conditions are:

> It starts to matter less whether you're hearing a voice inside your mind or outside your ears; or whether you're noticing internal moods or the weather outside.

Thoughts and moods normally carry authority because they seem to come from *you*. Step out one layer and they become weather. → [[Awareness itself is the sixth sense]]

**The two failure modes it sits between.**

- **Pure intellect** — overanalysis, endless pro/con lists, trying to think your way to a certainty spreadsheets can't produce
- **Pure emotion** — acting on gut without awareness, "where that gut feeling could just be fear, insecurity, or past trauma talking"

> The key isn't to abandon logic or emotion — it's to observe them both without getting lost in either.

**The distorting filter.** Both get run through **identity preservation** — choosing based on who you think you need to be rather than what's right for the company. → [[Self-ing is the mental effort to create and protect an image of who you are]]

**The procedure.**

1. **Pause before reacting** — a "sacred pause" between stimulus and response
2. **Notice what's happening inside** — sensations, thoughts, emotions, without labelling anything right or wrong
3. **Separate signal from noise** — fear feels urgent and constricting; intuition feels clear, spacious and obvious → [[Fear feels urgent and constricting, intuition feels clear and spacious]]
4. **Trust what remains after the noise settles** — "the deeper knowing is always there. It was just buried under mental clutter"

## How Dave Uses It

The substitution at the decision point: instead of *what should I do?*, ask **what's actually happening inside me right now?**

His summary of what separates the best founders: "they don't just think. They don't just feel. **They observe.**"
