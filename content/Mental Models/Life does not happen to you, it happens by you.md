# Life does not happen to you, it happens by you

The shift Dave calls the foundation of unlocking your power, stated as a pair.

> **Life doesn't happen to you. It happens by you.** This shift — from "To Me" to "By Me" — is the foundation of waking up to and unlocking your power.

| | Victim consciousness | Creator consciousness |
|---|---|---|
| | Life is happening to you | You are the author of your experience |
| | You blame external circumstances | You own your actions, choices and mindset |
| | Frustrated, disempowered, reactive | Empowered, resourceful, intentional |

The case he opens with is a CEO with vision, talent, capital and a good team, whose every conversation was a list of reasons: the market's too tough, sales cycles too long, my team isn't stepping up, there's not enough time. One question moved it — *how are you creating this?* → How am I creating this is the question that converts acceptance into power

Two guards Dave puts around it.

**It is not a character judgment.** "Victim consciousness isn't a moral failing. It's a lack of awareness. Most people are in victim consciousness most of the time — and they are unaware of it. Like fish in water, they think *this is just how it is.*"

**And the first move is acceptance, not correction.** "First, you must accept yourself for being in victim consciousness. **You can't shift what you don't accept.**" → [[You cannot change what you do not accept]]

Why it matters beyond the individual: "as a founder, your consciousness sets the tone for your entire team. If you operate in victim consciousness, your team will mirror that energy." → [[Your state is contagious to your team]]

Dave credits the Conscious Leadership Group explicitly at the end of the piece.

## Also in [[GA 01 - The Inner Game of Life, Danny Miranda Podcast 394]]

Dave's extension is *for* me. His worked case is a group of friends in Sun Valley planning an outing he couldn't make, then declining to send calendar invites ("we're not your secretary, Dave"). He wrote out his own drama triangle on his phone, and saw he'd been sourcing belonging from them.

> I sometimes like to say it happens for me because I can start to see everything as here for my growth in my ability to be present.
