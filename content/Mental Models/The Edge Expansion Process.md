# The Edge Expansion Process

## What It Is

The model for how a founder's emotional comfort zone becomes their company's ceiling, and how to move that ceiling. An **edge** is the boundary between what feels safe and familiar and what feels threatening and unfamiliar — "fear lives at the edge between the known and the unknown."

> Your personal edges create your company's ceiling.

→ [[Your personal edges create your company's ceiling]]

## The Model

**Step 1 — Understand the five core fears.** Each produces a predictable leadership breakdown. → [[The Founder Fear Map]]

**Step 2 — Realise when you're at your edge.** The tell is physical, and it arrives before the thought does: constriction in throat, chest or stomach; racing heart; sweaty palms; holding your breath. Then the behavioural tells: distraction patterns (doomscrolling, email, drinking), prioritising easy work you already know how to do, irritation, urgency, defensiveness, mental spinning. → [[The body signals that you are at an edge before the mind does]]

The instruction at this point is explicitly not to judge it, only to register: *I'm at an edge.*

**Step 3 — Recognise the cost of letting fear stop you.** Each fear's cost is paid by the team, not by you — and facing that is what supplies the motivation to cross. "Your company's culture is a mirror for your personal edges."

**Step 4 — Expand beyond the edge, one rep at a time.** The work is not to fix the fear:

> The work is simply learning to feel the fear — without collapsing into it or pushing it away — and then act anyway.

→ [[Courage is being with fear and acting anyway, not the absence of fear]]

Over enough reps: authenticity becomes default, risks get bigger, honesty gets easier, delegation becomes natural, decisions get simpler.

## How Dave Uses It

The "one rep at a time" framing makes this trainable rather than aspirational, and it shares its physiology with [[The Law of Expanding Possibilities]] — edges are where capacity actually gets built. He quotes a coach of his own: our most fulfilling lives and relationships are one five-minute sweaty-palmed conversation away.
