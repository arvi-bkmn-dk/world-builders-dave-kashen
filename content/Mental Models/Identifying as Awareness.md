## Why It Is Built This Way

**The obstacle is identification, not effort.** *"The reason most people don't want to let go of it is because **they think it's them.** When you think you're your thoughts, of course you don't want to transcend your thoughts — you'd be transcending yourself."*

**The longing is made of what it seeks.** From Spira:

The **squished ball**: awareness compressed into the mind, naturally wanting to return to its full shape. *"What we think of as the ego trying to feel peaceful is actually **awareness trying to return to itself.**"*

**And why the character can't get there.** Two images, both Spira's. The **dream**: a character desperate to wake up can do nothing *as the character* — only wondering whether they are the whole truth of what's here does anything. And **King Lear**: *"there's nothing King Lear can do **as King Lear** to realise he's actually an actor playing a role — except start to do that deeper inquiry: **who am I really?**"*

→ [[The limited perspective cannot have the expanded perspective]]
