# Clean Fuel and Dirty Fuel

## What It Is

Dave's core distinction for *what a founder is running on*. Both fuels generate tension, and tension genuinely does drive action — so dirty fuel is not a delusion, it works. The difference is what it costs, what state it leaves you in, and what kinds of ideas become available from that state. It is the frame underneath most of his coaching: before changing what a founder does, change what they are drawing energy from.

## The Model

**Dirty fuel** — motivation manufactured out of lack and fear.
1. **Lack** — not good enough, behind, things aren't the way they should be.
2. **Fear** — the projected catastrophe. "I'm going to end up scared, hungry and alone if my startup fails."
3. **Self-attack** — the whipping room. Beating yourself up as a performance strategy. → [[The startup is a growth dojo but most founders run it as a whipping room]]
4. **Team version** — "if we don't get this right, this bad thing happens, we won't raise the next round."

**Clean fuel** — motivation generated out of vision, love and creative energy.
1. **Acceptance of current reality** — without making it wrong. → [[Creative tension requires accepting current reality, not making it wrong]]
2. **A desired future state** held at the same time. → [[Desired Future State (DFS)]]
3. **Creative tension** — the charge between the two, which is generative rather than punishing.
4. **Team version** — "if we do get this right, here's what becomes possible and who we get to serve." → [[Motivating a team from vision unlocks ideas hustle cannot reach]]

## How Dave Uses It

He uses it to surface the belief that keeps founders on dirty fuel: that their drive is *made of* the fear, and would disappear without it. His move is rarely to argue — it's to test it, as with Jesse's week of deliberately doing nothing. → [[Founders choose fear because they believe their drive would disappear without it]] · [[Letting go of self-criticism does not make creative drive disappear]]

He also uses it to explain why clean-fuel teams find non-obvious ideas: the expansive, present, creative state reaches solutions "not in the local maximum that we can hustle our way to."
