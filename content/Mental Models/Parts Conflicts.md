# Parts Conflicts

## What It Is

Dave's explanation for wanting two incompatible things at once, and the negotiation that resolves it.

The premise underneath it is [[Every part of you has an intended positive outcome]]:

## The Model

**Externalise the parts.** Dave has clients stretch their arms out, palms up, and imagine one part in each hand — then facilitates a conversation between them. *"Sometimes we even name the parts to make the experience more playful."*

His worked example is meditation, with **Ommm Oscar** (pro) and **Achiever Aaron** (anti).

**Ask each: what do you really want?**

And the diagnosis, which is the model's real insight:

**Then build trust**, using the same sequence as any below-the-line stuck point — **Awareness → Acceptance → Willingness to Shift:**

- *What are you most scared of?* · *What don't you trust about the other part's strategy?*
- *Would you be willing to feel the fear and accept yourself just as you are — scared and below the line?*
- *Are you willing to shift and find a win-win solution?*

**Then run an experiment** both parts will accept. His: *"what if we start with a one-week experiment of meditating for five minutes and exercising for forty minutes instead of forty-five?"* — so Oscar gets to meditate, Aaron sees it isn't as painful as feared, still gets a decent workout, and loses no work time.

## Why It Is Built This Way

**Both parts must be honoured or the negotiation fails:**

Which is the same rule as [[Core Beliefs|revising a belief]] — you cannot remove something by making it wrong, because *what you resist persists.*

**And it reframes "self-sabotage" out of existence.** → [[Every part of you has an intended positive outcome]] · [[Both parts want the same outcome and have different strategies]]
