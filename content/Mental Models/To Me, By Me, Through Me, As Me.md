# To Me, By Me, Through Me, As Me

> [!info] Not Dave's model — attributed
> *"There's a framework **originally from Michael Beckwith** that explains this progression."*

## What It Is

The developmental arc Dave watches founders move along, and his answer to why coaching successful founders keeps ending up in spiritual territory. Four stances toward your own life.

## The Model

**To me — victim consciousness.**
> Life happens **to me.** The board is frustrating. The market shifted. My co-founder isn't pulling their weight. **You're at the effect of circumstances, constantly reacting.**

This is [[Above and Below the Line|below the line]] in developmental terms.

**By me — creator consciousness.**
> You see yourself as the **source of your experience** — not as blame, but as empowerment. **If I created this, I can create something different.**

> This is where **most typical coaching lives**, and it's transformational.

→ [[One Hundred Percent Responsibility]] · [[Responsibility is not self-blame]]

**Through me.**
> The question shifts. Instead of *what do I want to create?* it becomes **what's wanting to happen through me?** You start relating to yourself as **a vessel for something beyond your individual ego** — like you're part of nature, and nature is trying to evolve through you.

→ [[There is a scary neutral period between dirty fuel and authentic desire]] · [[Authentic wants arise from wholeness, compensatory behaviors arise from lack]]

**As me.**
> The recognition that **we are awareness itself**, oneness expressing through this particular human form. **Not a concept to believe, but something that can be directly experienced.**

→ [[Having desire is different from being owned by desire]] · [[You are already whole, perfect and complete]]

## Why It Is Built This Way

**Each stage is a full stop that eventually stops working.** Dave is explicit that *by me* is where most coaching lives and that it genuinely transforms people — the model doesn't diminish it, it just doesn't end there.

**The transition is usually forced, not chosen.** *"Sometimes through crisis. Sometimes through curiosity. And sometimes, like Chesky, because they've achieved the success they were chasing and hit the ceiling."* The founders who get there fastest are often the ones whose external strategy worked completely. → [[Ask any founder what they want then ask why five times and it traces back to a feeling]]

**The questions change, not just the answers.** *"Not how do I create more? but where do my desires even come from? Why do I want what I want? And ultimately: who am I, really?"*
