> The state you're operating from is ultimately what determines your results.

## What He Means

The thesis sentence of the entire body of work, and the reason it's called the *inner* game. Not that mindset helps, or that stress is unpleasant — that state is the causal variable, and tactics are downstream of it.

Everything else he teaches is a special case: fear-motivated teams only reach the local maximum, attached founders show up less creatively, unarguable language delivered from threat still lands as an attack. If you only keep one line from Dave, this is the one the rest hangs off. → [[The state you operate from is what determines your results]]
