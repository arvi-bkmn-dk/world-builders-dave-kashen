# The mind thinks and the heart knows

## What He Means

The line Dave reaches for when founders try to figure everything out with the mind. The intellect is one source of wisdom; the heart and body are others, and they tend to *know* rather than reason their way there. He uses it right after a member notices how different it feels to be in her body after breathwork instead of in her head.
