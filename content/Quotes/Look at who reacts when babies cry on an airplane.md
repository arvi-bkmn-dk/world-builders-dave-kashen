> If you wanna know who is not allowed to cry, look at who reacts when babies cry on an airplane.

## What He Means

A diagnostic for something invisible from the inside. Resistance becomes automatic once practised — "our defenses are so well formed that we don't even recognise it's happening" — so you cannot find it by introspection.

But it shows up in irritation. "If I don't let myself feel that feeling, I get annoyed when other people feel the feeling." Which converts an unanswerable question into an easy one: **what feelings annoy you when other people have them?**

Dave adds judgment as the second flavour, working in both directions — judging someone else's anger, or your own. → [[What annoys you when others feel it is what you will not feel yourself]]
