> Unicorn founders aren't the ones who feel less. They're the ones who can feel more without shutting down and are able to lead from that expanded state.

## What He Means

The correction to the composure myth. The founders who hold up under pressure are not the ones who have successfully stopped feeling — that reading makes numbness the goal and suppression the method, which is exactly what caps capacity. They feel *more*, and stay functional while doing it.

It is the closing line of the five frameworks, and it reframes the whole document: none of this is about managing feelings down. → [[Your results are limited by which feelings you are unwilling to feel]]
