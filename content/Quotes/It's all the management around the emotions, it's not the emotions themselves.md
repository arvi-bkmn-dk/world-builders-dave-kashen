> The thing that creates the drama in the workplace and all the messiness that people are trying to avoid is the resistance to all of it. It's all the management around the emotions — it's not the emotions themselves.

## What He Means

Jesse's line, and the sharpest statement of the episode's central inversion applied to a company. Leaders avoid emotions at work because they fear chaos. The chaos is produced by the avoiding.

*Don't cry. This person cried and that's not appropriate. Let's handle this offline.* Each move is an attempt to contain, and each one adds to the thing being contained — the same loop as an individual resisting a feeling, running at organisational scale.

His alternative is to make space deliberately: "we're gonna go off on this client in this room for 20 minutes and be mad about this, and then we're gonna move through."
