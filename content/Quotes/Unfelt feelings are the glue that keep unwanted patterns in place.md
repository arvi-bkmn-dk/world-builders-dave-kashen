> Unfelt feelings are the glue that keep unwanted patterns in place.

## What He Means

The explanation for a failure everyone has had: seeing clearly that a belief is false and being unable to drop it. *I'm conflict avoidant. I'm a nice guy. I'm not good enough.*

Insight doesn't dissolve it, and neither does the next layer of insight. A founder can correctly see that the belief has been driving them and that they're scared to release it — "that's a useful insight" — and still be stuck. "But until they actually feel the fear, it doesn't go."

Dave reports revising his own method over this. He used to work the belief directly; he now looks for the feeling holding it in position. → [[Unfelt feelings are the glue that keeps unwanted patterns in place]]

Jesse's worked case is *I'm not worthwhile if I'm not busy* — a belief that survived the disappearance of every practical reason for it.
