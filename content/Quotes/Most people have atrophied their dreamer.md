> Most people have atrophied their dreamer.

## What He Means

Why "what do you want?" so often returns nothing. The faculty isn't missing — it has wasted from disuse. Children dream constantly; by the twenties and certainly the thirties, most people have stopped, because enough adults said *you can't have that.*

Atrophy is the precise word and a hopeful one: a muscle that wasted can be trained. Dave's move with clients is to lift the constraint before asking — "if you let go of all limiting beliefs, what do you really want to do?" — and what he finds is that the answer was there the whole time, pre-filtered as impossible. → [[Most adults have atrophied their dreamer]]
