> Stress and overwhelm are white collar words for scared. You're just scared.

## What He Means

A translation, and the two vocabularies point somewhere different. *Stressed* describes a workload and implies a workload fix — more hours, better systems, fewer commitments. *Scared* describes you, and points at something that can be felt through.

Dave reports that clients' systems visibly calm on the accurate word alone, before anything is solved. → [[Stress and overwhelm are white-collar words for scared]]
