> Emotion as energy in motion.

## What He Means

The definition Dave opens the emotions material with, and the one he recaps at the top of the next episode — which makes it the load-bearing frame for both.

Two things follow from it. Emotion is **physical**, so tuning into it means tuning into the body rather than thinking about how you feel. And it is **in motion** — it moves through and completes, unless something stops it. Jesse's 18-month-old cries for 25 seconds and is then fine; the adult version takes years because the movement gets blocked.

Dave credits Diana Chapman at Conscious Leadership, and notes she probably got it from someone else.
