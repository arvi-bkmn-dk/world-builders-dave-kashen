> Most trade-offs are false.

## What He Means

Dave's answer to the founder who cannot name what they want because they are busy arbitrating between two things they want. When someone struggles to state a desired future state, his read is that they are silently negotiating a trade-off they invented: *thirty hours a week* versus *a billion-dollar company.*

His move is to refuse the choice and read both back as one answer — "so what I hear you saying is you want to work 30 hours a week and build a billion dollar company." The struggle usually dissolves, because the either/or was the problem rather than the ambition.

He roots the pattern in threat: "when we're in a state of threat, we tend to think either/or." His own name for the alternative is **surf and turf**, and the question he asks is "how could you have it all?" → [[You can have it all, but it requires more creativity]]
