> Most people's lives are literally limited by which physical sensations they're willing to experience in their body.

## What He Means

The Law of Expanding Possibilities stated one level more precisely — at the level of sensation rather than named emotion. Since we decide emotionally and rationalise afterwards, decisions are being made to avoid specific bodily sensations, upstream of anything that reaches awareness.

Which is why the avoidance is invisible: nobody experiences themselves declining a sensation. They experience themselves having a preference or a reason. → [[Your life is limited by which sensations you are willing to feel]]
