> What you appreciate appreciates.

## What He Means

Appreciation's business case in four words. Name what someone did well and specifically, and they do more of it — Jesse's supporting study is the basketball coach whose team improved markedly only when he showed them film of what they did *right*.

Dave's reason it's a leverage point rather than a nicety: the negativity bias is already running in every head in the building, so constructive feedback adds to a saturated signal while appreciation supplies the missing one. He's explicit that this doesn't trade against candour. → [[What you appreciate appreciates]]
