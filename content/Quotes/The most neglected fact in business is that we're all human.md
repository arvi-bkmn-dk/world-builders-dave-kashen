> the most neglected fact in business is that we're all human

## Why It Qualifies

**Recurrence.** Dave quotes it in two separate sources in the same words, crediting [[Chip Conley]] in GA 03.

## What He Means

Business culture treats leaders and teams as if they could stop being human at work. Dave's whole approach starts from the opposite: the same fears, parts and patterns show up in the boardroom, and pretending otherwise is what makes them run the show. → [[All humans have all the same parts]]
