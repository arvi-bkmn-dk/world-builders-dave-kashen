> We don't tend to see the world as it is. We see the world as we are.

## What He Means

How a persona makes itself true. Someone identified as the one who handles crises filters for crises, attracts people who read situations as emergencies, and ends up in a world that confirms the identity. "To a firefighter, everything's a fire."

The clinical detail is that it arrives as a complaint: *Dave, I'm constantly having to fight fires* — said by someone who is, in Dave's reading, generating them. → [[We do not see the world as it is, we see the world as we are]]

Which is why handling the fires never resolves anything. Handling them is what the persona is for.
