> You really only have to know the next step. The path is made by walking.

## What He Means

Dave's answer to the leader who won't declare a future because they can't yet see how to reach it. The objection assumes the path precedes the walking; his claim is the reverse — the declaration generates the creative tension, and the tension is what produces the route.

His example is JFK committing to the moon in 1961, when the technology needed to invent the technology did not exist. "The creative tension is a big part of the how." → [[Declaring a future you do not know how to reach is part of how you reach it]]
