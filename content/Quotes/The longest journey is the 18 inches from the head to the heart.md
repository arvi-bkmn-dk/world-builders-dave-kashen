> There's some poem or quote — the longest journey is the 18 inches from the head to the heart.

## What He Means

The line Dave and Jesse close the emotions episode on, and the shortest statement of what the whole episode asks for: move attention below the neck.

Dave's instruction is literal — "for those listening, probably this whole time you've been listening from your head. Take a moment and bring your attention down to that thing below your neck." Jesse's version is an elevator from head to heart, which Dave taught him and he still uses.

The distance is short and the journey is long because the head is where most people live, and because what's waiting below is the feelings the head exists to avoid.
