> Resistance to anger is blocking your flow. Anger is the flow.

## What He Means

Dave interrupts Jesse mid-sentence to make this correction, and the interruption is the point: describing anger as the blockage prescribes resisting it, which installs the actual blockage. The framing generates the problem.

It is the single-emotion case of his general claim that no emotion is painful — the resistance is. → [[Resistance to the emotion is what blocks flow, not the emotion]]
