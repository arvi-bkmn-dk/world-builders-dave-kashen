> I've been fond recently of saying, an emotion is worth a thousand words.

## What He Means

Dave's own coinage, and his compression of why emotional work is faster than analysis rather than softer than it.

The setting is a team or a person spinning: "our minds tend to overcomplicate almost everything. Simplicity on the other side of complexity is almost always — there's just some feeling that's not being felt. And once it's felt, the issue is easily resolved."

So the claim is about efficiency. A thousand words of discussion can circle a thing that one felt emotion resolves, because the words were never addressing what was actually in the way.
