> Shame is like fundamental, I'm bad. Guilt is, I did bad.

## What He Means

The cleanest available distinction between two feelings people routinely collapse. Same emotional ingredients — fear and sadness plus a story — differing only in where the story attaches.

Guilt indicts an **action**, which leaves the person intact and something to repair. Shame indicts the **person**, which leaves nothing to act on. That is why guilt can motivate and shame tends to immobilise.

It earns its place when Dave walks founders down to what they are actually afraid of: not the startup failing, but the shame underneath it. → [[Shame says I am bad and guilt says I did bad]]
