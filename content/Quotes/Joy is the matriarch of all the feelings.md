> Joy is the matriarch of all the feelings — and if you're not letting her children in, she ain't coming.

## What He Means

The argument against selective feeling, in one image. All emotions run through the same pipe, so the attempt to block anger, sadness and fear while keeping joy is what makes joy unavailable — the mechanism doesn't discriminate.

Dave's fuller definition is that joy is "what's left over when you just allow everything else to move through": a residue rather than a target, which is why pursuing it directly tends to fail. → [[All emotions run through the same pipe and joy is the matriarch]]

## Renderings

Dave says this more than one way. Same essence, different wording:
