> Your task is not to seek for love, but to identify all the barriers you've built against it and embrace them. — Rumi, quoted by Dave

## What He Means

Dave's favourite statement of the via negativa, and the shape of his whole method. Nothing is being accumulated; what's sought is already present, and the work is removing what blocks it.

*Embrace* is the operative word — consistent with welcoming the resistance rather than defeating it. Read this way his entire toolkit is subtractive: the facts-and-stories exercise removes a belief, the entitlement practice removes a baseline, meditation adds nothing at all. → [[Via negativa says there is nothing to attain, only barriers to remove]]
