> The last line of defence is our jaw. Don't say that. Don't speak it. Don't cry out.

## What He Means

Where suppression ends up in the body once everything else has failed to stop the feeling. Dave offers it as an explanation for jaw pain and teeth grinding — the clench is the final block on sound, and sound is one of the two routes an emotion wants out through.

It sits inside his rough body map — neck and shoulders for anger, stomach for fear, chest and behind the eyes for sadness — and is the most specific claim in it. → [[People can access the physical sensation more easily than the feeling]]

The corollary is in the remedy: if the jaw is the last defence, then sound is what gets past it, which is why his practice for anger involves screaming as much as movement. → [[Healthy expression is the third option between reacting and holding it in]]
