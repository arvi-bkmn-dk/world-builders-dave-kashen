> You have a bottle of water with sand in it and it's murky. And going into your head to try to get clear is like shaking the bottle of water — which just gets more and more murky.

## What He Means

Why founders cannot think their way to clarity. Clarity is what **settling** produces; rumination is agitation. So working the problem harder cognitively makes the answer less available, not more.

Dave's claim is that clarity, not capability, is usually the real bottleneck: "most founders are pretty badass — if they're clear, they can run after something." And the route to it is the feeling rather than the analysis. → [[More thinking makes clarity worse, like shaking sand in a bottle]]
