> Either you play your persona or it plays you.

## What He Means

The line the persona episode keeps returning to, and the reason it isn't about eliminating personas. They're useful — patterns learned in the first eight or ten years as ways of getting needs met, and a founder genuinely needs the firefighter sometimes.

The variable is consciousness. Played deliberately, a persona is a tool you pick up for a situation. Unexamined, it picks the situations — filtering what you notice, and getting mistaken for identity. "For a lot of people, they think of that as who they are." → [[Either you play your persona or it plays you]]
