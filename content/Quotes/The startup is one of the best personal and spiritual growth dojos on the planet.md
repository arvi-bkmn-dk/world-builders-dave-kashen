> My view is that the startup is one of the best personal and spiritual growth dojos on the planet. And that's not the experience or the way most entrepreneurs are experiencing it.

## What He Means

Dave's framing for why he works with founders rather than in a therapy room: the startup already supplies everything a growth practice needs — sustained pressure, real stakes, constant feedback, and no way to avoid your own patterns. It is a dojo whether or not you treat it as one.

The second sentence is the part that does the work. He is not claiming founders are having a spiritual experience; he is claiming the conditions are there and almost nobody is using them. That gap is the whole offer. → [[The startup is a growth dojo but most founders run it as a whipping room]]
