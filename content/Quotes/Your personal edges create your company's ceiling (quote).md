> Your personal edges create your company's ceiling.

## What He Means

The single sentence that justifies doing emotional work on a founder when the presenting problem is operational. Wherever a founder stops — at the conflict they won't have, the decision they won't make, the control they won't release — the company stops too, and the limit is invisible because it presents as judgment rather than avoidance.

Its companion line, said the same way: *your company's culture is a mirror for your personal edges.* → [[Your personal edges create your company's ceiling]]
