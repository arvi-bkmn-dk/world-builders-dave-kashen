> Heroes require victims.

## What He Means

Three words that answer the founder who asks what's wrong with saving the day. The hero role is not neutral toward the people in it: taking it assigns everyone else the victim role, and the assignment travels. "Even just seeing them that way will affect the way they see themselves."

Then it closes on the hero. People treated as victims feel diminished rather than grateful, so the appreciation never comes, and "the heroes keep heroing and then they expect to be celebrated — and when they're not, they get really resentful." → [[Heroes require victims]]
