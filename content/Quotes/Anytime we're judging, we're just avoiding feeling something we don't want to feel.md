> Anytime we're judging, we're just avoiding feeling something we don't want to feel.

## What He Means

Dave's reclassification of judgment — not a moral position but an avoidance manoeuvre, and one that works, which is why it's so available.

It arrives right after Jesse's account of asking his anxious part what it needed to feel safe, and being told: *for the judging part to stop judging me.* Two parts of one person, one policing the other. Jesse then recognises the same move pointed outward, at his wife's anxiety on planes — "what are you worrying about? Here's the statistics."

Both directions are the same mechanism. Judging the feeling — in yourself or in someone else — is what you do instead of feeling it. → [[Every part of you has an intended positive outcome]]
