> The issue is actually not fear. The issue is resistance to fear.

## What He Means

Dave issuing a correction to his own teaching, on air. Having called fear dirty fuel for years, he had apologised to his community the day before, because the phrasing implied fear was bad and should be avoided.

The revision is precise. What drives the rumination, the compulsive figuring-out, the toxic push to achieve — none of it is fear. It is **not wanting to feel the fear**. Which inverts the remedy: "paradoxically, if you fully allow yourself to feel the fear, it actually tends to quiet the mind."

Jesse's compression of the pattern: "you want to be successful so that you won't have to feel the fear of not being successful." → [[No emotion is painful, the resistance to it is]]
