> What you resist persists. You can't change what you don't accept.

## What He Means

Dave's compressed argument for why acceptance is a *prerequisite* for change rather than an alternative to it. Arguing with a fact that already obtains — "this shouldn't be happening," "this isn't fair" — consumes the attention that would otherwise go to responding, and keeps the nervous system in threat, where solutions aren't visible.

The line does double duty across his work: it applies to circumstances on [[The Responsibility Roadmap]] and to feelings in [[The Law of Expanding Possibilities]], where resistance is precisely what extends an emotion past its natural 60–90 seconds. → [[You cannot change what you do not accept]]
