> If you could behave any way any other human behaves, you could achieve anything any other human achieves.

## What He Means

The prize on the other side of persona work, and Dave's argument that it isn't self-understanding for its own sake.

A persona is a standing restriction on the range of responses available to you, which means it caps what you can build — every situation calling for an out-of-range response gets the in-range one instead. Remove the restriction and you get what he calls **total behavioural flexibility**: the ability to "show up the best way to meet the moment."

The only thing in the way is the sentence Jesse supplies before Dave can — *that's not who I am.* Dave: "That's the comfort thing." → [[You could behave any way any other human behaves]]
