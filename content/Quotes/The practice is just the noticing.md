> It's not that you're supposed to stay focused on your breath for 10 straight minutes. The practice is just the noticing.

## What He Means

The correction that keeps people meditating. Most quit because they judge themselves against sustained concentration and conclude they're bad at it — but the drifting is what supplies the reps. Nine and a half minutes lost in thought with one noticed return is a real session.

> If there was a muscle to build, it's the muscle of exerting conscious choice around where you put your attention.
