# The Inner Game of Life — Danny Miranda Podcast 394

## Summary

Dave's long-form guest appearance on [[Danny Miranda]]'s podcast, which Dave himself later calls "very vulnerable, authentic." Danny opens on a tweet of Dave's: *is it possible you actually have no problems other than the ones your mind invents?* Dave builds the answer from first principles: ego minds as problem-solving machines, a brain wired for threat level 10 in a threat-level-1 world, the power of distinctions (the mechanic's view of an engine), then **context versus content** and **above and below the line**, credited to Diana Chapman and Jim Dethmer at the Conscious Leadership Group. From there he explains **creative tension** (credited to Peter Senge), including the paradox of accepting yourself while imagining who you want to become.

Danny pushes on the hard cases. Did someone in the Holocaust create their experience? Dave separates being a victim from victim consciousness. He talks through chronic pain and cancer, and tells a psilocybin journey on which he believed he would die, and came back by noticing that in the now moment he wasn't dead. He runs **the four steps** (awareness, acceptance, willingness, shift) and describes making *where am I?* his North Star, with the Aikido master who "just recovers so quickly."

Then the personal arc: 25 years of trying to prove his worth, the Landmark Forum, leaving money on the table to coach, periodic depression, psychedelic experiences of being love, the Hoffman Process, Brian Franklin's idea that the ego is a feature, and losing about 70% of his net worth in 2021–22 ("what do you love about yourself that has nothing to do with your money or success?"). He closes with the **who am I** challenge and **the two games**.
