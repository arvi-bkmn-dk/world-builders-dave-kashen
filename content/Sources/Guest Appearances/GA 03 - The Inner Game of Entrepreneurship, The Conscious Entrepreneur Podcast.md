# The Inner Game of Entrepreneurship — The Conscious Entrepreneur Podcast

## Summary

Dave on [[Alex Raymond]]'s *The Conscious Entrepreneur*. The interview is framed as a conversation between old friends, and the file is titled *The Entrepreneur's Inner Journey*. Dave opens with the two games of life: making life match your mind's idea of it, or being with life as it is. The second is the one you can win, and you win twice. He retells his arc: 25 years proving his worth, Wharton, Goldman and a top hedge fund, the empty top of the mountain, four hard years building Wellsphere, a first coach, the seminar where he dropped the unworthiness story, and coach training.

Asked where a founder should start, he gives the talker and the listener, the fisherman and the bait, and living meditation over morning meditation. He covers catching the mind imagining the future you don't want, and the newborn-worthiness thought experiment. Then a full conscious-leadership primer, with deep credit to Diana Chapman and Jim Dethmer: content vs context, above and below the line, the where-am-I reminder practice, responsibility not self-blame, the Recipe, and emotions (90 seconds, Joe Hudson on resistance, fear and excitement).

On loneliness at the top, he separates conviction from certainty, credits Lencioni on the CEO's invulnerability, quotes Chip Conley, and asks whether you're sharing a feeling for you or for your team. It closes on his course and the first Sun Valley Founders Retreat (January 2025), which is promotion and isn't extracted.
