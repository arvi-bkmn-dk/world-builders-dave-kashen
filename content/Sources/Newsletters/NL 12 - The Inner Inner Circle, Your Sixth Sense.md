# The Inner Inner Circle, Your Sixth Sense

## Summary

Answers a question Dave says founders bring him constantly: *I trust my instincts, but sometimes I can't tell if it's real intuition or just fear disguised as wisdom.*

His model adds a layer to the usual picture. Most people experience an inner circle of their own thoughts and feelings and an outer circle of external conditions. Beyond both is an **inner inner circle** — awareness itself, "the part of you that watches your own mind at work." Operating from there, an internal mood becomes as much a condition to respond to as the weather, which is what creates room to choose.

He names the two ways founders decide badly: **pure intellect**, overanalysing toward a certainty that spreadsheets can't deliver; and **pure emotion**, acting on a gut that may just be fear or old trauma. Both are then filtered through **identity preservation** — the self-ing process — so the choice gets made by who you think you need to be rather than what's right for the company.

The practical core is a four-step sequence (pause, notice, separate signal from noise, trust what remains) and a clean somatic test: fear feels urgent and constricting; intuition feels clear, spacious and obvious, "almost like it was there all along."

The substitution at the decision point: instead of *what should I do?*, ask *what's actually happening inside me right now?*
