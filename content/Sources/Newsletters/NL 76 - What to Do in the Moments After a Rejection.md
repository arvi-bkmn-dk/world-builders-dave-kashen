# What to Do in the Moments After a Rejection

## Summary

Dave's sixteen-year-old announced he didn't want college: he wanted to be a **race car driver**. Dave felt fear and the urge to control his future, and also: *this is showing me an edge.* He treats life as a **presence dojo**, and an investor's no is just another sparring partner.

**Why a no hits so many layers.** Disappointment, then identity (*maybe I'm not that great*), then the old childhood wiring that hears *I don't want to be your friend.*

**What to do in the moment.** Slow down and notice you've been affected. Feel it in the body: a feeling is worth a thousand thoughts, and felt all the way through it doesn't take long. Accept yourself for being below the line. Look at the stories (Seligman: temporary and specific, not permanent and pervasive). Take 100% responsibility for creating the experience, not self-blame in responsibility's clothing. Only then get curious about what to change. Short on time: four by four breathing.

**High expectations.** Don't lower them. Hold the dream without attachment: every right to the dream, no right to the outcome. Dave supported his son, who won four of his first five races.
