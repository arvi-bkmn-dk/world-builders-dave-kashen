# Projection: How Your World Is a Mirror

## Summary

At the last Sun Valley Founders Retreat, the group sent each founder to dinner as **their opposite**. The warmest went as cruel, the humblest as arrogant, and Dave as *the guy with no interior life whatsoever*. Dave's explanation of why busy founders would spend an evening on this:

**Double-sided judgments.** One filter, pointed both ways: what you judge in others you judge in yourself, and what you admire points to what you haven't claimed. Diana Chapman: *if you spot it, you got it.*

**The third rail.** Accepting a disowned quality feels dangerous, but what you resist persists. A quality core to your identity becomes a line your system won't go near, and its energy turns inward. The woman who was "not cruel" couldn't be direct, and attacked herself instead. **The disowned part relocates.**

**The identity you built to survive.** Everything after "I am" is a construct for approval, security and control. The negativity bias overweights criticism of the self. All humans have all the parts, and loving all of them gives you choice.

**Beyond the limits of identity.** Inhabit the denied parts and let yourself be loved for them: perception clears, and you gain behavioural flexibility.
