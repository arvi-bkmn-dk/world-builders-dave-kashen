# How You and Your Team Can Avoid Burnout

## Summary

Opens with a founder calling himself "chief reminder officer" — exhausted at **50 hours a week**, fewer than most, asking *"other founders work way more hours than me and seem fine. What's wrong with me?"*

Dave's answer: **burnout is caused as much by resistance as by working a lot.** His own case is tax season — simple work he'd approach as an obligation, dragging his feet and getting grumpy with his family, until a coach pointed out that taxes are a choice with consequences. Once he chose it, "doing my taxes became no big deal."

The generalisation: anything you do while simultaneously resisting drains both you and your team.

The second half introduces **four zones**, from *The Power of Full Engagement*: **Performance** (high energy, positive, in flow, team feeds off it), **Survival** (high energy going negative, pushing and forcing, team feels the strain), **Burnout** (the body forces shutdown), and recovery. Dave's point is that founders try to ignore their body's rhythms until the body decides for them.
