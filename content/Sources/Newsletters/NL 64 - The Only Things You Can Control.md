# The Only Things You Can Control

## Summary

**You are stuck to the earth, and it doesn't bother you**, because you aren't trying to control gravity. Dave's claim: the feeling of stuckness in a business or a relationship isn't caused by being stuck. It's caused by trying to control what you can't.

**Where the need to control comes from.** Security, approval and control, sourced externally. Each becomes a control strategy: running a chess game before hard conversations (approval), over-functioning to soothe fear about outcomes (security), forcing people and circumstances (control).

**Sorting the Files**, from the Conscious Leadership Group. Two folders, and the standard for *can control* is that you can guarantee the outcome. Other people's thoughts, feelings and choices, the macro environment, the outcome of your startup, even your next thought: can't. Your responses, your attention, what you say and do, your boundaries, agreements and state: can. The key is **100% responsibility, not more, not less.**

**Caring without controlling.** Dave doesn't believe he makes his wife upset, and still chooses not to snap at her.

**Less control, more power.** We don't fight gravity, and we still build planes and launch rockets. You still play to win, but from presence rather than threat.
