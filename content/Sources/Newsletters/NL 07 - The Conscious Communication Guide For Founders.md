# The Conscious Communication Guide For Founders

## Summary

The most practical newsletter in the set — a communication toolbox Dave tells readers to bookmark, credited almost entirely to the Conscious Leadership Group.

It opens on a CEO whose board member kept cancelling their 1:1s. She had built the story that he thought little of her. When she finally checked it, she got the opposite: *"You're my strongest CEO. I figured you didn't need me."* Weeks of tension resolved in minutes.

The frameworks: **fact vs story**, with the four buckets of speaking unarguably — fact, feeling, story, want. **Conscious listening** across head, heart and gut, reflected back before responding. **Candor**, and the Withhold-Withdraw-Project cycle that calcifies a relationship when truth goes unsaid. **Assumptions to agreements**, with who/what/when and an explicit yes, plus a 90/9/1 culture of integrity. And **above-the-line accountability**, which uses learning rather than blame.

The through-line is that most team drama is not interpersonal chemistry but missing structure — unlabelled stories, unspoken truths, and expectations that were never agreements.

## Notable Quotes

> We often confuse facts — what a video camera would record — with stories.

> By labeling your interpretation as a story, you avoid arguing over 'who's right' and keep conversations grounded in reality.

> When you listen to fix, it keeps you from fully listening and leaves the other person not feeling fully heard.

> Not sharing is what kills relationships.

> Once we withhold our truth from someone, we tend to disconnect from them.

> Most drama in teams and relationships is caused by a lack of clear agreements.

> 90% of the time, we do what we say we will. 9% of the time, we will renegotiate as soon as we know we won't deliver. 1% of the time, we will acknowledge our broken agreements.
