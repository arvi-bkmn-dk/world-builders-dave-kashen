# The Recipe That's Keeping You Stuck

## Summary

A founder trying to buy out a cofounder who'd stopped contributing years ago but still wanted a hefty payout. *"I did all the work. He wants more than is fair. It's infuriating."*

Dave runs him through **The Recipe** — the Conscious Leadership Group exercise also called *teach the class*, built on one question: **if you had to teach a class on how to create the exact problem you're experiencing, what would the lessons be?**

The premise: most of us are experts in the thing we claim we don't want. "We say we want clarity, but we unknowingly teach a masterclass in confusion."

The founder's syllabus came out in one breath — start a business without clarifying expectations, avoid the equity conversation when the dynamic changes, resent him silently while doing all the work, make up stories about what's fair, let pressure build, then negotiate from superiority and blame. *"Boom. That's the class."*

> Once he saw it, something cracked open. He wasn't a victim of his cofounder. He was the author of his own experience.

Closes with the instructions to run it yourself, including the step that makes it land: **read your recipe out loud.**
