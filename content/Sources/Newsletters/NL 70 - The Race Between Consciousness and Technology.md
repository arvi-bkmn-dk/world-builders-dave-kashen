# The Race Between Consciousness and Technology

## Summary

A client measured a **30x** productivity gap between engineers fully using AI and those who aren't. Clients are laying off engineers while growing. For founders not building AI, the treadmill sped up, and underneath runs a current of fear and doom.

**The problem of speed.** The AI race is a Prisoner's Dilemma: nobody can trust the others to slow down, so everyone keeps pace. Tristan Harris compares it to nuclear nonproliferation, but AI lacks visible mutually assured destruction and hard-to-build materials.

**Leading your team.** Don't hero the engineer who won't adopt AI. As *mayor of the town*, hold both empathy and reality: if the company doesn't adapt, everyone loses their job. Resisting AI is resisting what is.

**What we've been training for.** Staying present amid fear is what keeps you creative. Activism from below the line stops at *this is bad*; from above the line you welcome the fear and build the most beautiful future you can imagine. From victim to creator: *what's mine to do here?* AI doesn't take the magic away, it makes you a more powerful magician.

**The question.** The Prisoner's Dilemma only traps us if we see ourselves as separate. What would 8 billion interconnected humans do about AI?
