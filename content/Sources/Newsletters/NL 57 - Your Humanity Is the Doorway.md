# Your Humanity Is the Doorway

## Summary

Dave's stated main lesson of the year, and a public correction of his own earlier aim. He had treated peak states — the psilocybin journey, the spontaneous awakening, oneness in meditation — as the target: *"if I could just get back there and stay there."*

**"The most true path is full integration, not transcendence."**

The frame arrives via *Siddhartha*, shared by a client and friend. The young ascetic believes *"if I abandon desire, I will find Truth,"* masters fasting and self-denial, and fails — because **rejecting humanity is just ego in disguise.** *"Spiritual bypassing doesn't dissolve ego. It refines it. This is why holiness can become pride. Why purity can become violence."* The key line: **you cannot transcend what you have not fully lived.**

Dave then catches himself doing the emotional version — *"I was welcoming feelings transactionally. I'd welcome sadness so it would take its turn and leave, making room for my 'real friends.' That was still resistance in disguise."* He discloses periodic mild depression, and the fear that letting sadness in would consume him.

And the biological version, where he concedes a long-held position after a mentor said *"once it becomes a biochemical thing, it really needs a biochemical treatment."* **"Biology precedes and greatly influences psychology… It's a 'yes and,' not an 'either/or.'"**

The resolution is the river, which *"does not reject calm or turbulence, drought or flood, purity or pollution. It includes all of it, and remains the river."* Oneness does not eliminate form; it expresses itself as form. What changes is not the content of a life but possession: **the difference between having desire and being owned by desire.**
