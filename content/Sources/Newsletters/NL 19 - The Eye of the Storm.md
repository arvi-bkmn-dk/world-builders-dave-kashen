# The Eye of the Storm

## Summary

Written into a period of compounding chaos — startup volatility, a new administration, markets wiping out trillions, AI accelerating past comprehension — and it opens by refusing the consolation that it will pass.

"I'm not willing to bet that this chaos will slow down and stabilize in our lifetime." Alongside business turbulence there are aging parents, health crises, relationship issues, "and the growing realization that there's no finish line where challenges simply stop happening." Waiting for conditions to stabilise before finding peace is a bet Dave wouldn't take.

Which makes the instinctive response — work harder, hustle more, get ahead of it — a losing strategy, and he says so bluntly: *but this approach hasn't worked yet, has it?*

The practices are inward: find your center point, a still place that stays constant regardless of conditions; lead from stillness, which he defines as being a calm port rather than projecting false confidence; and his own example of choosing a 40-minute meditation over an hour of extra preparation before a coaching call.

It closes on the limit, from his own coach: **go as far into the darkness as you can without extinguishing your own light.**
