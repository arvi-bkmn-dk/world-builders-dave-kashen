# The Parts You Deny Are Running Your Business

## Summary

The written version of the shadow material from [[HoE 13 - The Shadow]], built around Dave's own "I am not an asshole."

He was the kind, supportive coach who internally cringed at anyone bulldozing a conversation. Then, in a session with a founder whose struggling team needed a cheerleader and who said *"that's not me, that's not authentic,"* the words came out before he could stop them: **"Bullshit. It's just not comfortable for you."** The room went silent — and he realised he'd just used a part of himself he'd denied for decades, and it was exactly what the moment required.

The claim: **the parts of yourself you deny are secretly running your business.** Denying them costs behavioural flexibility, and they leak out anyway — Dave names where his went: "snapping at my wife or yelling at my kids."

The paradox he lands on is the point: the more he accepted the part, the more **choice** he had about when to use it.

Second half covers judgment as the thing that stops flow, with the river metaphor.
