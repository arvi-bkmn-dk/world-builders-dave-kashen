# What Your Feelings Know Before You Do

## Summary

**A client didn't know he was sad.** Selling his company below the number he'd always imagined, he thought he was being rational and built around the sadness instead of feeling it. The contract included a call option at exactly that number. He ran the company five more years without the value moving a dollar: one unfelt feeling shaped a whole chapter.

**Love through a prism, continued.** Most people sort emotions into good and bad, but what you resist runs you from below the surface, and avoiding a feeling is one of the main ways we leave presence.

**Find it in your body.** Joy in the chest and core, sadness in the eyes and throat, anger in the back and shoulders, fear in the belly, excitement as energy. Felt directly, an emotion moves through in under two minutes; one that lasts days is a thought loop about it.

**Ask the wisdom question** (from the Conscious Leadership Group): what wants to be celebrated, let go of, stopped, known, created?

When the client felt it, he let go, brought in a new CEO and sold, happier for it. All emotions flow through the same pipe, so kinking it for sadness kinks it for love too.
