# Why I'm Giving Up, 2024 Realizations

## Summary

The most personal piece in his work, and the one that names the year's insight: the **Via Negativa** — the path of releasing rather than striving, on the premise that we are already whole, perfect and complete, so the journey is realising it rather than becoming it.

Its practical form is *drop your resistance*, with a five-part checklist of what people actually resist — tasks, feelings, thoughts, habits, and imagined bad outcomes — and Dave's strongest statement on burnout: using force to control reality is the deepest and truest cause of it. Force is resistance. **Force is trying to control by fighting against life; power is creating by flowing with life.**

It builds to four words. After a seven-day retreat high faded into irritability — "wound up, coiled, like a snake ready to strike" — Dave told his coach he had done fifteen years of work and was still battling anxiety and sadness. *I'm so tired of the struggle.* She said: **give up the fight.** He broke into tears.

The piece also inventories 2024, including the launch of this newsletter, the IGE program and the Heart of Entrepreneurship podcast — three of the primary sources in this graph.
