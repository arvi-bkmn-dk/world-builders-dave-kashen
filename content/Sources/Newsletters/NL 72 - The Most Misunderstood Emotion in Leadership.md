# The Most Misunderstood Emotion in Leadership

## Summary

In Dave's family, anger was for uncivilised people, so he became **cool, calm and collected**, carried it into his leadership, and was proud of it. It was costing him. Founders show the same pattern: late teams, underperformers, patience exhausted.

**All emotions are love through a prism.** Anger is love refracted as a boundary: pure boundary energy, and love can be fierce (*"Stop."*). The reactivity people associate with anger comes from the **unwillingness to be with it**, in the moment or accumulated over time. Suppression doesn't prevent reactive anger; it causes it.

**What anger tells you.** Light anger: a want isn't being met. Strong anger: something isn't okay. Ask what wants to be stopped out there, and in here.

**How to access it.** Identify it in the body, welcome it, express it safely (Dave's wiffle ball bat, chopping wood, screaming in the car), then communicate the want or set the boundary. Founders frustrated that the team won't move often haven't said *I want us to do this.*

Not feeling his anger left Dave struggling to decide. On the other side of the feeling is clarity.
