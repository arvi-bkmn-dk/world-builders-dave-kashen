# The Foundation of Everything

## Summary

The first newsletter, and it opens the series on **presence** as the foundation of everything else. Dave's framing: what you think is holding you back — misaligned expectations, growth strategy, team bottlenecks, too many hats — is usually downstream of a lack of presence.

His definition is operational rather than spiritual: "having your attention on what's actually happening in the moment, seeing reality clearly, and choosing the response most aligned with your authentic self and intentions." Most people are not present most of the time, because humans are wired to perceive threat and drift into seeing reality through a distorted lens of fear.

The claim that earns presence the front position is that it is what lets you see through the lies — the voice saying *you're not enough* or *you're going to fail* is simply not true, and presence is how you tell what is real from what is imagined. Which lands on Dave's recurring line: the team that sees reality clearest wins.

It closes with a concrete 32-second practice — 4x4 breathing, engage the senses, redirect without judgment.
