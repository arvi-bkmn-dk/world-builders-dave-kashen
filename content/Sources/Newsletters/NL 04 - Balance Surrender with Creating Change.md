# Balance Surrender with Creating Change

## Summary

A direct response to reader pushback on the previous newsletter: *the whole point of coaching seems to be making change, but your email seemed to be about the opposite.* Dave takes the objection seriously and spends the piece resolving it.

His answer is that surrender and creating change are not opposites — surrender is what makes creating possible, because the alternative is forcing outcomes from a state of threat. The mechanism is the **do-have-be** inversion: most people do things to have results so they can finally be safe, worthy and whole, on the false premise that they are not already. Reversed to be-do-have, action flows from a settled state rather than toward one.

The operational distinction is **preference versus attachment**. You keep the vision; you drop the belief that your wellbeing depends on it. From there creative tension works as intended — hold current reality and the desired future simultaneously, without collapsing the future into the present.

It closes with worked examples: micromanaging as resistance to the fear of losing control, fundraising and sales as resistance to the fear of rejection, relationships as resistance to the fear of conflict.
