# The Edge You Find When You Let Go

## Summary

At a workshop for the [[One Mind Accelerator]] (about 25 founders building mental health technology), the familiar fear surfaced: **if I accept myself and my startup as they are, will I lose my edge?** For these founders, accepting reality felt like abandoning the mission.

**Acceptance is not resignation.** It is scoped to right now and says nothing about the future. Accept that people suffer today, imagine a world where they don't, and build from that image. Resisting reality instead is exhausting and corrosive, like insisting the sky shouldn't be blue.

**The attachment underneath the attachment.** What holds founders is the belief that frustration is necessary, held together by fears: losing an identity built on drive, becoming apathetic, abandoning the mission.

**Your true edge.** Non-attachment isn't apathy; it's not believing your well-being depends on an outcome. What lets you care deeply without attachment is **the willingness to have your heart broken**, and keeping your heart open when it wants to close is the edge. Point it at the mission, your people and the way you build.
