# Welcome to the Inner Game of Entrepreneurship

## Summary

The first email, and the thesis statement for everything that follows. Not numbered in the archive — it sits before #1.

Dave defines the newsletter against its category: *"instead of giving you more business tactics, concepts, and strategies, we'll be going straight to the root cause of what actually enables founders to succeed."*

Then the frame itself, from **Timothy Gallwey's *The Inner Game of Tennis***: athletes play two games, the visible **Outer Game** of technique and the unseen **Inner Game** within. Applied to entrepreneurship — Outer Game is the pitch, the frameworks, the scaling advice; Inner Game is *"your state, perception, mindset, thought patterns, beliefs, self-image, and emotional resilience."* **Your outer results are a reflection of your inner world.**

Three supports: mindset drives action; leadership starts within; and lasting fulfillment — *"chasing only the Outer Game leads to burnout and dissatisfaction, even if you win."* Master the inner game and **you win twice.**

He then lists his own Outer Game in full — Worklife (YC W15, acquired by Cisco), Wellsphere (acquired by IAC/HealthCentral), Fearless Ventures, SPO Partners, Goldman Sachs, Wharton, Stanford GSB — precisely so the conclusion can't be dismissed: *"despite achieving all of these 'outer' successes, I still felt unfulfilled."*

And the story underneath it: *"about 15 years ago, I discovered that I'd been driven by a story I told myself — that I was unworthy."*
