# The Key to Overcoming Scarcity

## Summary

A founder with **two weeks of runway**, refreshing his bank balance and rehearsing the conversation he'd have with his team. Dave's intervention: with two weeks left, *"nothing could be more important than using his precious creative energy to create the future he wanted instead of wasting it imagining the future he didn't."* Weeks later he had raised over a million dollars. **"He didn't find more resources. He found more resourcefulness."**

The distinction doing the work: **finite is not the same as scarce.** Time is finite and the bank balance is a number — those are facts. *"But scarcity has within it the notion of not enough. And 'not enough' is always a story."*

Scarcity stories put the system into threat, and *"your creative energy is the resource that actually matters."*

**On time:** eight hours of capacity and fifteen hours of list means *"you will never do more than eight hours of work in eight hours. The only thing there is to do is prioritize well."* **Overwhelm is an optional overlay to reality.**

**On money:** *"before you started your company, it had zero money. Your resourcefulness brought in the capital. What makes you think you can't do it again?"*

And the trap that compounds it: **self-reliance learned early becomes maladaptive at scale** — you spend creative energy on work you could hand off, reinforce the not-enough-time story, and cut yourself off from resourcefulness, all at once.

*"The scarcity was never in reality. It was in his interpretation of reality."*
