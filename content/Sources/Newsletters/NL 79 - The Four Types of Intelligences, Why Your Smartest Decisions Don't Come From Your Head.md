# The Four Types of Intelligences: Why Your Smartest Decisions Don't Come From Your Head

## Summary

A client's new CTO interviewed perfectly. His wife shook the man's hand once and got **"a weird feeling."** Monday, he never showed up, and reneged by email that afternoon. She couldn't explain it because it didn't come from the part of us that explains things, and that part is only one of four intelligences.

**Your mind is wired for survival, not truth.** Built for predators, running at threat level ten in a world close to one, reading a hard board meeting like a lion. Its fear-based predictions are almost always wrong. And it presents itself as serving your fulfillment. The heart pumps blood and the mind pumps thoughts; we don't treat every heartbeat as news.

**The four intelligences.** The **head** thinks, and is the slowest. The **heart** is emotion and desire: following it is effortless. The **gut** knows instantly, though fear and shame can block it. **Universal intelligence** isn't limited to the body; the mind is a receiver.

**Building the muscle.** Drop into the body, calibrate with extremes (powder day vs tax prep), practise on low stakes, check fear and anger when the gut is quiet, look for the whole body yes, then sense your company as a living organism (Frederic Laloux). Aliveness is every intelligence saying yes at once.
