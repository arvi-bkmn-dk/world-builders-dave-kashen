# What You Feel Is Not What You Want

## Summary

The founders of the Conscious Leadership Group gave Dave a choice: go all in as a partner, or move on. Imagining leaving brought overwhelming sadness, so he **turned the feeling into evidence** that he wanted to stay. It was real, and it was pointing away from what he wanted.

**Feelings and wants are not the same thing.** Most decisions are made to feel or avoid a feeling. Feelings are weather; wants are a compass bearing, audible only when you're not using the decision to manage a feeling. Sadness is the energy of letting go, and grief honours what you loved without meaning you should keep doing it. The same trap keeps founders from firing someone, shutting a product, or leaving a company.

**How to hear what's underneath.** Joe Hudson's question: *if you knew you'd be happy either way, what would you do?* Name the loudest feeling, ask the question, then ask whether you're willing to feel what the choice requires.

Dave left CLG crying like it was a funeral, and it was one of the clearest decisions of his life.
