# Champagne Bubbles, Wiffle Ball Bats and Premature Scaling

## Summary

Dave opens by naming the practices that would strike most founders as strange — beating pillows with a wiffle ball bat, sitting still and feeling "champagne bubbles" in the body, releasing the need to do or know anything — and reporting that the more he leans in, the more effortless action becomes.

The substance is a challenge from a founder at his workshop: *if you're really at peace, will you have the drive to achieve massive success?* She wanted proof in the form of unicorns run by conscious leaders. Dave named Shopify, Asana, Canva, Devoted Health, Gusto and Fetch.

A reader of the previous newsletter had pressed the same doubt from the other side: *the leaders I see up the org chart don't exhibit any of these behaviors, yet they're in positions of power.*

Both questions point at the same thing — whether conscious leadership and massive success can coexist — which is the premise the whole newsletter and podcast exist to argue.
