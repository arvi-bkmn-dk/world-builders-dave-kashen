# How to Make Clear, Conscious Decisions

## Summary

A founder at 2am with a \$100m term sheet, 48 hours to decide, and a gut that says \$150m. Seven years, 80-hour weeks, missed family dinners. Spreadsheet says take it; heart says hold; the mind ping-pongs between *what if this is the only offer* and *what if I'm leaving \$50m on the table.*

Dave's move is to relocate the problem entirely: **content doesn't determine your decision quality, context does.** Content is the facts and data; context is the lens you view them through. Which means the decision "isn't really about the external circumstances — it's about the unconscious beliefs and emotional state driving how you process the choice."

That explains the paralysis he says he gets called about constantly: sharpening the analysis while the context is fear just produces more precise fear.

## Key Teachings

- [[Content does not determine your decision quality, context does]]

Restated here from earlier sources:

- [[A choice only feels like a hard decision when you are scared of it]]
- [[More thinking makes clarity worse, like shaking sand in a bottle]]
- [[Fear feels urgent and constricting, intuition feels clear and spacious]]
- [[We make decisions emotionally and rationalise them afterwards]]
- [[Figuring out the right answer is a strategy for avoiding the feeling]]
- [[Both parts want the same outcome and have different strategies]]
