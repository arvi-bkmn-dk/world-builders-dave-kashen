# Planning for the Future While Staying Present

## Summary

**Which moments of building a house happen outside the present?** None. The blueprint gets drawn at a real desk on a real afternoon. Planning is done in the present, like swinging a hammer. Mistake it for leaving presence, and people get good at responding while they stop creating.

**Presence doesn't mean doing nothing.** A client could sit on a park bench for hours in presence, then open his to-do list and be back at the grindstone. Less on your plate and operating from presence often arrive together, but your work is where you train presence. What actually pulled him out was worry: he accepted what is in the moment, then pre-resisted a feared future moment.

**Finding what pulls you out.** Don't try to be present all the time; relax into it. Write your own recipe for drifting out of presence. Separate thinking about what could go wrong from worrying about it. Scenario plan from presence, and trust future you.

**The blueprint still gets drawn.** When nothing needs fixing, you can go create what you want, and see clearly what needs to happen when.
