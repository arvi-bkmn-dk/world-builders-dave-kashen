# Why Presence Is the Foundation of Everything

## Summary

A founder in a one-on-one realises he hasn't heard a word in five minutes. Dave's claim is that this isn't poor focus: **if you're not present, you can't receive anything**, from your team, a mentor, or life.

**Why presence slips.** Thoughts are reactions to not welcoming feelings. A feeling arises, the mind escapes into strategizing and replaying, stories fill the gap, and the stories calcify until you are reacting to a world your mind built.

**Where it costs most: listening.** Dave's own filter is correcting (*"Wait, I don't think that was Tuesday"*). Beyond diagnosing, defending and debating, he names the ones people miss: **self-referencing, knowing, avoiding conflict, efforting.** Each is the same escape, played out in conversation.

**Listening without a motive.** Head (what they're saying), heart (what they feel), gut (what they really want), then reflect it back. People visibly relax and start solving their own problems; Dave has run sixty-minute sessions of pure listening that clients thanked him for as advice.

**Where it begins.** You can't listen to others because you aren't listening to yourself. Start with the body: feet on the floor, notice the chest and gut, breathe four by four, four times. That quality of attention **is what love is**, and it is what seeing reality clearly means.
