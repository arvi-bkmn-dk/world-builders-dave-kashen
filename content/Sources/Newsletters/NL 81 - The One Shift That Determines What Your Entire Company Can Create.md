# The One Shift That Determines What Your Entire Company Can Create

## Summary

Dave's Twitter handle was once *@AwesomeCulture*. After years of building culture scaffolding, he kept watching a triggered leader spread fear and turn the values into hypocrisy. So between designing a founder's culture and working on their **state**, he'd choose the state: **who you're being beats what you're saying.**

**Two things determine success:** who's on your team and the state they operate from, which together set the density of creative energy aimed at your vision. Both trace back to the founder's state.

**Who's on your team.** Leaders who'd run the company as well as you. Fear is what stops you letting go of people below the bar, and what tempts you to settle.

**The state they operate from.** Your state spreads. At Fetch Rewards the CEO flipped *if we don't, we can't raise* to *if we do, good things happen*, and the tension loosened while creative output jumped.

**Why you can't see your own state.** Fish in water. The newsletter closes on enrollment for the Sun Valley Founders Retreat.
