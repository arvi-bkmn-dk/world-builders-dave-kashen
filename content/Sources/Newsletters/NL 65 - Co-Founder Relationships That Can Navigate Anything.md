# Co-Founder Relationships That Can Navigate Anything

## Summary

Follow-up to [[NL 62 - How to Choose a Co-Founder You Won't Regret]]: choosing well matters, and **maintaining the relationship matters just as much.**

**The story that took eight years to check.** [[Alex Lieberman]] of Morning Brew believed he was the weak link. He had never said so to his co-founder. One two-and-a-half-hour call dissolved it.

**Withhold, Withdraw, Project**, from Gay and Kathlyn Hendricks. Withhold your truth and you withdraw; once disconnected, you see the other person through your projected story and gather evidence for it. **The antidote is Reveal, Connect, Own.**

**Invest in the relationship.** The emotional bank account fills through dedicated time on the relationship itself (5% more time, 100% more uncomfortable), sweaty-palm conversations, and appreciation, which most founders are stingy with.

**Friendship on the other side of conflict.** Two kinds of harmony, and only the second builds trust, which is an efficiency multiple. Vulnerability compounds into trust. At Fearless Ventures, Dave and his best friend ordered it: growth, friendship, then the fund. The ultimate test: **could you fire your co-founder from their role and keep the friendship?**
