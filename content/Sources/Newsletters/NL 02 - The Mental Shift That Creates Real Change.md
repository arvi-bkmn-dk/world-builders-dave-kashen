# The Mental Shift That Creates Real Change

## Summary

Victim and creator consciousness, opened with a CEO who had vision, talent, capital and a good team, and whose every conversation was a list of reasons things weren't working. One question moved it: **how are you creating this?**

The shift Dave names is from "To Me" to "By Me" — life doesn't happen to you, it happens by you. He is careful on two points. Victim consciousness is not a moral failing but a lack of awareness, and most people are in it most of the time without knowing: "like fish in water, they think this is just how it is." And the first move is self-acceptance rather than correction, because you can't shift what you don't accept.

The founder-specific stake comes at the end: your consciousness sets the tone for the whole team. Operate as a victim and they mirror it.

Dave credits the Conscious Leadership Group explicitly for the distinctions.
