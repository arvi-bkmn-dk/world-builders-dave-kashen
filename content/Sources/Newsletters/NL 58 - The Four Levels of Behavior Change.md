# The Four Levels of Behavior Change

## Summary

Built on Dave's own failure. At **Meeting Hero** — the product he and co-founder Val built to improve meetings — users loved it, added agendas, tracked action items. *"For about two weeks. Then they'd stop."* Published January 18th, when *"80% of New Year's resolutions have already failed."*

**Level 1 — Friction.** BJ Fogg's **B = MAT**. The counterintuitive part: *"they try to increase motivation. According to Fogg, that's actually the least effective thing you can do. What's most effective is shrinking the ability required."* Don't commit to running three days a week — put your running clothes out before bed. *"Our brains resist change. The more change, the more resistance."*

**Level 2 — Interpretation.** *"For years, meditation felt like eating my broccoli… At some point it shifted from eating my broccoli to eating my chocolate. Same activity. Completely different relationship."* Plus Joe Hudson's reframe: not *I failed* but **"that experiment didn't work."**

**Level 3 — Identity.** Self-concept and self-judgment. **"When being meets doing, being wins."**

**Level 4 — Authentic Wants.** The foundation: **authentic wants arise from wholeness; compensatory behaviors arise from lack.** *"Is this something I authentically want? Or am I trying to fix something I think is wrong with me?"*

Diagnose downward, fix at the broken layer — *"most people start with Level 1 tactics when the real breakdown is at Level 3."*

Ends with a 30-year smoker who quit almost immediately after feeling real self-love for the first time. *"That's what happens when all the levels align."*
