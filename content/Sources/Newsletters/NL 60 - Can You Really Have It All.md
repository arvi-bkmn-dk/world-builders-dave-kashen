# Can You Really Have It All

## Summary

Written after the second annual Sun Valley Founders Retreat — *"in less than four days, 10 founders went from strangers to a deeply loving, connected group of friends."*

The universal thread among them: **is it possible to experience inner peace while still being ambitious and successful?** Most feared it wasn't.

Dave names the either/or as a below-the-line artifact and then refuses to argue with the counter-evidence. *"Elon Musk. Travis Kalanick. Many fear-based founders and toxic cultures do seem to create massive valuations. So here's what I said to the group: **yes, we know you can build a big company from fear, lack and stress. But let's raise the bar.**"* His name for the alternative is **surf and turf** — *"I want to have it all. Both the steak and the fish."*

The room tested the claim from both ends. A founder who had **just shut down a startup after raising $20M** was *"experiencing the thing founders fear most, and he was totally okay."* A founder who had achieved the dream exit *"was not somehow just totally perfect."* Conclusion: **peace and success are completely independent of each other**, so you're free to pursue both.

The **love seat** — five minutes of being appreciated by the group — was *"the thing people were most nervous about. Hearts racing. Voices shaking."*

## Notable Quotes

> Is it possible to experience inner peace while still being ambitious and successful?

> This either/or, binary thinking typically shows up when we're below the line, operating from fear and scarcity.

> Yes, we know you can build a big company from fear, lack and stress. But let's raise the bar.

> Because you can also build a big company from love, presence and a beautiful inner state.

> One of my core values I jokingly refer to as surf and turf. I want to have it all. Both the steak and the fish.

> Not despite his success, but because of how he's building it.

> It helped everyone separate their 'being' from their 'doing'.

> Achieving outer success does not lead to lasting inner fulfillment.

> Peace and success are completely independent of each other.

> Just how difficult it is for most of us to simply let love in.

> Not deferring those feelings to some future milestone, but allowing yourself to experience them now.

> That's surf and turf. Success without suffering.
