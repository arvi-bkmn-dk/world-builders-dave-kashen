# Breaking Free, How Your Hidden Beliefs Shape Your Startup's Reality

## Summary

Takes "your beliefs create your reality" and strips the mysticism out of it, replacing it with a five-step loop: beliefs determine what you notice → perception shapes thoughts and emotions → those drive decisions and actions → actions create results → results reinforce the original belief. Which explains how two founders in identical circumstances arrive at opposite outcomes.

The piece's real contribution is **meta-beliefs**. Asking *is this belief true?* usually fails, because a second belief makes examining the first feel unsafe. So the working question is **"what am I afraid would happen if I let go of this belief?"** — which surfaces the guard rather than attacking the wall.

Dave grounds their origin in childhood protection: a child not getting the attention they want concludes they are unworthy of it, "because this creates the possibility of becoming worthy, rather than the untenable belief that there might be something deficient about their parent." The nervous system files these as survival-critical and locks them away, which is why they resist inspection rather than merely being wrong.

Closes with the seven he sees most in founders — impostor, control, struggle-as-prerequisite, failure-as-identity, scarcity, wealth-as-shallow, and needing everyone's approval.
