# Are You Actually Cut Out to Be a Founder

## Summary

A client, eyes down: *"I don't think I'm cut out to be a founder."* Dave notices the same knot in his own stomach, because he's had the thought "countless times" and whispered it to himself at 2am.

His first move is to puncture the premise — was anyone born cut out for this? Was Zuckerberg at 23 equipped to run a global company? — and then reframe the doubt: **doubt doesn't mean you're not cut out, it means you're in the arena.**

The substance is the loop. Founders avoiding the feeling of helplessness get trapped in needing to be right and have all the answers, which blocks the curiosity they'd need to actually grow. **Avoiding helplessness keeps them helpless.** Hustle harder, don't ask for help, don't admit uncertainty — every move preserves the appearance of competence at the cost of acquiring it.

Closes on three core capacities, starting with resourcefulness over resources: *"you never start with all the resources. You didn't learn how to walk already knowing how to walk."*
