# Reading This Email is Meditation

## Summary

A newsletter that is itself the practice rather than a description of one.

Dave's premise: meditation isn't confined to sitting still with your eyes closed. And the sharper claim — **the most effective founders aren't the ones who meditate twenty minutes every morning, they're the ones who can find presence within the chaos.** Most practices pull you away from your life; the question is whether you can bring the practice into it.

The body of the email walks the reader through it live: soften focus until peripheral vision returns, notice the words still read effortlessly, expand awareness into temperature and sound and the weight of the body, feel the contact points of the device, watch thoughts arrive without following them.

The instruction that does the work is the one about sensation over label — *instead of "I'm anxious" or "I'm calm," feel the raw sensations. Not your thoughts about your body. Not the story about why you feel a certain way.*
