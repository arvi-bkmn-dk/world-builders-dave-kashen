# The Box You're Starting In, How Your Enneagram Type Shapes Your Leadership

## Summary

Uses the Enneagram as a map of the **box** a founder starts inside — "patterns that may have served you as a child but are now unconsciously running your life."

Dave's reason for preferring it: other assessments tell you what you do; the Enneagram tells you **why**. The type is the lens through which you see, decide and respond to stress by default.

The core mechanic is that your greatest strength, overused, becomes your greatest weakness — which is why effort makes it worse. And the patterns don't just limit you, they manufacture the problem you're solving: the micromanager creates a team that can't function without them; the conflict-avoider builds a culture where issues go unaddressed.

It walks all nine types with the belief underneath each, then makes the point that knowing your box buys **choice** rather than a label — "respond with intention rather than react from habit," which is the same destination as full behavioural flexibility reached from a different direction.
