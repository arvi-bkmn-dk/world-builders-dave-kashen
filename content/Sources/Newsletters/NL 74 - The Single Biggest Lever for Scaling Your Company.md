# The Single Biggest Lever for Scaling Your Company

## Summary

**Be bored to build a billion-dollar company.** The one lever that makes it possible, and that most founders under-invest in: a leadership team that runs the company without you. Most end up with a very good team instead, not for lack of talent but because holding the bar and letting people go is hard.

**What world-class looks like.** Leaders you believe would do the job as well as or better than you. Netflix cut 30–40% in 2008 and total performance improved: talent density drives performance, and people notice what gets tolerated.

**Why letting go is hard.** Attachment spikes after hiring, and the team starts to feel like family. Better frame: a coach who loves players and still trades them, because a team's purpose is the mission. Holding on is a hero move; see them as capable of their feelings.

**Put it to work.** Ask of each leader whether they'd do the job as well as you, then run the Netflix Keeper Test, checking whether your answer is about the person or the situation. Name the fear stories: the gap, morale, departures. A players hire A players. Eric Ries: **choose your hard.**
