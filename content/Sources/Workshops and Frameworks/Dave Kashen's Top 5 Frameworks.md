# Dave Kashen's Top 5 Frameworks

## Summary

The single most important source in the corpus, because it is Dave naming his own models rather than teaching around them. Five frameworks, each written up as a step-by-step process with the failure modes labelled: the **Focus-Motivation Model** (Flow as clean fuel, and the two drifts out of it), the **Storyteller Mind** (stories as meaning added to neutral facts, the seven Storyteller archetypes, and the Story Release Technique), the **Responsibility Roadmap** (the fork between "this is a problem" and "it is what is"), the **Edge Expansion Process** (the Founder Fear Map — five core fears, five leadership breakdowns), and the **Law of Expanding Possibilities** (emotional capacity as the ceiling on what a company can become).

One thread runs through all five and it is the same one that opens the podcast: the founder's inner state is the causal variable, and every operational symptom is downstream of it. Each framework attacks that from a different angle — motivation, cognition, responsibility, fear, capacity — and each ends in the same place, with the team mirroring whatever state the founder is actually in.

- [[A story is the meaning your mind adds to neutral facts]]

- [[The narrating voice in your head is a survival mechanism, not your self]]
- [[Your stories set the emotional climate of your team]]
- [[Naming a story out loud creates separation from it]]
- [[Interpreting a situation as a problem is what drops you into blame]]
- [[You cannot change what you do not accept]]
- [[How am I creating this is the question that converts acceptance into power]]
- [[Acceptance is the doorway to effective action, not apathy]]
- [[Your state is contagious to your team]]
- [[Your personal edges create your company's ceiling]]
- [[Courage is being with fear and acting anyway, not the absence of fear]]
- [[The body signals that you are at an edge before the mind does]]
- [[Your results are limited by which feelings you are unwilling to feel]]
- [[Emotional capacity problems get misdiagnosed as performance problems]]
- [[An unresisted emotion moves through the body in 60 to 90 seconds]]
- [[Treat the startup as an emotional intensity gym]]

## Notable Quotes

> The reason building feels hard is not the work itself. It's the relationship you have with the work.

> Resistance and attachment = suffering.

> It's the Storyteller Mind — an ancient survival mechanism that interprets neutral facts through the lens of fear and conditioning.

> Two founders with identical challenges can have completely different experiences. The difference isn't the facts. It's the story they're believing about those facts.

> Taking responsibility for your current situation empowers you to create the future you want.

> What you resist persists. You can't change what you don't accept.

> Your personal edges create your company's ceiling.

> Courage is not the absence of fear. It's being with fear and doing it anyway.
