# WS 01 - The Inner Keys to Becoming a World-Class Leader

## Summary

A ninety-minute public workshop for founders and leaders, a live taste of the Inner Game program. Dave defines a leader as someone who creates a future that otherwise wouldn't occur, then argues the core problem: **humans are wired to survive, not to thrive**, so most of a founder's creative energy is spent on the brakes. That's the Ferrari with one foot on each pedal. The key to success is the share of your and your team's creative energy aimed at the future you want.

He names five default obstacles and their inner keys: **threat → presence, control → communicating to reveal, resistance → welcoming, rightness → preference, fear → inspiration**. Three run as live exercises: the Conscious Leadership [[The Recipe|recipe]] exercise for taking responsibility, a resist-then-welcome emotion exercise (credited to Joe Hudson's insight), and a guided creative tension visualization.

The Q&A carries some of the most practical teaching: a likely-unicorn client who believed he could have it all and built a leadership team that runs without him; staying human when anxiety returns at 5am; integration as a toddler learning to walk; decisions as fear, and authenticity as the real "secret" of admired founders; and a buyout dispute worked through fairness-as-story, win for all only counting if you're winning, and interest-based negotiation. The course pitch and pricing aren't extracted.
