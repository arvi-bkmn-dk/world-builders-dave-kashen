# HoE 04 — Self-Awareness and the Body

## Summary

The most emotionally live episode so far, because it opens with Andrew in it rather than discussing it. He and Jesse look at the same balance sheet — a business running 50% profit margins in Q4 — and have opposite reactions. Andrew ends up close to tears, and says the thing underneath: *the numbers are a representation of who I am.* Dave reflects it back and asks for the feeling. It's fear.

That becomes the episode's subject: the self-awareness that everything else depends on. Jesse's account of his first year with Dave is the frame — he wanted to be taught how to be a great CEO and got *first you must notice*, which he heard as Mr Miyagi nonsense for about a year. Dave reframes his own work as **self-discovery, not self-improvement**, on the grounds that your true self can't be improved.

The mechanics are the best part of his work so far. Dave's opening question to new clients — *how do you know when you're thinking?* — leads to the observation that hearing a voice in your head requires both a talker and a listener, and that most people spend their entire lives identified as the talker. Then the body: we make decisions emotionally and rationalise them afterwards, so **most people's lives are limited by which physical sensations they're willing to feel**. Jesse's early practice was a thoughts/feelings/sensations check-in at the start of every session, which eventually let him say *I feel fear* out loud in a meeting instead of dressing someone down.

The last third is the theory of emotion. Anger isn't blocking your flow — resistance to anger is. All feelings run through the same pipe, so blocking one blocks all, and joy is the matriarch who won't come if her children aren't let in. No emotion is painful; the resistance is. And the fear that you'll drown in a feeling if you let it in is, in Dave's flat assessment, a lie.
