# HoE 06 — The Acknowledgement Party

## Summary

The longest episode of the batch and the most uncomfortable to be inside, because Andrew spends most of it being appreciated against his will.

It opens with Jesse's story from Half Moon Bay in 2018: Dave and another coach sat his exec team at a dinner table and had them go round asking each other for acknowledgement. It was excruciating — the first round took thirty minutes, and the more senior the person the worse it got. Then partway through the second round it flipped. People stopped asking and started giving, unprompted, to colleagues they'd worked with for years. Two hours, everyone crying. Dave's name for it: an **acknowledgement party**.

The theoretical spine is that **receiving is the blind spot**, not giving. Dave tells the story of believing for most of his life that his father didn't love him unconditionally, until he realised he simply wasn't letting it in — the love hadn't arrived in the form he'd decided it should take. What finally pierced it was his dad saying *you know how you feel about Ben and Brody? That's how I feel about you.*

Andrew then demonstrates the thesis in real time, repeatedly deflecting appreciation, negotiating the terms of a text to his wife, and — when Jesse finally acknowledges him — reporting embarrassment. Jesse slows the tape: something good was felt for a moment, then a part jumped in to ask why the acknowledgement wasn't bigger, then a third part got embarrassed that this was the best available.

Along the way: hiding behind "that's not authentic for me," *what you appreciate appreciates*, the entitlement practice, the NLP line about survival conditions, and a closing seed about already being whole, perfect and complete.
