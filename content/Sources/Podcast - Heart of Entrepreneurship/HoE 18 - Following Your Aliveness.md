# HoE 18 — Following Your Aliveness

## Summary

Jesse brings a document he keeps sneaking back to between calls, and the instruction Dave kept giving him: *"I just want you to follow your aliveness."* This episode is him asking what that actually means.

Dave has just returned from **Welcoming Fear**, a four-day retreat with cliff jumps and a 300-foot highline — which frames the whole conversation, because *"there seems to be a relationship between fear and aliveness."*

**What it is.** Aliveness as *"the truest guide to our unique, authentic path"* — and known physically, not conceptually: energy rising, weightless, buzzing, versus constricted and heavy. Dave's image for navigating by it is a rope: *"the way you know whether you're on it is you feel the energy of it… your job is to detect, rather than figure out."* He is careful that it doesn't have to be loud — the **still small voice**, quiet moments, and **connection** all count. Jesse's examples are new business ideas, deep breathwork, and the moment each of his children was born.

**The anti-patterns.** Survival patterns learned before age eight, running now in fundraising meetings — *"if you're focusing on survival, you're not focusing on fully living."* His client example distinguishes *the job* from *the way I'm doing the job*: the man loved the work, hated the heroing. Then the central irony: since everything we fear is ultimately a feeling, and what we want underneath everything is aliveness, **we take a path that doesn't lead to aliveness as a strategy to get to aliveness.**

**The tell.** Being in your head. *"All of that spinning is actually just a strategy to avoid a feeling."*

**The blockers**, via the CLG Four Pillars — with an important correction on unkept agreements: keeping every one *"would make you a slave to your former self."*

**Fear, resolved.** Jesse pushes on the apparent contradiction with dirty fuel, and Dave corrects his own past framing: the issue is not fear, it's **resistance** to fear, and "fear-based motivation" means *being motivated by not wanting to feel scared.*

Ends on Diana Chapman's question: **what are you willing to risk for your full aliveness?**
