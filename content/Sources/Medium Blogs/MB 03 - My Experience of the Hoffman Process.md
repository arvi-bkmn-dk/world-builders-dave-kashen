# - My Experience of the Hoffman Process

## Summary

Dave's write-up of the **Hoffman Process**, the 7-day residential retreat he did from 6 to 12 May 2023, written three days after finishing. He frames it from fifteen years as a conscious leadership coach, and admits he went in skeptical and was "blown away."

The frameworks come first. The **Negative Love Syndrome**: children adopt their parents' negative patterns to stay connected and loved. The **Quadrinity**, which Dave credits to Bob Hoffman. The **left road and right road**, which he maps to below and above the line. **Be > Do > Have**. And the **Cycle of Transformation**: Awareness, Expression, Compassion & forgiveness, New ways of being.

Then the personal account. His core pattern was the belief that "spirit isn't real", sitting under a long list of smaller patterns and a core shame message. He describes forgiving his parents and stepparents, a truce between his intellect and his emotional child, the qualities of his essence, and a vow. He closes by naming his fear of seeming "too woo woo", and publishes anyway.
