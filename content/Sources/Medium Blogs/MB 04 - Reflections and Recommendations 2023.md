# - Reflections and Recommendations 2023

## Summary

Dave's year-end post for 2023. It opens with an image that carries the whole piece: a light bulb walking around its whole life asking "where's the light?"

It covers highlights, including the Hoffman Process, Tony Robbins' Unleash the Power Within and Date with Destiny, a silent retreat, becoming a Sun Valley ski instructor, and a "very vulnerable, authentic interview" on the Danny Miranda podcast. Then eight reflections:
- Letting go of the thought *Spirit isn't real*.
- The Via Negativa: nothing to attain, only barriers to remove.
- Contribution reduces suffering.
- The six needs everyone ranks differently.
- Surrender makes his coaching more powerful.
- Two ways of getting answers.
- The three pillars of state.
- Narrow rules for feeling good and broad rules for feeling bad.

It closes with declarations for 2024 and recommendations: face the local maximum you're living at, distinguish Being from Doing, do an immersive experience, and get a coach.

## Key Teachings

- [[All behavior is an attempt to meet the same needs in a different priority order]]
- [[The more you focus on contributing to others, the less you suffer]]
