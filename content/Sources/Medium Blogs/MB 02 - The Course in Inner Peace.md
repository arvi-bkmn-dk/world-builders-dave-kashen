# - The Course in Inner Peace

## Summary

*The Course* is Dave's attempt at "a course in inner peace" in the fewest, simplest words, with a promise: do the practices and you will experience inner peace.

It builds in short steps. **Subject and object:** you have thoughts, feelings, sensations, a body and a self-image, and you are none of them. **All events are neutral;** only thoughts about them are positive or negative. Peace at a sunset comes from letting go of wishing anything were different. **Suffering is a byproduct of misidentification.** The nature of who you are is peaceful, "like the fragrance of a flower."

Then love. To love is to experience directly, without judgment, resistance or attachment. Love is a choice, and refusing it means you are scared. The practice is to locate what you haven't been willing to love and what you're unwilling to let go of, and release it, including giving away at least 10% of your net worth.
