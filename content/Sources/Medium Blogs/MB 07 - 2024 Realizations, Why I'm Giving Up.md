# - 2024 Realizations, Why I'm Giving Up

## Summary

The Medium publication of [[NL 03 - Why I'm Giving Up, 2024 Realizations]], the same story built around the four words *give up the fight*. The two are one piece published twice. This note carries the Medium text, and new material lives on the ideas rather than being restated here.

What the Medium version makes explicit:
- The Via Negativa definition is credited to **Loch Kelly**.
- The retreat whose afterglow faded was **Joe Hudson's Groundbreakers**.
- A longer closing section on results argues that surrender is the most resourceful state (quoting Tony Robbins on resourcefulness), that decisions are emotional (the brain-injury research), that hard decisions are the ones we fear, and that leaders should feel rather than figure, crediting the Art of Accomplishment episode *Feel over Figure*.

## Key Teachings

Restated here from earlier sources:

- [[Give up the fight]]
- [[Force is trying to control by fighting against life, power is creating by flowing with life]]
- [[What have I been resisting this year]]
- [[You are already whole, perfect and complete]]
- [[We make decisions emotionally and rationalise them afterwards]]
- [[A choice only feels like a hard decision when you are scared of it]]
- [[Figuring out the right answer is a strategy for avoiding the feeling]]
- [[Scarcity is a mindset, not a reality]]
- [[Joy is the experience of recognizing your true self]]
