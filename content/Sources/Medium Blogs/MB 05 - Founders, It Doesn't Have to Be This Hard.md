# - Founders, It Doesn't Have to Be This Hard

## Summary

The article Jesse tells HoE listeners to read (*"I googled Dave Kashen DFS… Founders, it doesn't have to be this hard"*), and the fullest written treatment of **Creative Tension** in his work.

It opens on a myth: that pain and insecurity are necessary for a founder to succeed. "Chip on your shoulder, chips in your pocket." Founders motivate with tension built from lack (resisting what is) and fear (attachment to what they want), and those are "the only two things that actually cause stress." The article then works through each objection:
- **The sky shouldn't be blue:** telling yourself things should be different.
- **Letting go of success being better than failure:** illustrated by Dave losing nearly 70% of his net worth in 2022 and discovering he was okay.
- **FEAR as False Expectations Appearing Real.**
- **Apathy versus non-attachment,** and **preference versus attachment:** you can play to win without being attached.

The replacement is **Creative Tension**: Current Reality (without resistance) plus Desired Future (without attachment), a leadership fractal for all-hands meetings, one-on-ones and yourself. It lists three pitfalls (collapsing the future into the present, focusing on the undesired future, extrapolating setbacks), with client stories: a founder who raised $1mm in three weeks, and a 1,000-person company that doubled revenue. It ends on JFK, the reticular activating system and the rubber band.

- [[Scenario planning is chosen once, rumination replays the undesired future all day]]

Restated here from earlier sources:

- [[Resistance and attachment are what make the founder journey feel heavy, not the circumstances]]
- [[Founders choose fear because they believe their drive would disappear without it]]
- [[Suffering is the gap between life and your mind's idea of how life should be]]
- [[Are you willing to see how your startup failing could be as good as it succeeding]]
- [[Failing might be the thing that liberates you from your ego]]
- [[What do you love about yourself that has nothing to do with your money or success]]
- [[Fear makes bad predictions]]
- [[You can play to win without being attached to winning]]
- [[The less attached you are to an outcome, the easier it is to create]]
- [[Frustration is a desired future collapsed into the present]]
- [[Fear is the undesired future, which is the same sentence framed backwards]]
- [[What you focus on is where you steer]]
- [[Declaring a future you do not know how to reach is part of how you reach it]]
- [[Creative tension is a rubber band that can be too slack or snapped]]
- [[Apathy is not caring, non-attachment is not believing your well-being depends on the outcome]]
