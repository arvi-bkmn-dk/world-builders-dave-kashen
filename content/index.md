Dave Kashen coaches startup founders. He was a two-time venture-backed founder himself — Worklife (YC-backed, acquired by Cisco) and Wellsphere (acquired by HealthCentral) — and he now works with CEOs on the part of the job that strategy decks don't cover: fear, identity, presence, and what a leader's inner state does to a company. He co-hosts *The Heart of Entrepreneurship* with Jesse Pujji.

His material is scattered across 142 newsletters, episodes, articles, interviews and workshops. This is that material, broken into linked notes so an idea can be followed wherever it appears.

## How to read it

**Every quote is verbatim.** Blockquotes are Dave's exact words from a published source, not paraphrase. Each note names the source it came from.

**Attribution is deliberate.** Much of what Dave teaches, he learned — from the [[Conscious Leadership Group]], [[Joe Hudson]], [[Katie and Gay Hendricks]] and others — and he says so. Notes record the chain as he reports it, hedges included. See [[Quote Attributions]].

**Notes link outward.** A teaching links to the model it belongs to, the practice that trains it, and the adjacent claims that qualify it. Following the links is the point; the index tables are just doors.

**Sources are published material only** — newsletters, podcast episodes, public articles and guest interviews. Private coaching sessions and unreleased work are not part of this.
