# Alex Raymond

Host of **The Conscious Entrepreneur Podcast** and organiser of the Conscious Entrepreneur Summit (Boulder). He introduces himself as a longtime friend of Dave's who stepped back from running a company. His lines in the interview are his own and are never attributed to Dave:

- the inner game as an **infinite game**
- zooming out to see *it's just what I'm doing today, not my life*
- "if-then thinking", which he credits to Srikumar Rao
- *The Untethered Soul* as his most-given book
