# Hoffman Institute

## Who

The organisation behind **the Hoffman Process**, a 7-day residential personal growth retreat created by **Bob Hoffman**. It was formerly called the Hoffman Quadrinity Process. Dave completed it from 6 to 12 May 2023 and wrote it up in detail, and he later names it among the immersive experiences he recommends to clients.

## What Comes From Here

- **The Quadrinity:** the four aspects of a human being (Emotional, Intellectual, Physical, Spiritual). Dave: *"a term coined by Bob Hoffman."* → [[The Quadrinity]]
- **The Negative Love Syndrome:** we adopt our parents' negative patterns to feel connected and loved → [[We adopt our parents' negative patterns in an attempt to feel connected and loved]]
- **The Cycle of Transformation:** Awareness, Expression, Compassion & forgiveness, New ways of being
- **The left road and the right road:** Dave maps these to Below and Above the Line → [[Above and Below the Line]]
- **Expression tools:** using voice and body to release patterns → [[Move anger with sound and movement in a safe way]]
