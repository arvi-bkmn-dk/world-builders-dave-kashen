# Jesse Pujji

Co-host of **The Heart of Entrepreneurship** with [[Dave Kashen]] and [[Andrew Warner]].

Appears as a speaker on every processed HoE source note. The podcast is the largest category in his work — 23 sources, 251,171 words — which makes teachings drawn from it **co-authored rather than solo Dave**.

## Stories

**The Ampush sale that didn't happen.** Jesse had a nine-figure valuation in his head; the offers came in well below it. He never let himself feel the disappointment, so instead of selling he built a Red Ventures deal around an option to buy the rest of the company at the number he'd fixed on — and the enterprise value barely moved over the next five years. Dave uses it as the worked case of an unfelt feeling making a business decision. Told in [[HoE 04 - Self-Awareness and the Body]] and [[HoE 09 - Emotions and the Wisdom in Them]]; carried in [[Unfelt sadness keeps you in the wrong business, job or relationship]]. Jesse's contrast between Ampush ("running for the door") and Gateway X sits in [[You cannot decouple strategy from what you actually want]].
