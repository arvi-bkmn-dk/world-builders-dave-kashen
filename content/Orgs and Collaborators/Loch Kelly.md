# Loch Kelly

## Who

A meditation teacher whose words Dave uses to define the **Via Negativa**, the frame he says best describes his 2024.

## What Comes From Here

- The definition of Via Negativa as "a way of negating all the many things that obscure the living truth of our being" → [[Via Negativa]]
- *"So close you can't see it. So subtle your mind can't understand it. So simple you can't believe it. So good you can't accept it."*, which Dave uses on the objection that letting go can't be this simple → [[Give up the fight]]
- Recognizing your true self → [[Joy is the experience of recognizing your true self]]
