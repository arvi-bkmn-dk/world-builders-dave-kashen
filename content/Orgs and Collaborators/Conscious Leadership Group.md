# Conscious Leadership Group

## What This Is

The single most-cited source in his work. **Conscious Leadership appears in 11 of the 12 processed sources** — every one except Dave's own Top 5 Frameworks document. It is not an occasional reference; it is the vocabulary Dave thinks in.

CLG sits **upstream** of the graph as a *teacher*, not a collaborator. Dave did not work with them on Inner Game of Entrepreneurship — he learned from them, and says so on air.
