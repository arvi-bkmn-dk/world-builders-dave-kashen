# Gagan Biyani

Founder of **Udemy**, **Sprig** and **Maven**. The first guest on The Heart of Entrepreneurship, interviewed by Dave in [[HoE 15 - Gagan Biyani on Control, Proof and Comparison]] after the two spent four days together at a founders' retreat in Sun Valley.

A **guest**, not a teacher — he isn't a source of Dave's frameworks. He is a case: a founder narrating the arc Dave's work describes, from the outside.

## The arc

Started a speech-and-debate camp at 13. Co-founded Udemy at 21 and was fired by his co-founder at 23. Built Sprig — an early ghost kitchen — to 1,300 employees and shut it down over eighteen months. Took three years off. Then Maven, which itself went through a rise and collapse before restarting on an edited model.

## What he contributes

- [[Talent is not the defining factor of your success in a high variance business]] — the Bezos-20%-dumber argument
- [[Every company has a co-founder who left and got erased from history]]
- [[If you are envious of someone you have to trade their entire life for yours]] — via Naval
- The **burden of proof** framing that Dave then dismantles → [[The burden of proof is an illusion]]

## Worth noting

His account of Sprig failing is the most specific emotional record in his work: eighteen months of "debilitating levels of dread," then **complete, immediate relief** the moment they decided to shut down — "and I never felt that dread again."
