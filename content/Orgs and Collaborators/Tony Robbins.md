# Tony Robbins

## Who

Author and seminar leader. In 2023 Dave completed Robbins' **Unleash the Power Within** (November) and **Date with Destiny** (December) and names both, alongside the Hoffman Process, as the courses that left him feeling called to "play bigger" in 2024.

**Context matters here.** Robbins is often just a name in his work: in the podcast, Jesse compares Dave to "the next Tony Robbins", which is not attribution. In the Medium posts Dave explicitly credits him, so the credit is real.

## What Comes From Here

- **The Six Human Needs** (Certainty, Variety, Significance, Love/Connection, Growth, Contribution). Widely attributed to Robbins; Dave uses the list without naming him in the 2023 reflections → [[All behavior is an attempt to meet the same needs in a different priority order]]
- **Toward and away values, and internal rules:** "this was the focus of Date with Destiny" → [[We set narrow rules for feeling good and broad rules for feeling bad]]
- **The Priming Exercise:** part of Dave's morning state practice
- **"Success is never a matter of resources but of resourcefulness"** → [[Scarcity is a mindset, not a reality]]
