# Harvard Negotiation Project

## Who

The Harvard program behind **interest-based (principled) negotiation**, popularised by Roger Fisher and William Ury in *Getting to Yes*.

**Credited by name, loosely.** Dave refers to *"Harvard interest based negotiation"* without naming the authors. The attribution of interest-based negotiation to the Harvard Negotiation Project is well established.
