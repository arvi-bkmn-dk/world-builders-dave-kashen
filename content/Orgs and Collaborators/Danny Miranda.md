# Danny Miranda

## Who

Host of **The Danny Miranda Podcast**. Dave appeared on episode 394, *The Inner Game of Life*, which he later calls "a very vulnerable, authentic interview" in his 2023 reflections.

His lines in the interview are his own and are never attributed to Dave. He is a substantive interviewer. He opens on a tweet of Dave's (*is it possible you actually have no problems other than the ones your mind invents?*), takes responsibility to its extreme with the Holocaust question, and reads Dave's subject-and-object passage from *The Course* back to him.
