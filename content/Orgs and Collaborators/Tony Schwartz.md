# Tony Schwartz

## Who

Author and founder of The Energy Project. Co-author, with Catherine McCarthy, of the Harvard Business Review article *Manage Your Energy, Not Your Time*, and, with Jim Loehr, of *The Power of Full Engagement*.

**Attributed, not credited, for the article.** Dave cites *"an article years ago called, Manage Your Energy, Not Your Time"* without naming its authors. The authorship is well established.
