# Ramana Maharshi

## Who

Indian sage whose self-inquiry teaching is built on the single question *Who am I?*

**Attributed, not credited.** Dave does not name Maharshi for this idea. The attribution is recorded because the idea is widely known as Maharshi's, so the graph never presents it as Dave's original.
