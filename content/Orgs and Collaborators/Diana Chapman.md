Co-founder of the [[Conscious Leadership Group]] and the individual Dave names most often within it. Cited in HoE 09, 10 and 11.

## Coaching Dave

Dave says Diana Chapman coached him through imagining the chip in his brain that holds the thought *I am Dave* being removed, and noticing what's left. For him it was nothing, and the nothing felt like peace. He also met the context-versus-content distinction at a workshop she ran with [[Jim Dethmer]]. → [[Who Am I, and Who Am I If I Am Even More Than That]] · [[GA 01 - The Inner Game of Life, Danny Miranda Podcast 394]]
