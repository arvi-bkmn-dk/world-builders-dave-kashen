# A Course in Miracles

## Who

A spiritual text (with a 365-lesson workbook) that a coach recommended to Dave. In the Danny Miranda interview he describes going through the workbook during an early psychedelic experience and feeling the truth of its introduction. He also says he is "not necessarily a Course in Miracles devotee" and doesn't tell clients to read it.

## What Comes From Here

- **Love and fear as the only two choices.** "The opposite of love is fear, but what is all-encompassing can have no opposite." Dave quotes this line in *The Course* (MB 02) without naming the source, and credits A Course in Miracles by name in GA 01 → [[When you choose not to love something, it is because you are scared]]
- *"Nothing real can be threatened. Nothing unreal exists."*, which Dave interprets as love being indestructible, including through death → [[Love is unconditional by nature, so the journey is from conditional to unconditional living]]
