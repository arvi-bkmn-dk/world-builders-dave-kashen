# Michael Singer

Author of **The Untethered Soul**, cited in HoE 05 and HoE 07.

## What Comes From Here

- **The journey of disidentification** — "I'm not my body, I'm not my mind" — which Dave uses as the route into the talker/listener distinction → [[Hearing a voice in your head means there is a talker and a listener]] · [[If you are not your thoughts, the question of who you are opens]]
- **Samskara** — the notion that when old material surfaces, "it's coming up to be healed or released" → [[The conditions you learned to survive become the conditions your survival depends on]]

## Samskara, used in the moment

In the Danny Miranda interview, Dave recalls Singer's advice in *The Untethered Soul* or *Living Untethered*: don't let a surfacing samskara go to waste by pushing it back down. He applied it while losing most of his net worth. → [[Failing might be the thing that liberates you from your ego]] · [[GA 01 - The Inner Game of Life, Danny Miranda Podcast 394]]
