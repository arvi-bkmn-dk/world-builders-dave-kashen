# Landmark Forum

## Who

The personal-development program where Dave first worked through **what happened versus the stories you make up about what happened**. He says it let him drop the story that he was unworthy and choose an authentic life, which led to leaving the company that had acquired his startup and starting to coach.

He says he doesn't widely recommend it to clients, because of its aggressive marketing, though it was "a profoundly positive experience" for him.
