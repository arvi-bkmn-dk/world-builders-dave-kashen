# Dad Pod

A podcast by two friends in Madison, **Mike** and **Luke** (first names only in the episode), who interview dads about how becoming a father changed them. Their lines are their own and are never attributed to Dave.

Luke is a substantive voice. He stepped down as a CEO about eight or nine months before the interview, and describes using the deathbed perspective to make that decision. Mike raises the "daddy postmortem" idea and the observation that people's best memories of their fathers are of presence.
