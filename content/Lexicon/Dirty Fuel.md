# Dirty Fuel

## Definition

Dave's name for motivation manufactured out of lack, fear and self-attack. It works, which is why founders keep using it, but it produces stress, a yo-yo effect and a narrower state. Dave later sharpened the term: the problem is not fear itself but resistance to feeling it.
