# Whole Body Yes

## Definition

The test for choosing an action from above the line: the head believes it will work, the heart wants to do it and believes it will serve others, and the gut feels positive about it. It replaces trying to make the "right" choice, which can't be known, with making choices that feel right and authentic from a state of presence.

> our head (mind, intellect) believes the actions will be effective at moving us toward our vision, our heart (emotions, compassion) wants to take the actions and believes they will serve others, and our gut (intuition) feeling is positive around them.

The term is widely associated with the [[Conscious Leadership Group]]. Dave uses it inside their Above/Below the Line frame, not as his own coinage.
